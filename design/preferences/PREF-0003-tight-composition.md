# PREF-0003 — Compositions fill their frame; no scattered empty space

**Polarity:** reject
**Facet terms:** `B.SPACE.scattered` ✗ · `B.SPACE.tight` ✓
**Scope:** brand graphics and presentation visuals; likely wider, not yet tested on product UI
**Evidence:** Sam rejected the Bit. banner v2 layout — logo on a plinth left, four exploded UI layers right — as "scattered with empty space", and asked for v3, a tight composition (2026-10-21)
**Date:** 2026-10-21

**The preference.** A composition should fill its frame deliberately. Large gaps are
acceptable only when they are doing something — separating, framing, or setting rhythm. Space
that is simply left over is a defect.

**Why.** Scattered space reads as an unfinished arrangement rather than a decision. It is the
difference between generous and empty, and the eye detects it immediately even when the
individual elements are good.

**Exceptions.** Not tested against a luxury or editorial register, where generous space is the
message. Treat `B.SPACE.generous` as unknown rather than rejected.
