# PREF-0005 — Visuals must present the subject, not decorate around it

**Polarity:** reject
**Facet terms:** `C.SAT.candy` ✗ · `C.MAT.velvet` ~ · `F.IMG.ui-as-image` ✓ · `F.3D.exploded-layers` ✓
**Scope:** brand graphics, case studies, presentation visuals
**Evidence:** Sam rejected the Bit. banner v1 AI pass — red velvet steps and props — saying it read "like a candy shop" and "didn't present interface design". The replacement showed the logo on a plinth with four exploded frosted-glass UI layers labelled Grid, Tokens, Components, Product page (2026-10-21)
**Date:** 2026-10-21

**The preference.** A visual about a design system must show design-system material — grids,
tokens, components, screens. Rich materials and lighting are welcome as *finish*; they are
not the subject. When the decoration becomes the content, the piece has failed regardless of
how well it is rendered.

**Why.** The work has a job: to communicate what the thing is. A beautiful render of
unrelated props communicates craft and nothing else, and reads as sweetness rather than as
professional interface work.

**Exceptions.** Velvet and similar soft materials are not banned — they were rejected in a
composition where they carried no meaning. As a finish on the right subject they are open.
