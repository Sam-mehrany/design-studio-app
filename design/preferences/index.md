## What this set does and does not cover

Twelve records, and they cluster hard: **consistency** (0002, 0012, 0013), **compositional
discipline** (0003, 0005), **material truth** (0004, 0006), **locale** (0007, 0009),
**voice** (0011), and one governing rule about ambition (0001).

What is **not** in evidence yet, and is therefore wide open:

- colour taste beyond "one accent, strictly semantic" — no hue preference observed
- radius, density and spacing defaults for product UI
- motion character: spring or ease-out, restrained or rich
- illustration and icon style — no evidence at all
- whether the brand-graphics preferences (0003–0006) transfer to product UI

Most of the existing evidence comes from **brand and presentation work** — the Bit. design
system and the Markito redesign. Product-UI taste is thin, and the next projects are where it
gets recorded.
