# REF-0035 — Footer with cobalt-and-cream duotone illustration

## Nature

Footer on its own with a duotone illustration.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Strict duotone: cobalt `#003696` field and cream `#feeac4` for the illustrated ice; link text in pale blue, headings near-white.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque; small column heads in white, links in a lighter blue, a short tagline under the wordmark.

**Layout & harmony.** Brand + tagline left, three link columns right, illustration filling the lower 55% full-bleed.

**Shape & surface.** Flat; no containers, no borders.

**Buttons & components.** None beyond text links and social icons.

**Gradient & imagery.** Textured editorial illustration (penguins on ice) printed in the two brand colours. Without it the footer is a thin strip over empty blue.

**Contrast.** Column heads `#edffff` on `#003490` = 10.7:1. Links `#76a1f0` = 4.2:1 and tagline `#81abff` = 4.9:1 — links marginally **fail** 4.5:1 (anti-aliasing may understate).

## Why the verdict

The two-colour illustration discipline is portable; the footer is carried by bespoke artwork (hard imagery dependency).
