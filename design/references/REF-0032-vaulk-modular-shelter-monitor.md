# REF-0032 — Vaulk — "French engineered UHPC modular shelter" full-bleed photo landing on monitor

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the desk staging, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a modular-shelter company's landing on a monitor on a wooden desk — a full-bleed garden photograph with a white headline centred over it and a minimal top nav.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: the photograph is the palette — greens (about #5A6B3A), sky (about #D8DEDD), stone grey — with white type and a black nav. No UI accent hue: the only colour is the photograph itself.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "French engineered UHPC modular shelter, CBRN-ready. Deployed in 7 days." in a grotesque at about 22–26 px, nav at about 9–10 px, "SCROLL" micro-label at about 8 px. Two-tier scale — the headline is the only display size, everything else is microcopy.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: nav bar across the top (logo left, links centre, "Contact us" pill right), headline centred over the photograph's middle band. Hierarchy is ranked by position (the headline sits on the brightest part of the photo) and by size. Density is very low — one photo, one headline, one nav.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the "Contact us" pill is the only curve; everything else is sharp. Separation is by the photograph alone — no cards, no borders, no shadows inside the screen. The monitor's thin frame is the only edge.

**Buttons & components.** One white pill CTA ("Contact us") top-right and a "SCROLL" indicator mid-left. Both are quiet relative to the photograph — the image is the page, the controls are annotations on it.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. One full-bleed photograph of a garden with a shelter is the entire screen: delete it and there is no page left, just a headline on white — so the dependency is hard. The staging doubles the effect — the monitor sits in front of a real window with real greenery behind it.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white headline on the mid-tone green photograph is about 2.5:1 estimated — fails 4.5:1 where it crosses the brighter grass and sky (estimated, not measured). The black nav on the pale sky area is about 10:1 estimated — passes. The "SCROLL" micro-label over foliage is the least legible element on screen.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the full-bleed photograph with a centred headline and a minimal pill nav is a transferable landing mechanism at low cost, but the page collapses without its photograph (imagery_dep hard) and the headline fails contrast over the photo, so adapt, not adopt.
