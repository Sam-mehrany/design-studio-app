# REF-0039 — Skincare e-commerce app — "Welcome back, Olivia" home on iPhone

## Read

Values below: white background and green hero card were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a skincare store's home screen on an iPhone — a green Murad hero card, category chips, a "New arrivals" product grid with prices and black "+" buttons, a five-icon bottom tab bar.

**Colour.** Measured: white page #FFFFFF, green hero card #84CF58. Single accent: the green hero card is the only saturated surface; the rest is white, black type and product photographs. A second, greyed-out hero card peeks in from the right edge, showing the carousel's next slide.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "Welcome back, Olivia" at about 18–20 px, hero title "Retinol Youth Renewal Night Cream" at about 20–22 px bold, section titles ("Categories", "New arrivals") at about 15–16 px semibold, prices "$14.50 USD" at about 12–13 px, chips at about 12–13 px. Two-tier scale — titles and prices are one tier, body and chips another — with tabular numerals in the prices.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: greeting and search icon top, hero card, category chips in a horizontal row, product grid in two columns (cards half-cut at the right edge to signal more), bottom tab bar. Hierarchy is ranked by size (hero title first), then by the green card's colour weight. Density is moderate — four sections stacked with consistent 16 px gaps (estimated).

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: hero card at about 20–24 px radius, product cards at about 12–16 px, chips at full pill radius, black "+" buttons as full circles. Separation is by tone and soft shadow — white product cards on a white page read through their shadows and their hairline borders.

**Buttons & components.** The components are the category chips (Fragrance, Makeup, Hair, Skincare), the product cards (badge, heart, photo, name, price, black "+" button) and the bottom tab bar (home, grid, AI, bag with a "0" badge, profile). The black "+" buttons are the loudest controls — small black circles that read as the buy action on every card.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Product photographs are the catalogue content — the hero jar and the product thumbnails. Delete them and the layout still reads (greeting, chips, prices, tab bar survive), so the dependency is soft; the photos sell the products, the structure does not depend on any one of them.

**Contrast.** Measured: white text on the green hero card #84CF58 is 1.90:1 — fails 4.5:1 (measured). The dark green hero title on the same card, estimated at about #1E4D1A (about 7:1 estimated — passes, estimated, not measured). Black prices on white are about 17:1 — passes. The "20% OFF | BUY NOW" pill on green is the clearest failure on the screen.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the greeting + hero card + chips + two-column product grid with black "+" buttons is a transferable, low-cost catalogue-home mechanism, but it is a staged concept mockup with perfect data (identical products and prices) and the hero card's white text fails contrast, so adapt, not adopt.
