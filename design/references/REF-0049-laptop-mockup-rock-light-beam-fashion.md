# REF-0049 — Laptop mockup on porous rock under a light beam, beige fashion-editorial site

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Near-black brown room #0e0a07 with a diagonal beam of light #b6a699; laptop space grey #7d7875. The screen is a warm paper beige #d9d3c7 with a black-and-white portrait — the screen and the beam share one warm neutral, the only light in the scene.

**Typography.** On screen: thin tracked all-caps nav (NEW ARRIVALS, SHOP, …) and a light all-caps CTA "YOUR DESIGN HERE >" (cap height ≈13.7 preview px). Single light weight throughout.

**Layout & harmony.** Vertical frame; laptop sits on stacked porous stones in the lower half, beam cuts across the upper half. Screen: model cut-out anchored bottom-left, nav top-right, CTA bottom-right, the rest empty paper.

**Shape & surface.** Environment is real geometry with hard directional light; the screen is flat with a paper texture and no containers.

**Buttons & components.** Text-only CTA with a chevron; the active nav item is a hairline underline.

**Gradient & imagery.** Both the scene render and the on-screen portrait are load-bearing.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: CTA #100b08 on #d7d1c3 12.85:1; nav #413a30 on #dad4c8 7.60:1.
