# REF-0038 — Footer as frosted-glass panel over a surreal photo

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Taken from the photo: slate blues `#2e4051`–`#394e5d` and olive greys `#3d413c`. White for the headline and subscribe button; pale grey-green for links.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque; large white headline, small links.

**Layout & harmony.** Full-bleed image with a newsletter headline + pill input on top and a translucent footer panel below holding link columns.

**Shape & surface.** Pill input and button; footer panel is frosted glass with blur over the photo.

**Buttons & components.** Pill email input with an embedded subscribe action.

**Gradient & imagery.** The AI-generated photograph is the design. Live blur on top of it.

**Contrast.** Headline white on `#2c3c4c` = 11.2:1. Links `#9d9f9a` = 4.2:1, description 4.2:1, placeholder 4.1:1 — **fail**, and they depend on whichever part of the photo sits behind.

## Why the verdict

Fails two killers at once: hard imagery dependency and high cost (photo + live blur), with text contrast at the mercy of the image.
