# REF-0019 — Stock artwork: gold line-art florals with script title on textured white

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — paper white #fdfafb / #fbf9f8 (≈73%) with a fine texture; one gold family: #b77c33, #d7ad73, #f3d7af, cream #fef8de.

**Typography** — "Golden Floral" in a connecting script, subtitle "BACKGROUND" in light tracked caps beneath; display + text pairing.

**Layout & harmony** — ornament crowds the left edge, type block sits right of centre; stationery layout.

**Shape & surface** — monoline floral illustration, no fills.

**Gradient & imagery** — a slight gold tonal shift in the line art; the ornament is half the composition.

**Contrast** — script #a36117 on #fefcfb 4.80:1; subtitle #d8b77b on #fffcf9 1.87:1 — fails.

## Why the verdict

Ornamental stationery register and a subtitle at 1.87:1; nothing here is a screen mechanism.
