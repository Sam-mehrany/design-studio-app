# REF-0058 — Black-and-red battle tank with red LED strips — 3D render in white hangar

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: a 3D render of a black battle tank with red armour panels and red LED strips, staged in a white hangar — the red counterpart to REF-0057's blue. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: black hull and turret with large red side panels (sampled dark red around #55181D in shadow, brighter where lit) and thin red LED strips, against a white hangar field (#FEFEFE dominant). Single accent — red — used at two scales: broad flat panels and thin glowing lines. Dark-first value strategy, like REF-0057, but the red panels make this the most colour-saturated vehicle on the board after the orange pair.

**Composition & form.** Side profile, cannon pointing left, tank centred, the long barrel crossing the upper third as a horizontal line with red banding. The red side panels are cut as sharp angular polygons — a chevron on the hull side, a slab on the turret rear — so the colour itself draws the facets. Seven road wheels repeat along the bottom under a black track band.

**Light & surface.** Bright hangar light from above, glossy floor reflection with a red tint where the LED strips reflect. Surfaces are polished black armour with gloss highlights and matte red panels; the LED strips glow, and their reflection in the floor doubles the line. The barrel's red rings echo the side panels at a smaller scale.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: black vehicle against the white hangar is very high separation, and the glowing red strips against black are the highest local contrast in the image. The red panels against black are moderate — dark red on black needs the light to read. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the red-at-two-scales system — broad panels plus thin glowing lines — is the transferable lesson, and the pairing with REF-0057 makes the blue/red comparison useful, but a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
