# REF-0093 — HONK! — Website footer over a 3D desert road scene

## Read

**Colour.** Sampled from the image: the footer card is #FEFEFE on a #F6F6F6 page field, the HONK! logo mark is orange at approximately #E67739, and the scene below runs from a pale #F5C594 road through an #E28242 dune field to a deep #C85228 foreground. One orange hue carries all the colour; the footer itself is neutral-only, so the accent does no semantic work in the chrome and all of it in the scene.

**Typography.** A neo-grotesque sans in a single family, two weights only: bold column headings (Explore, App, Legal Pages) against regular-weight links, with the copyright line in the same regular weight at a smaller size. Exact point sizes cannot be determined from the screenshot, and no tabular numerals appear anywhere in the frame.

**Layout & harmony.** The footer is one wide card holding a four-zone row — logo left, three link columns right — above a hairline divider, with the copyright line and four circular social icons on the row below; the 3D scene fills the rest of the frame and bleeds past the card on all sides. Hierarchy is carried by weight and position alone, and the card's generous internal padding is the only spacing system visible.

**Shape & surface.** The footer card has a large corner radius (exact pixels cannot be determined from the screenshot) and a soft drop shadow that lifts it off the scene, while the social icons are full circles with thin grey outlines. Separation inside the card is by a 1px hairline divider and space, not by borders or further containers.

**Buttons & components.** There are no buttons in the frame: the only interactive elements are eleven text links in three columns and four icon-only circular social buttons (Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn) with dark glyphs on white. The social circles are the loudest chrome in the footer, louder than any link.

**Gradients & imagery.** The entire lower two-thirds is one full-bleed 3D render — matte clay-like dunes, a winding pale road, and a small truck — lit with a warm directional light and soft shadows. It is the whole visual event; the footer card is plain white type on white, and nothing in the composition survives the render's removal.

**Contrast.** Near-black headings on #FEFEFE are high contrast. The grey link and copyright text samples around #757575, which measures approximately 4.6:1 on white — above the 4.5:1 floor but only just, and the exact link colour cannot be determined precisely from this compressed screenshot, so that figure is an estimate, not a measurement.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the mechanism — a plain white footer card floated over a full-bleed rendered scene, with three grouped link columns, a hairline divider and circular social icons — is transferable, but the design is hard-dependent on its imagery and the large 3D render is high cost, so it cannot be adopted as-is.
