# REF-0065 — HOFIN — real-estate landing page, photo hero with search bar and property cards

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a very long full-page real-estate landing capture — photo hero with search bar, about section, featured property, how-it-works, property grids, FAQ, contact form and a giant "HOFIN" footer — with prices from $4,200 to $40,000.

**Colour.** Measured: page field white (#FFFFFF dominant), hero sky sampled at #BDD7E6 (pale blue), property photos carrying the only saturated colour. Neutral-only palette for the chrome itself — black, white and grey — with colour delegated entirely to the photography. The black contact section near the foot is the page's one dark inversion, and it is the only place the chrome uses a dark field.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: hero headline "We Bring New Experience of Your Dream Property" at about 40–48 px grotesque, white on the photo; section headlines at about 28–32 px; body at about 13–14 px grey; prices at about 16–18 px semibold. Dramatic scale, hero roughly 3.5× body. The giant footer wordmark "HOFIN" spans the full width at an estimated 200+ px — the largest single type element in this batch — and functions as image, not text.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, very long capture: hero, centred about paragraph, featured property with hotspot labels ("Balcony 2nd Floor", "Sunset view", "Swimming pool"), three-step how-it-works beside a search-results panel, two property grids ("Explore our latest property", "Top Picks Rent Property"), FAQ accordion, contact form, giant wordmark footer. The page is long but each section is one idea; the hotspot labels on the featured photo are the most distinctive layout device — small pill annotations pinned to the image.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated by eye: large radii (about 16–20 px) on the hero, property cards and form; separation by tone and by the photos themselves, with thin borders on the search bar and form fields — no shadows. Property cards overlay a white info panel on the bottom of the photo, a consistent device across both grids.

**Buttons & components.** The search bar is the primary component: Rent/Buy toggle, four dropdowns (Location, Type, Style, Price Range) and a black "Search" button — the densest control cluster on the page. Elsewhere the actions are small: heart icons on cards, carousel arrows, a black "Send" button on the form. The search bar is louder than any headline on the page, which is correct for the job.

**Gradient & imagery.** Photography is the whole page: hero villa, interior, property thumbnails. Hard dependency — delete the photos and the page is a skeleton of headlines and empty cards; the layout has no independent visual idea. The hotspot pills on the featured photo are the one non-photographic idea, and they depend on the photo existing.

**Contrast.** Measured: black text on white is about 18:1 — passes. The risk is the hero: white headline over a pale sky photo (#BDD7E6 sampled) — the sky is light enough that white type over it is low-contrast where the photo is brightest, and the headline survives only because the lower half of the hero photo is a darker building. Grey body copy is estimated by eye at about #6B7280 on white, roughly 4.8:1 — passes, estimated, not measured. Several body paragraphs render in very light grey in the capture and are close to failing; flagged as estimated, not measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the hotspot-labelled featured photo and the search-bar-as-hero-component are both transferable mechanisms, but the page is hard-dependent on photography and some light-grey body copy is borderline, so adapt, not adopt.
