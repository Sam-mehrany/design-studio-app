# REF-0050 — Phone wallpaper — centred 梦 'dream' over red Hokusai wave

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — near-black with a green cast `#0c1002` covers 87% of the frame; the only accent is a red band, `#d70e10` deep to `#f25e49` highlights. Off-white type `#ede7e9`.

**Typography** — one CJK sans glyph (glyph box 55 px on a 1308 px canvas) over a tiny italic serif "dream" (13 px): a sans + serif pair at ≈4×.

**Layout & harmony** — dead-centre micro-lockup at ~38% height, a red wave bleeding off the bottom edge, everything between is empty. Position and emptiness do all the ranking.

**Shape & surface** — flat; the wave is a recoloured woodblock-style illustration with fine line detail.

**Gradient & imagery** — no gradient. Without the wave the frame becomes a plain centred lockup on near-black — still coherent, loses its weight at the bottom.

**Contrast** — 梦 on ground 15.77:1; "dream" 13.63:1; red `#d70e10` against the ground 3.63:1 (decorative, not text).

## Why the verdict

The transferable part is the lock-screen/empty-state mechanism: one tiny centred lockup on a near-black field with a single accent anchored to one edge. Wallpaper art, so low confidence.
