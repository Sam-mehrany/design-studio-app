# REF-0086 — Newspaper reader — striped shirt and cap, ladder left, speaker stack right

## Read

This is a photography / mood reference, not a UI screen — no UI archetype applies. Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); proportions estimated by eye. One line of context: a fashion editorial photograph — a man in a striped shirt, wide dark trousers and a paint-splattered cap reading a tabloid newspaper ("SEALED WITH A LOVING KISS"), a weathered wooden ladder at left, a stacked pair of wooden speakers at right, against a pale void.

**Colour.** Measured: pale void #D3D9CF, black #0F1012, wood browns (sampled #4C3C2D), newsprint cream. The palette is near-neutral with the newspaper as the colour event: cream paper, black headlines and one small red flag ("NO.1 FOR LEAGUE") — the red is a tiny accent inside the paper, not in the scene. The blue pinstripe shirt is the only cool hue on the figure.

**Composition.** Three verticals in a row: ladder (left), figure (centre), speakers (right) — a triptych of tall objects with the figure as the middle panel. The newspaper is a horizontal block across the figure's waist, breaking the vertical rhythm; its two headlines ("JUNIORS IN WILD BRAWL", "SEALED WITH A LOVING KISS") are the image's text layer. The upper third is empty void.

**Light & form.** Flat, even studio light — the ladder's worn paint and the speakers' wood grain read by texture, not by shadow. Forms are vertical and rectilinear: ladder rungs, speaker boxes, trouser legs. The newspaper is the only diagonal, held open with a slight curve, and its crease is the image's one soft line.

**Typography.** The newspaper headlines are the image's type: heavy grotesque all-caps tabloid heads ("SEALED WITH A LOVING KISS", "LIFE BAN ON CLUB") at display size, with small body columns below — real print typography inside a photograph. The shirt carries a small script embroidery and the cap a white logo, both garment details. The type is found, not designed for this image.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is three props as a set: a ladder, a newspaper, a speaker stack — objects that suggest a life (work, news, music) without showing a room. The ladder's chipped paint is the most textured surface in the batch; the speakers' wood veneer is the warmest. The void makes the three objects read as a collection, not a scene.

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the triptych mechanism: three verticals, one figure, one horizontal interruption — a compositional formula that works for any three-element layout, including a three-card row.

**Contrast.** The dark trousers and black cap read against the pale void (black #0F1012 on #D3D9CF ≈ 14:1, computed from sampled colours). The newspaper's black headlines on cream are high contrast by print standards; the small body text is not legible at this scale and is not meant to be read.
