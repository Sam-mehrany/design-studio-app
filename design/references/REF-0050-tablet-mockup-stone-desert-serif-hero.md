# REF-0050 — Tablet mockup flat on stone, condensed-serif headline over desert aerial photo

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Environment is cold greyscale: #161616 ground, #444245 stone slab, #010101 bezel. The screen is all warm earth from the photo — dune #7b3a1a, shadows #28140d, thumbnails #cbaa87. Type is warm off-white #fff6ec; no UI accent hue.

**Typography.** Condensed transitional serif for the headline and the pull line, a small neo-grotesque for nav and card body. Measured cap heights (preview px): headline 32.3, secondary serif line 13.3 — ≈2.4×; card body is micro.

**Layout & harmony.** Top-down tablet centred on a stone slab. Screen: nav bar top, three-line serif headline top-left with a ghost "Explore More" link, serif pull line bottom-left, then a three-column row of thumbnail + title + body cards.

**Shape & surface.** Flat on screen — no cards, no borders; thumbnails are small squares. The environment relies on rendered stone texture and soft top light.

**Buttons & components.** Only a text link with a circular arrow icon; nav is plain text.

**Gradient & imagery.** The full-bleed aerial photo is the whole page; without it the serif type has nothing to sit on.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: headline #fff6ec on #412a1a 12.5:1; serif pull line #c5ada9 on #3d190d 7.4:1; nav #ab9486 4.9:1. Failure: card body #8a6f64 on #391d12 3.34:1.

## Why the verdict

Presentation shot with a photo-led page: hard imagery dependency and left-anchored serif headline (breaks RTL). The cold-environment / warm-screen contrast is the adaptable idea.
