# REF-0006 — Film photo: silhouette leaping over a bonfire at dusk

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — pastel dusk sky: peach #fce9db / #fbe2bb at mid-frame, lavender-grey #b9aab6 at the top, #d1b8b5 / #a4928f clouds; ground and figure brown-black #302521 (43%); fire #d37330 is 1.4% of pixels.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — figure in the upper-middle against open sky, fire low-centre; a vertical figure-over-flame stack.

**Shape & surface** — film grain throughout; soft horizon haze.

**Gradient & imagery** — the sky gradient is photographic; the image is the whole artefact.

**Contrast** — silhouette #302521 on sky #fce9db 12.62:1.

## Why the verdict

A mood photograph; the only transferable part is a peach-to-lavender dusk ramp, which is too thin to carry a record. Hard imagery dependency.
