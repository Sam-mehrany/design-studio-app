# REF-0031 — Clear cassette with turntables as reels, composite

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Pure black ground `#000000` (≈53% incl. `#080707`), translucent shell greys `#464543` / `#b7b19f`, and a single yellow `#eebe26` on the two platter labels.

**Layout & harmony.** Object centred on black, visual pun (turntables replace the reels) carries the idea.

**Gradient & imagery.** Photo/render composite; collapses without it.

**Contrast.** No text. Yellow `#eebe26` on black = 12.03:1 — the accent reads by value as well as hue.

## Why the verdict

Conceptual photo-composite with hard imagery dependence; nothing UI-transferable beyond "one bright accent on true black".
