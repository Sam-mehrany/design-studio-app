# REF-0022 — Ap-Mockups — red-orange gradient grid promo on monitor

## Read

Values below: gradient reds were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a mockup-product promo screen on a monitor — a red-to-orange gradient field of rounded-square tiles with a white "Ap-Mockups" title top-left and a white "ap" mark bottom-right.

**Colour.** Measured: deep red at the top #8B061B, bright orange at the bottom #FF391C. A two-hue vertical gradient, red to orange, is the entire palette — white type is the only other colour, and there is no accent beyond the gradient itself.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "Ap-Mockups" at about 28–32 px semibold, "01" at about 14 px, body microcopy at about 8–9 px in two small columns, "ap" mark at about 40–44 px bold. Two-tier scale — the title and the "ap" mark are the display sizes, the microcopy is one small tier.

**Layout & harmony.** Modular grid, estimated by eye: the screen is a uniform grid of rounded-square tiles (about 10 columns), the title sits top-left, two microcopy columns sit top-centre-right, the "ap" mark sits bottom-right. Hierarchy is ranked by size and by the gradient's own light-to-dark fall (the eye goes to the bright orange band). Density is low — the grid is texture, not content.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: tiles at about 6–8 px radius, the screen itself sharp-cornered with a thin black frame. Separation is by tone only — tile edges are the gradient shading itself, with no border and no shadow.

**Buttons & components.** There are no buttons and no controls; the components are the tile grid, the title and the wordmark. The loudest element is the gradient field itself, not any control.

**Gradient & imagery.** The gradient is the whole design: a vertical red-to-orange wash over a tile grid. No photography, no illustration. The gradient is not decoration on top of a design — it is the design — but it is a flat CSS-style gradient, so it costs almost nothing to reproduce.

**Contrast.** Measured: white on deep red #8B061B is 9.81:1 — passes. White on bright orange #FF391C is 3.59:1 — fails 4.5:1, so the "ap" mark sitting on the brightest orange band is below AA (measured, from the sampled gradient values). The title sits on the dark red and passes.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the gradient-over-tile-grid as a promo field is a cheap, transferable background mechanism at low cost, but it is a mockup-product promo, not a product screen, and the bright end of the gradient fails white-type contrast, so adapt, not adopt.
