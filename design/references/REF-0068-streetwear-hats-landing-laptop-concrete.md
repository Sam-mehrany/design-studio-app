# REF-0068 — Laptop-on-concrete mockup — black streetwear store hero with heavy caps headline and cyan accent

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye.

**Colour.** Screen `#020202`, white headline, grey body `#b9c0cc`, nav `#a0a0a0`; cyan `#1da6c9` used for the active nav item and a hand-drawn ellipse around "HATS". The photo cards add blue (`#0259a4`) that sits close to the accent. Environment: dark gradient `#0d0f13` over raw concrete.

**Typography.** Heavy geometric sans ALL-CAPS headline "NEXT-GEN HATS." (two lines), regular body below — weight contrast does the ranking. Headline-to-body about 4× (*estimated*).

**Layout & harmony.** Split hero: copy and CTA left, a horizontal carousel of rounded photo cards right with a circular chevron control. Sparse; size and weight rank.

**Shape & surface.** Large radius on photo cards (~16px, *estimated*), pill buttons; flat, no shadows.

**Buttons & components.** Ghost pill buttons with white 1px outline ("DISCOVER", "Shop now") — quiet, letting the photos lead.

**Gradient & imagery.** Photography carries the right half and all the colour (hard).

**Contrast.** Headline white on `#031421` = 18.7:1; body `#b9c0cc` on `#021012` = 10.6:1; nav `#a0a0a0` = 7.9:1; cyan on black = 7.3:1.

## Why the verdict

Competent but generic dark e-com hero; the scribbled ellipse is the one distinctive move. Photo-dependent and staged → adapt, low confidence.
