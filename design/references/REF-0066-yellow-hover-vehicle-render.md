# REF-0066 — Yellow hover vehicle 3D render, hazard livery on studio white

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — studio grey-white `#d5d7d6` → `#ececec`; one safety yellow `#f1ad29`–`#fab012` (≈18% of frame) against charcoal `#35392f` panels and black hazard stripes.

**Typography** — livery decals: numbered roundel "05", small caps labels ("MREXFLY", "OPEN"), warning pictograms.

**Layout & harmony** — single hero object floating with a soft ground shadow; three-quarter view.

**Shape & surface** — hard-surface panels, hazard-stripe pattern, decals as an information layer.

**Gradient & imagery** — a large render; the livery system is the only abstractable part.

**Contrast** — "05" `#1b1816` on yellow 9.42:1; decal text `#6d5c4b` on light yellow `#fec56c` 4.11:1 (fails AA); yellow vs. the grey ground 1.35:1 (as a UI fill on a light surface it would need a border); charcoal vs. ground 8.17:1.

## Why the verdict

The livery language (one safety yellow + charcoal + hazard stripes + numbered roundel) could become an accent/status system; the render itself is heavy. Low confidence.
