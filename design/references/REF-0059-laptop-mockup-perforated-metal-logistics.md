# REF-0059 — Laptop on black metal grid with red perforated panel, condensed-caps logistics hero

## Nature

Laptop on a black metal grid with a red perforated panel; rendered.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Scene: black #000000 with a black metal grid bench #141311 and a red perforated panel going from #410000 in shadow to #ff4a00 where lit; silver laptop #c5c5c5. Screen: white #ffffff, black type, one accent orange-red #ff4100 used for the second headline line and the primary button; a red container #570002 in the photo picks it up.

**Typography.** Bold condensed grotesque all-caps, two lines: black "SMART LOGISTICS", orange "GLOBAL CONNECTIONS" — colour, not size, splits the headline. Small regular body to the right.

**Layout & harmony.** Laptop three-quarter from above. Screen: thin dark nav bar, headline top-left overlapping a large rounded photo of container stacks, body and two CTAs top-right.

**Shape & surface.** Large radius on the hero photo; small-radius rectangular buttons.

**Buttons & components.** Orange "Request a Quote" and black "Explore More", side by side, equal weight.

**Gradient & imagery.** Container photo is decoration under the headline; the typographic hero stands without it.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: black on white 21:1. Failure: orange #ff4100 headline line on white 3.50:1 (passes only as large display), white on the #ff4300 button 3.47:1 — fails for its small label.

## Why the verdict

Two-colour condensed-caps headline is cheap and adaptable; orange on white fails at button size. Breaks RTL: the headline is a left-anchored two-line sentence overlapping the photo.
