# REF-0051 — Grey clay render of a tracked armoured vehicle — untextured 3D model

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: an untextured grey "clay" render of a tracked vehicle on a plain grey background — a form study with no colour, no environment, no staging. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: mid-grey body sampled at #676767 on a light-grey field (#AEAEAE dominant). Strictly monochrome — one grey, value range only — so every distinction in the image is made by shading and edge, never by hue. The clay treatment deliberately removes colour as a variable.

**Composition & form.** Rear three-quarter view, vehicle centred, filling most of the frame. With colour removed, the form reads purely as stacked volumes: layered hull plates, twin turret domes, side sponsons, twin-barrel sponson guns. The silhouette is blocky and stepped, with the track run as a dark base band. This is the clearest form read in the whole board precisely because nothing competes with it.

**Light & surface.** Soft diffuse light from above-left, gentle shadows under the hull, no reflections — a matte clay material throughout, no gloss, no texture. Surface interest comes only from panel breaks, rivet rows and grille slats, all modelled as geometry rather than painted on.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: mid-grey #676767 body against the #AEAEAE field is moderate separation only — the vehicle does not pop, it emerges from the ground. That low separation is the point of a clay render (form without contrast tricks), and it is also its limit as a presentation image.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the most useful form reference on the board — colour stripped out, so the panel logic and massing can be judged on their own — but a concept render, so adapt as a form study only.
