# REF-0081 — Earth Studies — man reclining in camp chair on a sand circle, studio void

## Read

This is a photography / mood reference, not a UI screen — no UI archetype applies. Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); proportions estimated by eye. One line of context: a fashion editorial photograph — a man in a cap and sunglasses reclining in a folding camp chair on a small disc of sand, a tote bag of cans beside him, against a seamless light-grey studio void.

**Colour.** Measured: light-grey void #D6D6D6, sand tan (estimated ~#C8A87E), navy shorts (estimated ~#1F2A5A, sampled dark #2A2C3C nearby), skin and wood tones. The palette is near-neutral: warm grey, sand and cream, with the navy shorts as the single dark mass and one small olive-green can as the only saturated accent. Colour is descriptive of the scene, not semantic.

**Composition.** The figure sits low and right of centre, at roughly the lower third; the upper half of the frame is empty void. The sand disc is a horizontal ellipse anchoring the chair; the chair's X-frame diagonals echo the crossed legs. The composition is asymmetric and bottom-weighted — the eye enters at the empty top and lands on the figure. Negative space is the device: about 60% of the frame is blank.

**Light & form.** Soft, even studio light with almost no hard shadow — the figure is lit flat, the sand disc casts a faint soft edge. Forms are simple and readable: the chair's wooden X-legs, the round sand disc, the rectangular tote. Depth comes from overlap (chair over sand, legs over chair), not from shadow or perspective.

**Typography.** The only type is the "Earth Studies" wordmark across the sweatshirt chest, set in a small serif (estimated ~14 px at this scale), black on cream — a brand label inside the photograph, not a design element of the image. No other text.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is the reference: a minimal environment built from three props (chair, sand disc, tote) on a seamless void. The sand disc is a deliberate set piece — a circle of "outdoors" placed indoors — and the scattered pebbles on it are the only texture. The environment is staged, not found.

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact; there is nothing to delete it from. The image's value as a reference is the mood mechanism: one figure, one small ground, vast empty space — a formula for making a single subject feel calm rather than empty.

**Contrast.** The figure reads against the void by value: dark navy and skin tones on light grey #D6D6D6 (black-on-light ≈ 16.8:1 computed for the sampled extremes). The cream sweatshirt is close in value to the void, so the figure's upper body nearly dissolves — the navy shorts and the chair legs carry the silhouette, not the torso.

## Why the verdict

Adapt (provisional, as a mood reference): the one-figure-one-ground-vast-void formula is directly stealable for campaign and landing imagery, but this is a brand fashion photograph, not a UI pattern, so it can never be adopt — adapt, as imagery direction only.
