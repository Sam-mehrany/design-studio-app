# REF-0007 — Papercut print "We Are Starlight" — three figures around a fire (Nikki McClure)

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — two inks on black: ground #070304 (83%), peach #f2b27c (8%) for figures and wood, orange #c66e10 for the flame core, white for the stars and title. Black + one warm ink + white.

**Typography** — "WE ARE STARLIGHT" in small hand-cut caps, upper-left, white; tiny vertical credit bottom-right.

**Layout & harmony** — upper half is star-field and space; figures and fire sit in the lower half, sparks rising up the centre line; the title is the only off-axis element.

**Shape & surface** — cut-paper edges, no gradients, no shading beyond the two inks.

**Gradient & imagery** — none; the illustration is the work.

**Contrast** — title #fffeff on #080405 = 20.27:1; peach #f2b27c on black 11.17:1.

## Why the verdict

The restriction is the mechanism: black ground, one warm ink, white as the highlight — a strong dark-first palette discipline. Left-anchored title makes the layout risky to mirror.
