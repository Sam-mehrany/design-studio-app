# REF-0058 — Laptop on orange velvet armchair, legal-services split hero with frosted service cards

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Scene is terracotta wall #994c30 and orange velvet #ff9647 — a warm set picked to echo the brand's orange logo. Screen: cream #faf3f0 left half, photo right half. Action blue #033bd7 on the "Te llamamos" pill; WhatsApp chips in green #40c451 and blue #477bea; frosted service cards #65615f.

**Typography.** Light geometric sans headline "Tus abogados de confianza" (cap ≈31 preview px) with a hand-drawn underline stroke, regular body (≈8.4) — ≈3.7×. A script note in orange ("¡La primera consulta es gratuita!").

**Layout & harmony.** Laptop three-quarter view on the chair seat. Screen: split hero — copy, phone-number field with inline button, contact chips and social proof on the left; photograph with stacked frosted cards and a carousel indicator on the right.

**Shape & surface.** Pills everywhere: field-plus-button combo, chips, WhatsApp/phone buttons. Frosted live-blur cards with medium radius over the photo.

**Buttons & components.** Inline field + blue pill submit is the primary; secondary channels as small outlined pills.

**Gradient & imagery.** Photograph decorates the right half; the left half converts without it.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: headline #010202 on #fcf5f2 19.3:1, body #373331 11.4:1, button text on #033bd7 7.27:1, frosted card text #ece3d9 on #5f5b5a 5.29:1. Failure: placeholder #90857f on white 3.59:1.

## Why the verdict

The inline phone-field-plus-button conversion block and contact chips are a solid adaptable pattern; the staged scene is costly. RTL risky: split hero mirrors, but the hand-drawn underline and arrow note are directional.
