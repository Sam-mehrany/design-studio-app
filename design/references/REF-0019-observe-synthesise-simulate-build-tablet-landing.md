# REF-0019 — Observe, Synthesise, Simulate, Build — autonomous-intelligence landing on tablet

## Read

Values below: screen background and CTA orange were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a landing hero on a tablet in a stand — italic serif headline, orange CTAs, an isometric diagram of labelled blocks — with a press-logo strip along the bottom.

**Colour.** Measured: screen white #FFFFFF, CTA orange #FE5F1D. One accent only — the orange carries every action (pill badge, both CTAs, diagram highlights) against black type and hairline grey grid; no semantic hues, the accent is purely directional.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "Observe, Synthesise, Simulate, Build" in an italic serif at about 28–32 px, body at about 11–12 px, micro-labels at about 8–9 px. Two-tier scale — the serif headline is the only display size, everything else is one quiet tier; the serif/sans pairing is the voice of the page.

**Layout & harmony.** Split grid, estimated by eye: headline and CTAs in the left ~40%, the isometric diagram in the right ~60%, logo strip pinned to the bottom edge. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by the orange CTA. Density is moderate — the diagram fills its half with small labelled blocks and generous white space around them.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: pill CTAs at about 6–8 px, diagram blocks sharp-cornered in isometric. Separation is by tone and hairline rules, not shadow — a faint grid underlays the whole hero. No elevation; the tablet bezel is the only frame.

**Buttons & components.** Two CTAs: a filled orange pill ("Start Building") and an outlined pill ("Explore the Docs"), plus a small orange pill badge above the headline. The filled CTA is the loudest element on the page after the headline; the nav is quiet text links with a second orange pill at the far right.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient and no photography. The imagery is a flat isometric illustration of labelled blocks (Spheres, M3M0, Data Silo) with orange and black tops. Delete it and the hero still reads — headline, CTAs and logo strip survive — so the dependency is soft; the diagram is decoration that explains the product shape.

**Contrast.** Measured: white text on CTA orange #FE5F1D is 3.06:1 — fails 4.5:1 for normal text, so the filled CTA's label is below AA. Black type on white #FFFFFF is about 17:1 — passes. The small grey micro-labels, estimated by eye at about #8A8A8A on white (about 3.5:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the serif-headline + single-orange-accent + isometric-diagram hero is a transferable landing mechanism at low cost, but it is a staged concept mockup and the filled CTA fails contrast, so adapt, not adopt.
