# REF-0052 — Laptop mockup on black, editorial mixed-type hero with sage pill CTA

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Black void #000000 and table #0d0d0d, laptop #363636. The screen is light grey #e7e5e6 with near-black type #1c1c1c. One muted accent: sage #a0a496 on the pill CTA, echoed by a sprout cut-out; the rest is greyscale.

**Typography.** Deliberate mix: bold condensed grotesque caps ("WE PROTECT NOT JUST", "WE PROTECT") alternating with a high-contrast condensed serif and a script italic ("People", "The PLANET.") — display-text pairing with heavy weight contrast. Display cap height ≈48 preview px against small body.

**Layout & harmony.** Laptop slightly off-centre on a black table, a blurred dark object in the background. Screen: stacked mixed-type headline left, a cut-out object (fountain pen with a sprout) centre-right, body and CTA under the headline.

**Shape & surface.** Pill CTA beside a dark circular arrow button; otherwise flat, no containers.

**Buttons & components.** Sage pill "[ book a consultation ]" with a bracket-wrapped label, plus a black circle arrow — a paired CTA.

**Gradient & imagery.** One masked product cut-out on screen; type carries the page without it.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: headline #1c1c1c on #e9e9e9 14.0:1. Failures: body #717171 on #eaeaea 4.06:1, nav ≈2.66:1, CTA label on sage ≈2.1–3.1:1.

## Why the verdict

Mixed-family display type is the transferable idea; it reads as a left-to-right sentence (breaks RTL) and the sage CTA fails contrast.
