# REF-0067 — Compatto — furnishing-market landing page, warm beige photo-led sections

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a full-page furnishing-market landing capture — photo hero, "Who We Are" with a 150+ suppliers stat, a numbered "How We Simplify" section, and a "Why Choose Compatto" card grid — all in warm beige and brown.

**Colour.** Measured: page field warm off-white (#F8F7F3 dominant), hero photo in warm browns (sampled around #5B4828 in shadow), beige card tints throughout. Neutral-only chrome — warm greys, beige and brown — with no hue accent at all; the photography supplies the only colour. Light-first value strategy, with one dark brown card ("No Variety Restrictions") as the single dark inversion near the foot.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: hero headline "Bringing Simplicity In The Furnishing Market" at about 28–32 px grotesque, white on the photo, sentence case; section headlines at about 22–26 px with one word picked out in a lighter brown ("Simplicity", "Compatto"); body at about 12–13 px grey. Two-tier scale, headline roughly 2.5× body. The "150+" and "15+" supplier statistics are set at about 28 px — the numerals are the loudest elements in the "Who We Are" row.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, sparse and generous: hero, stat row, "How We Simplify" with a large step numeral "5" beside a photo card, then a bento-style "Why Choose" grid (three small cards over two wide ones). Spacing is the most generous in this batch — large gaps between sections, small content blocks floating in them — and the hierarchy is by size and position, with the photos doing the rest.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated by eye: large radii (about 16–20 px) on the hero, cards and photos; separation by tone (beige tints, white page, dark brown card) with no shadows and hairline borders only. The hero sits inside a floating white frame with a pill nav bar overlapping its top edge — the page's one layered moment.

**Buttons & components.** One visible action: a small white pill "Start Your Furnishing Journey" on the hero, quieter than the headline. The numbered step card ("5 — After-Sales Support and Maintenance") is the page's main component, pairing a large pale numeral with a photo. The "Why Choose" cards are content, not controls — no buttons, no links visible.

**Gradient & imagery.** Photography is the page: hero interior, workshop photo, hotel-lobby photo, furniture thumbnails. Hard dependency — delete the photos and the page is headlines on beige; the layout has no independent visual idea beyond the warm tint. The step numeral "5" is the one typographic device that would survive without imagery.

**Contrast.** Measured: page field #F8F7F3 against black headlines is very high (about 17:1). White headline on the hero photo depends on the photo being dark enough where the text sits — the headline sits over the darker left half of the image and survives, but the margin is thin and is estimated, not measured, because the photo varies. Grey body copy is estimated by eye at about #6B7280 on off-white, roughly 4.7:1 — passes, estimated, not measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the warm-beige tonal system and the large-numeral step card are calm, transferable devices at medium cost, but the page is hard-dependent on photography and the hero headline's contrast depends on the photo, so adapt, not adopt.
