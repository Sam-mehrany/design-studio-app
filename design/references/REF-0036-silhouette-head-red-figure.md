# REF-0036 — Navy profile head with red figure as mind, digital painting

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Duotone on white: near-black navy `#0e1014` silhouette, saturated red `#991517` → `#ca292a` for the figure and its glow; white `#fcfcfc` ground ≈42%.

**Layout & harmony.** Profile silhouette faces one way with the figure inside the skull; large white negative space carries the composition.

**Gradient & imagery.** Painted glow and soft edges around the red — raster, moderate cost.

**Contrast.** No text. Navy `#0e1014` on white = 19.04:1; red `#991517` on white = 8.49:1.

## Why the verdict

Concept artwork; the duotone-on-white idea is generic, and the directional profile makes it RTL-risky if used as a hero.
