# REF-0047 — Monitor mockup on dark pedestal, industrial 3D-render hero with ghost logo backdrop

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Fully achromatic. Ground #2f2f2f with an oversized ghost wordmark one step darker at #1f1f1f; pedestal near-black #0a0000, stand #5f5657. Screen runs from #464646 (left, machine render) to #919191 (right). Only white and black on the UI.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque, title case, white. Hero cap height ≈20 preview px on a 883-px-wide image; nav and buttons are micro-size.

**Layout & harmony.** Centred monitor on a ridged plinth; ghost logo fills the backdrop behind it. On screen: a dark pill nav bar at top, render left, headline and white pill buttons right.

**Shape & surface.** Pill nav and pill buttons on screen; the environment is tonal greys with no light source drama.

**Buttons & components.** White pill CTAs (#bdbdbd as photographed) against grey — the loudest marks on the screen.

**Gradient & imagery.** On-screen hero is a large 3D machine render; the scene is itself a render. Nothing stands without imagery.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: white headline over the light render area #9f9f9f ≈2.65:1 — fails even as large text; nav #969696 on #1c1c1c 5.76:1.

## Why the verdict

Rejected on contrast: the headline sits on a mid-grey render at ≈2.65:1, and the whole scene is grey-on-grey (low contrast, hard imagery dependency, high cost).

Original file: `pin-mockup-idea-1wr41w0Rf0jA.jpg`. The ghost-wordmark backdrop behind the device is a reusable presentation device in its own right.
