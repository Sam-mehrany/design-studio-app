# REF-0012 — Product photo: silver honeycomb pendant with citrine cells and a gold bee

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — graded grey backdrop #4e4e50 (top) → #6c6b6b → #7a7b77 (bottom), 81% of pixels; one warm accent, citrine/gold #c9bf94–#6b5220, plus silver highlights #f3f1e9.

**Typography** — script-italic watermark "wingedlion.etsy.com" in white along the bottom.

**Layout & harmony** — object centred, chain leaving the top edge, reflection pooling below; one subject on a lit field.

**Shape & surface** — staged studio: a grey sweep with falloff and a reflective floor.

**Gradient & imagery** — the backdrop gradient is what makes the object glow; without the photo nothing is left.

**Contrast** — watermark #c7c0b3 on #797972 = 2.43:1 (fails); citrine highlight on grey 2.79:1, carried by hue not value.

## Why the verdict

Staging mechanism transfers: neutral grey field + one warm accent + reflective ground. The finish is product photography, not interface.
