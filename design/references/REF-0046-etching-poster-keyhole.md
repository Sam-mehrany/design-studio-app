# REF-0046 — Ornate etching-style poster: woman, ribcage, keyhole

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Black ground `#000000` with cream/tan line work `#ad9a7a` / `#887860` / `#605342`; one muted oxblood circle (ribcage) as the only accent.

**Layout & harmony.** Symmetrical: arched frame, central figure, keyhole triangle below, banner ribbons at the foot; every gap filled with roses, smoke and stipple — maximal density.

**Shape & surface.** Etching/stipple texture throughout; framing borders double as structure.

**Gradient & imagery.** Dense hand illustration; heavy to reproduce, impossible to template.

**Contrast.** Cream `#b19d79` on black = 7.97:1.

## Why the verdict

Maximalist gig-poster illustration; the density and texture are the piece, and none of it is reusable as a mechanism.

## Notes

Board "poster". Metal / gig-poster lineage.
