# REF-0084 — Puffer jacket seated by cabinet — plants, driftwood, spotted rug, cream void

**Composition.** The frame is a horizontal still life with a figure in it: the seated figure at left-centre, the cabinet centre-right, the tall plants far right, the spotted rug running along the bottom. Forms are a catalogue of textures: quilted nylon, wood grain, woven basket, spotted wool, glossy ceramic pot. Depth comes from the objects' overlap and the floor line, not from shadow.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is the reference: a "room" built from five objects (stool, cabinet, driftwood, plants, rug) on a seamless void — a minimal environment that still feels inhabited. **Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the density lesson: this is the fullest image in the batch, yet it still reads as minimal because every object has its own zone and the void above absorbs the clutter — minimalism by spacing, not by object count.
