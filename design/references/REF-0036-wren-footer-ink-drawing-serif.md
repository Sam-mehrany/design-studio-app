# REF-0036 — Cream footer with cobalt ink drawing and serif links

## Nature

Footer on its own with an ink drawing and serif links.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Cream `#fcf4ea` ground, cobalt ink `#2c4e8e` for the line drawing; text in warm greys and near-black — duotone, muted.

**Typography.** Transitional serif for the links and wordmark register; column heads near-black `#14171c`, links mid-grey `#5f666e`.

**Layout & harmony.** Wordmark + tagline left, link columns right, drawing across the lower half.

**Shape & surface.** Flat, frameless; content runs to the edges.

**Buttons & components.** Text links only.

**Gradient & imagery.** A hand-drawn cobalt pen illustration is the image; without it the footer is a few lines of text on cream.

**Contrast.** Heads 16.3:1; links `#5f666e` on cream = 5.3:1; tagline 7.0:1; ink `#2c4e8e` on cream = 7.5:1 — all pass.

## Why the verdict

Same duotone-illustration mechanism as REF-0035 with better contrast; still illustration-dependent.
