# REF-0037 — Izum.Study — Russian coding-course landing on laptop (orange gradient, wireframe blob)

## Read

Values below: orange gradient and black header were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a Russian coding-course landing on a laptop — "НАУЧИСЬ ВЕРСТАТЬ ВМЕСТЕ С IZUM.STUDY" bottom-left over a black-to-orange gradient, a glowing wireframe blob above, a small nav on top.

**Colour.** Measured: orange gradient #A43309 at mid-screen, black header #000000. Two hues — black and orange — with the gradient doing the work of a background image; white type is the only other colour.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline in a grotesque all-caps at about 20–24 px, nav at about 8–9 px, small body at about 9–10 px. Two-tier scale — the caps headline is the only display size, everything else is microcopy. All copy is Russian (Cyrillic).

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: nav across the top, the wireframe blob centred in the upper half, headline bottom-left, a small text column and a white pill CTA bottom-right. Hierarchy is ranked by size (the blob dominates by scale and glow), then by the headline. Density is low — four elements on a gradient field.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the white pill CTA at full pill radius, everything else sharp. Separation is by the gradient's own light fall — no cards, no borders, no shadows inside the screen.

**Buttons & components.** One white filled pill CTA bottom-right ("ХОЧУ В ТОП" with a small icon) and text links ("TELEGRAM", "DRIBBBLE") bottom-centre. The CTA is the loudest control after the headline; the blob is louder than both, which is the page's gamble.

**Gradient & imagery.** A black-to-orange gradient backs the screen and a wireframe line-art blob (concentric contour lines, glowing orange) is the hero illustration. Delete the blob and the page is a headline on a gradient — it still reads, but it loses the only distinctive shape — so the dependency is soft.

**Contrast.** Measured: white on the orange gradient #A43309 is 6.87:1 — passes (measured, from the sampled gradient value). White on black #000000 is 21:1 — passes. The small grey body copy over the orange mid-tones, estimated at about #D8D8D8 (about 3:1 estimated — fails for small text, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the gradient field + glowing wireframe blob + caps headline is a transferable, low-cost course-landing mechanism, but it is a staged concept mockup with Russian-only copy and the blob carries the page's identity, so adapt, not adopt.
