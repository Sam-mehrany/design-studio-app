# REF-0059 — Death Star hairline drawing on charcoal — phone wallpaper

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — charcoal `#141414` (≈80%), lines in greys `#2b2b2b`–`#424242`, brightest 0.5% at `#989898`. Neutral, zero saturated pixels.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — a single sphere of concentric hairlines centred, lots of margin.

**Shape & surface** — uniform hairline strokes only.

**Gradient & imagery** — line drawing is the whole image.

**Contrast** — typical line `#424242` on `#141414` 1.83:1: deliberately low, ambient.

## Why the verdict

The useful mechanism is a near-invisible line drawing as an ambient dark background (≈1.8:1), which sits behind content without competing. Low confidence: it is a wallpaper.
