# REF-0024 — DIY hanging jar planter, craft video still

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Near-neutral light ladder — tablecloth `#eef1f0` / `#e7e8e7` / `#dcdbd9` / `#c6c7c3`, one warm mid `#84766a` (hands, cord). No accent; strongest saturated sample is a dull khaki `#a79064`.

**Layout & harmony.** Single centred subject (glass jar on black cord, hands either side) in a portrait 4:5 video frame; no grid.

**Gradient & imagery.** The image is the photograph; nothing remains without it.

**Contrast.** No text. Tonal range is compressed into the top quarter — the four surface clusters sit within ~`#c6`–`#f1`, so figure/ground separation leans on the dark cord alone.

Craft-tutorial video still with no design artefact; fails imagery_dep (hard) and carries nothing transferable to UI.
