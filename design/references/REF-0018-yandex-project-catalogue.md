# REF-0018 — Yandex careers programme catalogue — charcoal cards with pattern headers on cream

## Read

*Figures measured off the image with Python/PIL (median-sampled hex, WCAG 2.x ratios, ink heights in original-image pixels unless stated); type-scale ratios are derived from those ink heights; radii in px are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Cream page `#fef7e9`, charcoal cards `#222321`, and exactly two accents — mint `#8ddbac` and orange `#eb611d` — used alternately in the card header patterns and once on an arrow button (`#86cfa0`). Cream `#f0efe8` arrow buttons.

**Typography.** Large two-line page heading vs card titles vs small descriptions: a dramatic scale, two weights, sentence case. Grotesque (Yandex's YS family).

**Layout & harmony.** Asymmetric checkerboard grid: six cards leave deliberate gaps filled with small black pixel-arrow and route-diagram illustrations. Each card: pattern header strip → title → description → circular arrow button bottom-right.

**Shape & surface.** Large radius (~24px) cards; the dark body overlaps the pattern strip with its own rounded top, giving a tab-like layer. Flat.

**Buttons & components.** One circular arrow button per card, cream on charcoal — consistent, low-key.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradients. Pixel/geometric patterns (grids, circuit lines, dots) as header art — flat vector, cheap. The cards stand without them (soft).

**Contrast.** Heading `#222321` on cream = 14.8:1; card titles cream on charcoal = 13.7:1; descriptions `#9e9d99` on `#222321` = 5.82:1. Dark on mint = 9.67:1. All pass.

## Why the verdict

Shipped and coherent (two-accent system, real copy lengths). Adapt rather than adopt: the arrow-to-the-right buttons and left-to-right diagram flow need mirroring, and the patterns are brand-specific.
