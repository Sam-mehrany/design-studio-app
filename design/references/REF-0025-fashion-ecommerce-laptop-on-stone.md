# REF-0025 — Fashion e-commerce landing on laptop on stone ("Your design here" template)

## Read

Values below: cream background and black blazer were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a fashion e-commerce landing on a laptop staged on stacked stones — a black-and-white portrait left, a near-empty cream field right with a top nav and a "YOUR DESIGN HERE ›" placeholder line.

**Colour.** Measured: cream screen #D9D3C7, black blazer #171813. Monochrome warm: the cream field, the black portrait and black micro-type are the whole palette — no accent hue anywhere.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: nav items at about 10–11 px tracked caps, "YOUR DESIGN HERE ›" at about 14–16 px light caps. Two-tier scale, both tiers small — there is no display headline on this screen at all, which is the point of the template: the portrait is the headline.

**Layout & harmony.** Split grid, estimated by eye: portrait left (~35%), empty cream field right (~65%), nav centred across the top. Hierarchy is ranked by position (the portrait) and by the emptiness around it — the void is the luxury signal. Density is very low — one photo, one nav, one placeholder line.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: everything sharp-cornered, no radius anywhere. Separation is by tone only — the portrait's edge against the cream field. No cards, no borders, no shadow inside the screen.

**Buttons & components.** There are no buttons; the only controls are the six text nav links (New Arrivals underlined as active). The loudest element is the portrait, not any control — the page sells by image, not by interface.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. One black-and-white portrait photograph carries the entire left of the screen and all of the page's visual weight. Delete it and the page is an empty cream field with a nav — so the dependency is hard.

**Contrast.** Measured: black blazer #171813 on cream #D9D3C7 is 11.98:1 — passes (that pair is the portrait against its field, not text). Black micro-type on cream is about 15:1 estimated — passes. The thin nav type at 10–11 px is the smallest text on screen and the most fragile at this size.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the portrait-left / void-right luxury split is a transferable fashion-landing mechanism at low cost, but the screen is a mockup template with placeholder copy ("YOUR DESIGN HERE") and the composition collapses without its photograph (imagery_dep hard), so adapt, not adopt.
