# REF-0054 — White futuristic tracked excavator with glass cab — 3D render against stone

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points). One line of context: a 3D render of a futuristic tracked excavator — white sculpted body, glass cab, long arm — staged against pale stone. The only civilian machine on the board. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: white body sampled at #FEFFFF on a pale stone field, with black tracks and black glass as the only dark masses. Strictly neutral — no hue at all — so the whole image is carried by value and edge. The white-on-white staging is deliberate: the machine is distinguished from its background by shadow and black accents, not by colour.

**Composition & form.** Side profile, machine centred, the excavator arm rising to the upper left and the bucket hanging at the frame's left edge — the arm's diagonal is the image's main line, against the horizontal track base. The body is the curviest form on the board: smooth, car-like surfacing with a continuous glass canopy, a deliberate break from the board's angular military vehicles.

**Light & surface.** Soft, even light with a glossy floor reflection; the white panels carry subtle grey shading that models the curves, and the black glass cab is the darkest, glossiest element. Surfaces are polished and seamless — panel gaps are hairlines, not seams. Small dark lettering on the arm ("PCCAE", illegible at this size) is the only type, and it is decoration, not information.

**Typography.** Effectively none — a few small dark glyphs on the arm and cab are illegible at this resolution and carry no readable message.

**Contrast.** Measured: black tracks and glass against the white body and pale field are high separation; white body against the pale stone background is low separation — the machine's outline softens where white meets white, and only the black track band anchors it. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the strongest alternative-form reference on the board — smooth, car-like surfacing and a white-on-white palette against a run of angular, dark military renders — but a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
