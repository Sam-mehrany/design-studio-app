# REF-0057 — Laptop on mounting arm over lavender-to-rust gradient, techno-italic pricing screen

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Mesh-gradient field: muted violet #635e7d top-left, lavender #ada5c5 lower-left, rust #b4593c lower-right, near-black #170d12 right edge. Screen is dark charcoal #27282f with an orange glow (#c75b49) bleeding up behind the cards; CTA orange #fe713c. Chips are off-white #eeecea.

**Typography.** Wide squared techno display face, italic all-caps ("BUILT FOR YOUR BUSINESS", cap ≈24 preview px) with small sans body; the page frame uses tracked mono caps labels ("[16 - 28]", "PRICE SCREEN [MAIN PAGE]", cap ≈8).

**Layout & harmony.** Laptop on an articulated arm, seen from below, filling the centre; mono labels pinned to the top corners and centre like a spec sheet. Screen: centred headline, body, CTA, then two pricing cards side by side with chips at the bottom.

**Shape & surface.** Cards have chamfered folder-tab headers separated from the body by a dashed line — an asymmetric shape language. Glow sits behind translucent cards.

**Buttons & components.** Orange CTA with a separate arrow segment. Feature chips are small white rectangles.

**Gradient & imagery.** Mesh gradient environment and on-screen glow; strip them and the dark pricing layout still holds, but the shot does not.

**Contrast.** Measured: headline white on #2b2c32 13.56:1; frame labels #b1a9b0 on #40393d 4.90:1. Failures: body #ad989a on #665055 2.72:1, card body ≈1.6:1 where it sits over the glow, white on the #fe713c CTA ≈2.75:1.

## Why the verdict

Presentation is costly and imagery-bound; the spec-sheet mono labelling and the tabbed pricing cards are adaptable mechanisms. Body copy over the glow fails badly.
