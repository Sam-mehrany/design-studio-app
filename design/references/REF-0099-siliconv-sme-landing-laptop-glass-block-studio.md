## Nature

Low-angle laptop in a white glass-block studio; rendered.

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Screen `#1e1e1e`, white headline, blue `#027ef5` on both buttons, B/W architectural photo (`#677c83` mid). Scene is pale grey `#c5c5c5`–`#d9d9d9` with a gridded glass-block light pattern. One hue only.

**Typography.** Bold neo-grotesque, title case, three-line headline ("Unleash the Potential of Your SME with Silicon.V"); small grey body; outline pill tag above. Hero-to-body ~4× (*estimated* — the screen is in perspective).

**Layout & harmony.** Split hero: copy and CTA left, full-height photo right, a stat ("250+") starting below the fold. Moderate density.

**Shape & surface.** Small radii on buttons, outline pill tag, flat panels.

**Buttons & components.** Blue filled button in the nav and under the copy.

**Gradient & imagery.** B/W photo is atmosphere (soft).

**Contrast.** Headline white on `#212121` = 16.1:1; tag `#8d8f8e` = 4.96:1; nav `#707170` = 3.34:1 — fails. White on the blue `#027ef5` = 3.96:1 — fails for small button text.

## Why the verdict

Competent, generic dark SaaS hero; the bright studio staging is the reason it was saved. Adapt, med.
