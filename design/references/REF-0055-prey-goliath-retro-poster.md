# REF-0055 — Prey 'Goliath 5' retro-futurist propaganda poster

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — split ground: slate `#323838` left, navy-charcoal `#1c1e24` right, black `#050505` foot bar. Object colours are muted warm: cream `#eeddb5`, orange `#b06122`, rust red `#a01609`. Warm object on a cool ground; nothing semantic.

**Typography** — "GOLIATH" in thin geometric caps (Art Deco lineage, *estimated*), cream `#faf2c3`, with a superscript "5". One display line; everything else is tiny.

**Layout & harmony** — vertical rocket on the centre axis, wordmark across the lower third, a ghosted blueprint of the same vehicle on the right panel. The split ground does the framing.

**Shape & surface** — flat vector shapes with hard edges, no shadows; blueprint layer as low-contrast line work behind.

**Gradient & imagery** — no gradients. The illustration is the poster.

**Contrast** — wordmark on the measured ground mode `#2c292c` 12.67:1; on the left slate 10.55:1; on the right navy 14.72:1.

## Why the verdict

Transferable mechanism: a ghosted technical drawing as a second layer behind a flat object, on a two-tone split ground, with one display line. Flat vector so cost is low; the illustration itself is not reusable.

## Notes

Game promotional art (Prey, Arkane Studios), hence `kind: brand`. Superscript numeral in the wordmark is LTR-specific.
