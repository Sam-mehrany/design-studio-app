# REF-0086 — Phone mockup on terrazzo — NewCode process page, oversized cream caps overlapped by glossy orange-red 3D forms

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Screen near-black `#181419`, cream type `#e2dedf`, one hue: glossy orange-red forms `#d55622` with highlights `#dcaf94`, red logo mark `#da1010`. Scene: taupe tray `#3e3b36` on near-black terrazzo `#070706`.

**Typography.** Oversized ALL-CAPS grotesque, tight leading, five lines filling the screen width ("VAN DIGITALE CHAOS NAAR EEN ECOSYSTEEM DAT KLOPT."); measured line run 30px on a phone ~200px wide in the file. Body in small sentence-case sans. Two-tier, dramatic scale.

**Layout & harmony.** Poster logic on a phone: headline as the image, 3D forms passing both behind and in front of letters (they occlude parts of "NAAR EEN" and "KLOPT"), tag + logo at top, body bottom-left. Broken grid.

**Shape & surface.** Soft pill/lozenge 3D shapes with specular highlights against flat type. No UI chrome.

**Buttons & components.** None visible beyond a "Process" tag.

**Gradient & imagery.** Forms are decoration — delete them and the type poster stands (soft) — but rendering them is costly.

**Contrast.** Cream headline on near-black = 13.7:1; body sampled `#6b6b72` = 3.47:1 (tiny text, likely lighter as designed). The real failure is occlusion: forms cover glyphs.

## Why the verdict

Shipped identity work with a strong type-as-image move. Occluding the headline with renders costs legibility; take the scale and the single hue, not the overlap. Adapt, med.
