# REF-0018 — Stock banner: yellow hexagon outlines and clip-art bees on dark brown

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — dark warm brown #211b18 (78%); honey yellow #c5ae2c / #d3ba2e for outlines and bees, olive #695711 / #463b18 in shaded cells.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — scattered hex outlines across a wide banner, bees placed loosely; no grid discipline.

**Shape & surface** — outline hexes at a medium stroke over a flat ground.

**Gradient & imagery** — clip-art bees are decoration; the pattern stands without them.

**Contrast** — yellow #c5ae2c on #211b18 7.67:1 (brightest 2% #d3ba2e: 8.77:1).

## Why the verdict

Clip-art finish and loose composition; REF-0015 carries the same dark-ground + one-accent idea far better.
