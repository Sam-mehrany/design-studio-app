# REF-0024 — Security dashboard on a studio monitor

## Nature

Studio monitor on a stand in a seamless grey set, perfect soft light, no grain → rendered mockup.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Grey studio wall `#c2c2c2`; screen `#f0f0f0` with cards in the same tone. One vivid orange-red `#f85020` carries the 'High' severity block and the donut; a sequential pale ramp (`#f0c8c0`) finishes the donut; 'Critical' is near-black `#444444`.

**Typography.** Geometric sans (Poppins class, estimated). At 600px the KPI numerals are ~9px ink — the screen is too small in frame to measure a scale.

**Layout & harmony.** Sidebar + four KPI cards across + two large cards (stacked-bar overview, attack-surface semi-donut). Card grid, moderate density.

**Shape & surface.** Medium radii on cards; flat tonal cards on a same-tone ground. The device is a rendered monitor on a stand against a graded wall.

**Buttons & components.** Segmented All/Open/Close pill; status chips on KPI cards.

**Gradient & imagery.** The device render and wall gradient are the composition; delete them and what remains is a small flat screenshot.

**Contrast.** KPI value `#0d0d0d` on `#f0f0f0` = 17.1:1. Meta text samples at ~1.9:1, but at this resolution that figure is unreliable (glyphs are 1–2px).

## Why the verdict

Saved for the device presentation; useful only as a mockup technique, and the UI is too small to evidence anything.
