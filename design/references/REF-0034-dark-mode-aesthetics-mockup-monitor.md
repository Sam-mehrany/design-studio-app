# REF-0034 — Dark Mode Aesthetics — mockup-product landing on monitor (17 scenes, 3680×2760)

## Read

Values below: screen white was **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a mockup-product landing on a monitor watched by two silhouetted people — "DARK MODE AESTHETICS" in black condensed caps left, a 2×2 spec grid right ("Attention to details", "Easy to use", "17 unique scenes", "3680×2760 resolution").

**Colour.** Measured: screen white #FFFFFF. Monochrome: black type on white, one black pill CTA ("Explore more"), no accent hue anywhere — the product being sold is dark-mode photography, but the page itself is white.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "DARK MODE AESTHETICS" in a condensed grotesque at about 28–32 px, spec numerals "17" and "3680×2760" at about 20–24 px bold, body at about 10–11 px, micro-labels at about 8–9 px caps. Two-tier scale — the headline and the spec numerals are the display sizes, everything else is one small tier.

**Layout & harmony.** Split grid, estimated by eye: headline and body left (~45%), a 2×2 grid of spec cells right (~55%) divided by hairlines. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by enclosure (each spec in its own cell). Density is low — the screen is mostly white with two content blocks.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the black pill CTA at full pill radius, the spec grid sharp-cornered with hairline dividers. Separation is by the hairline grid and by tone — no cards, no shadows, no borders beyond the monitor's frame.

**Buttons & components.** One black filled pill CTA ("Explore more") under the body copy. It is the only control and it is quiet — black on white, same weight as the headline's first line. The spec cells are the content: two of them carry only a numeral and a label.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient and no photography on the screen itself. The imagery is the staging — two silhouetted people at a monitor — which is outside the screen and not part of the design. The screen stands entirely on type and grid.

**Contrast.** Measured: black on white #FFFFFF is about 17:1 — passes (from the sampled white). The small grey body copy, estimated at about #777777 on white (about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline, estimated, not measured). The "17" and "3680×2760" numerals in black on white pass easily.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the condensed-caps headline + 2×2 spec grid with hero numerals is a transferable, low-cost product-landing mechanism, but the screen advertises a mockup pack rather than a product, and it is a staged concept shot, so adapt, not adopt.
