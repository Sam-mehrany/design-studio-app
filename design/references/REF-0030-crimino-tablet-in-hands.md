# REF-0030 — Neutral sales dashboard on a tablet held in hands

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Neutral only: screen and cards `#fefefe`, idle bars `#f2f2f2`, active bar `#343434`, donut `#2a2a2a` / `#949494`; backdrop `#f6f6f4`. Only hue: muted green delta chips `#78a088`.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque; value `$1,992.34` large regular, muted helper `#7f7f7f` small. Perspective, scale not measured.

**Layout & harmony.** Top nav tabs, KPI row, donut + bar chart + table. Moderate density.

**Shape & surface.** Large radii; bars are rounded capsules. Tablet photographed in two hands on near-white.

**Buttons & components.** Tab nav, 'Last 7 days' dropdown pills.

**Gradient & imagery.** Hands-and-device photograph is the composition.

**Contrast.** Values `#000000` on `#fcfcfc` ≈ 20:1. Helper `#7f7f7f` on `#f8f8f8` = 3.8:1 — fails. Idle bars `#f2f2f2` on `#fefefe` = 1.11:1 — the chart's own marks are near-invisible.

## Why the verdict

Mockup; the monochrome chart has near-zero mark contrast, so nothing to take beyond the presentation.
