# REF-0062 — Monitor-on-desk mockup — full-bleed landscape hero with centred sans headline, brand cards on desk

## Nature

Monitor on a desk in a real interior with planting → photographed (low certainty).

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye.

**Colour.** UI chrome is neutral only: nav bar `#292929`, nav text `#a3a3a1`, one off-white "Contact us" button `#f9f9f2`. All colour comes from the landscape photo (foliage mid-tone `#5e633a`, sky `#f1f6f7`). The scene adds warm wood (`#bc9f90`) and a dark garden through glass (`#0c0d0c`).

**Typography.** Three-line centred neo-grotesque headline in sentence case, regular weight, white; tiny monospaced "SCROLL" tag sitting in a black chip. One sans plus a mono accent. Headline-to-nav roughly 3× (*estimated*).

**Layout & harmony.** Single centred column over a full-bleed photo; thin corner brackets frame the hero like a viewfinder. Sparse, nothing competes with the image.

**Shape & surface.** The contact button has one chamfered corner rather than a radius — a cut-corner shape echoing the shelter's geometry. Everything else is square.

**Buttons & components.** One light button in the nav; the rest of the nav is text links with a small orange caret on the active item. Quiet relative to the photo.

**Gradient & imagery.** The photograph is the design: remove it and you have a black bar and three centred lines. No scrim behind the headline.

**Contrast.** Headline `#fffff0` on the foliage median `#5e633a` = 6.27:1, but the photo runs to `#f1f6f7` sky directly above it — with no scrim the ratio depends on where the line lands. Nav `#a3a3a1` on `#292929` = 5.77:1.

## Why the verdict

Saved for the presentation: real desk, matching business cards, garden reflected in the glass — the mockup sells the brand system, not just the page. Hard photo dependency and a high-cost staged render → adapt.
