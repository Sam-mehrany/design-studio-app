# REF-0047 — Influence Pro Trading — "About Project" case-study boards (orange container, crane hero)

## Read

Values below: orange container was **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a case-study presentation in two stacked boards — "ABOUT PROJECT" with Goal/Result columns around a hanging orange container, and "INFLUENCE PRO TRADING" in giant condensed white type over a crane photograph with "WE ARE THE BEST IN OUR FIELD".

**Colour.** Measured: orange container #BE4B1C. Single accent: the orange container and the orange crane (about #E85A1A) against dark charcoal (top board, about #25262A) and pale sky blue (bottom board, about #B8C8D8). The orange is the brand colour — it appears only in the photographs, not in the UI.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "ABOUT PROJECT" and "INFLUENCE PRO TRADING" in a condensed grotesque at about 56–72 px, "GOAL" and "RESULT" labels at about 16–18 px, body at about 10–11 px, "WE ARE THE BEST IN OUR FIELD" at about 12–14 px bold caps. Dramatic scale — the board titles are roughly 6× the body — with one grotesque face throughout.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: top board is a dark grid with Goal text left, Result text right and the container photograph centred between them; bottom board is the crane photograph with the title across the top, nav links beneath it, an orange circle arrow button left and a small text block right. Hierarchy is ranked by size (board titles first), then by the photographs. Density is moderate — two boards, each with one title and two text blocks.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the orange circle button is the only curve; everything else is sharp-cornered. Separation is by tone — the dark top board against the pale bottom board — with a hairline grid behind the top board. No cards, no shadows, no elevation.

**Buttons & components.** One orange circle button with a white arrow (bottom board, left) and a hamburger icon (top-left of the bottom board). Both are quiet relative to the giant titles — the titles are the navigation here, the buttons are annotations.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Two photographs — the hanging container and the crane against sky — carry both boards' entire visual weight. Delete them and the boards are giant type on flat colour with small text blocks — the composition survives as a poster but the subject is gone — so the dependency is hard.

**Contrast.** Measured: white on the orange container #BE4B1C is 4.99:1 — passes (measured, from the sampled container; the orange circle button on sky is a similar pair). White on the dark top board #25262A is about 13:1 estimated — passes. The small grey body copy on the dark board, estimated at about #9A9A9A (about 7:1 estimated — passes, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the two-board case-study presentation (Goal/Result + giant-title hero) is a transferable, low-cost portfolio mechanism, but it collapses without its photographs (imagery_dep hard) and it is a concept presentation shot, so adapt, not adopt.
