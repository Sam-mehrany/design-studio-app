# REF-0028 — Fintech cards UI on a laptop with marketing caption band

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Board `#eaecf0`, screen `#f2f4f6`, white cards `#fcfefe`, black band `#000002`. One neon lime `#d8f800` (logo dots, promo tiles, a radial glow on black). Caption band is a grey gradient `#4a4c50`.

**Typography.** Light-weight geometric sans (Outfit/Urbanist class, estimated) everywhere, including a large thin caption (~50px ink). H1 'Cards' 24, section title 20, nav cap 7 (px).

**Layout & harmony.** Top nav with black pill, full-width dark card carousel band, then three columns (physical card, news list, promo tiles + partners).

**Shape & surface.** Large card radii, pill buttons and chips; laptop photographed on a white surface with a circular portrait inset.

**Buttons & components.** Black pill 'Order Card'; lime tiles used as feature buttons with dark text (`#1a2900` on `#d8fc08` = 13.1:1).

**Gradient & imagery.** Lime glow behind the black band, portrait photo, laptop render and caption gradient — the board is made of imagery.

**Contrast.** Nav `#1e2021` on white = 15.9:1; lime on black = 17.3:1. Body grey `#8c8e90` = 3.2:1, stat labels `#97999b` = 2.8:1 and dates 3.9:1 — **fail**.

## Why the verdict

A Behance-style presentation board; the device and caption are the point. Lime-on-black accent is the only portable idea.
