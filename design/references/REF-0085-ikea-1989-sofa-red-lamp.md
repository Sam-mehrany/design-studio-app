# REF-0085 — IKEA 1989 — woman in red satin on white sofa, red pendant lamp, cropped wordmark

## Read

This is a photography / mood reference, not a UI screen — no UI archetype applies. Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); proportions estimated by eye. One line of context: a campaign photograph styled as a 1989 IKEA poster — a woman in a red satin dress reclining on a white sofa, a red pendant lamp on a black-and-white stand at right, a vase of grasses at left, with a cropped "IKEA" wordmark and "1989" across the top.

**Colour.** Measured: light void #D7D1D5–#FAF5EF, black #000000, red satin and lamp (estimated ~#B01E3C). The palette is strict duotone: white/cream plus black, with red as the single accent — the dress and the lamp are the only red in the frame, and they rhyme with each other across the sofa. Colour means "the red things are the point"; everything else is neutral.

**Composition.** The sofa is a horizontal band across the lower middle; the figure reclines along it, head centre, legs left; the lamp hangs at right as a vertical counterweight; the wordmark is cropped by the top edge, bleeding off the frame. The upper half is empty void with a soft radial glow behind the figure. The crop of the wordmark is deliberate — the brand is half out of frame, which makes the image feel like a found poster rather than a layout.

**Light & form.** Soft, glowing studio light with a halo behind the figure — the only image in the batch with a visible light source as a compositional device (the glow and the lamp). Forms are simple and rounded: the sofa's cylindrical bolsters, the lamp's red sphere, the dress's satin drape. The satin is the only high-sheen surface in the batch.

**Typography.** The typography is the image's headline: "IKEA" in a heavy grotesque, cropped at both edges (estimated ~120 px cap height at this scale), and "1989" in a light grotesque at the top right. The crop is the design decision — the wordmark is treated as a graphic shape, not as a readable label. No other text.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is a minimal room reduced to four objects: sofa, lamp, vase, dress. The white sofa on the white void nearly disappears; only its bolster shadows and the figure's weight on it give it form. The red lamp is the set's exclamation mark, placed high and right to balance the figure's low-left mass.

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the accent-rhyme mechanism: one colour, two objects (dress, lamp), placed apart so the eye travels between them — the same device as a single accent used in two places on a screen.

**Contrast.** Black wordmark on light void #D7D1D5 ≈ 13:1 (computed from sampled colours) — passes. The red dress on the white sofa reads by value and saturation, not by luminance contrast; the lamp's red sphere is the darkest red in the frame and reads as a solid disc against the pale void.

## Why the verdict

Adapt (provisional, as a mood reference): the one-accent-two-objects rhyme and the cropped-wordmark-as-shape are directly stealable for campaign and landing imagery, but this is a brand campaign photograph, not a UI pattern, so it can never be adopt — adapt, as imagery direction only.
