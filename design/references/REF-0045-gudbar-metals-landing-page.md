# REF-0045 — Gudbar Metals — "Precision Stock. Ready to Cut." B&W industrial landing page

## Read

Values below: white section and dark photo were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: an industrial metal supplier's landing page, shown as a flat screenshot — a black-and-white welding photograph hero with "Precision Stock. Ready to Cut.", a logo strip ("Trusted by fabricators across Central Texas"), an "About" section and a steel-stock photograph.

**Colour.** Measured: white section #F5F5F5, dark hero photo #373332. Monochrome: black-and-white photographs, black type on white, grey nav pills — no accent hue anywhere.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: hero headline "Precision Stock. Ready to Cut." in a grotesque at about 28–32 px, section headline "We're stocked and ready across Central Texas" at about 22–24 px, body at about 11–12 px in two columns, nav at about 9–10 px. Two-tier scale — the headlines are the display size, everything else is one small tier.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: dark photo hero with nav on top, a white logo strip, a white About section with a two-column body, a full-width steel photograph. Hierarchy is ranked by size (hero headline first), then by the photographs' tonal weight. Density is moderate — four stacked sections, each with one job.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the "CONTACT" pill and the "ABOUT" tag at small pill radius, everything else sharp-cornered. Separation is by tone — the dark hero against the white sections — with a hairline rule under the logo strip. No cards, no shadows, no elevation.

**Buttons & components.** One white outline pill CTA ("Start Delivery") in the hero and one grey pill ("CONTACT") in the nav. Both are quiet relative to the photographs — the images do the selling, the pills only catch the click. The logo strip (six fabricator marks) is the trust component.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Two black-and-white photographs — the welder and the steel stock — carry the page's entire visual weight. Delete them and the page is black type on white with a logo strip — so the dependency is hard.

**Contrast.** Measured: white type on the dark hero photo #373332 is 11.45:1 — passes (measured, from the sampled photo tone; the headline sits on the darker part of the image). Black on the white section #F5F5F5 is about 17:1 — passes. The small grey body copy, estimated at about #777777 on white (about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the photo-hero + logo-strip + two-column About + photo band is a transferable, low-cost industrial-landing mechanism, but the page collapses without its photographs (imagery_dep hard) and it is a concept screenshot, so adapt, not adopt.
