# REF-0062 — TradingLayer — copy-trading bot landing page, blue accent, pricing and FAQ

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels, WCAG ratio computed from sampled colours); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a full-page SaaS landing capture for a Telegram copy-trading bot — hero, seven reasons, steps, pricing ($500/$150/Custom), FAQ, closing CTA — with phone and tablet UI screenshots as the imagery.

**Colour.** Measured: primary blue sampled at #3053EB; page field near-white (#EFF0F2 dominant). Single accent — that blue — used for the primary buttons, the "Most Popular" badge, checkmarks and the highlighted "Higher Conversion" card; everything else is black, white and pale blue-grey. The blue is the only hue, and it always means "act" or "this one" — a disciplined single-accent system.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: hero headline "Maximize revenue with One-Tap Copy Trading Bot" at about 32–40 px grotesque, with "Copy Trading Bot" in blue as the only colour-in-type; section headlines at about 24–28 px; body at about 12–13 px. Two-tier scale, hero roughly 3× body. Prices ($500, $150, Custom) are set at about 24 px bold — the numerals are the loudest elements in the pricing row, louder than the tier names.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, two-page spread in the capture: left page hero → reasons grid → steps; right page pricing → FAQ → CTA → footer. The reasons grid is the densest part — one wide card plus a blue highlight card, then three small cards, then two wide cards — a deliberate 2+3+2 rhythm that avoids a monotonous grid. Spacing is even and generous between sections.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated by eye: medium radii (about 12–16 px) on cards, pill buttons, separation by hairline border plus a very light blue tint on the popular tier — border-over-shadow throughout, no drop shadows. The pricing panel sits on a pale blue gradient wash that is the page's only large decorative field.

**Buttons & components.** Primary buttons are solid blue pills ("Get API Access", "Start Free Setup"); secondary are white/grey outline pills ("See Example", "Book a Demo"). FAQ is an accordion with chevrons; pricing checkmarks are small blue dots. Controls are small and consistent — the page's loudest elements are headlines and prices, not buttons.

**Gradient & imagery.** Imagery is UI-as-image: phone screenshots of the Telegram bot in the hero and steps, a tablet screenshot in the closing CTA. No photography, no illustration. Removing the phone screenshots would leave the copy intact — the page argues in words — but the product would be unproven; soft dependency. The blue gradient wash behind pricing is subtle and decorative.

**Contrast.** Measured: blue #3053EB on white is 5.89:1 — passes for body text and buttons. Black headlines on near-white are about 17:1 — pass. White text on the solid blue buttons is the same 5.89:1 — passes. No failing pair measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the most disciplined single-accent system in this batch — blue always means act-or-highlight, at low cost (flat colour, type, screenshots, no heavy effects) — but a concept capture, so adapt, not adopt.
