# REF-0046 — Stoop logistics — "We're available across the globe" landing scroll (slide 1 of 9)

## Read

Values below: cream background and orange card were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a logistics company's landing scroll, shown as a long screenshot — a partner-logo strip, "We're available across the globe" with a warehouse photograph, "Industries we support" with four cards (Food & Beverage in orange), and a dark "Demanding services across industries" section starting below.

**Colour.** Measured: cream page #F1EEE9, orange card #C64A0E, dark section #373634. Single accent: the orange marks one industry card ("Food & Beverage") and the forklift in the photograph; the rest is cream, black type and the dark section. The orange is categorical (the active card), not decorative.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: section headlines ("We're available across the globe", "Industries we support with reliable logistics solutions", "Demanding services across industries") in a grotesque at about 26–30 px, body at about 11–12 px, card titles ("Manufacturing", "Food & Beverage", "Heavy Equipment", "Healthcare") at about 14–15 px semibold, numbered labels ("01."–"04.") at about 10–11 px. Two-tier scale — the headlines are the display size, everything else is one quiet tier.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: logo strip, centred headline with a two-column body, full-bleed warehouse photograph, centred headline, a four-card row divided by hairlines, then a dark section. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headlines first), then by the orange card, which is the only coloured cell. Density is moderate — five sections in one long scroll.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: everything sharp-cornered, no radius anywhere. Separation is by hairlines between the four cards and by tone — the cream page against the dark section. No shadows, no elevation.

**Buttons & components.** One black pill CTA ("About Stoop") beside the first headline, small orange-dot eyebrow tags ("About", "Industry Coverage", "Service Provided") above each section, and line icons in each industry card (warehouse, burger, forklift, molecule). The CTA is quiet relative to the headlines; the orange card is louder than any control.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. One warehouse photograph (forklift with boxes between orange racks) fills a full-bleed band. Delete it and the page still reads — headlines, cards and the dark section survive — so the dependency is soft; the photo adds scale, not structure.

**Contrast.** Measured: white type on the orange card #C64A0E is 4.79:1 — passes 4.5:1 (measured). Black type #1A1A1A on cream #F1EEE9 is 15.04:1 — passes (measured). White on the dark section #373634 is 12.07:1 — passes (measured). The small grey body copy, estimated at about #777777 on cream (about 4.2:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the centred-headline + full-bleed photo band + four-card industry row with one accent card + dark closing section is a transferable, low-cost B2B-landing mechanism, but it is a concept screenshot (lorem ipsum body copy, identical card descriptions) and only slide 1 of 9 was supplied, so adapt, not adopt.
