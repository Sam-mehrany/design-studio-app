# REF-0087 — Hatton Labs — man in leather jacket watching stacked CRT TVs, white void

## Read

This is a photography / mood reference, not a UI screen — no UI archetype applies. Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); proportions estimated by eye. One line of context: a fashion editorial photograph — a man in a black leather jacket seen from behind, seated in a chrome-and-canvas chair, watching two stacked CRT televisions (one showing a bunny logo, one "HATTON LABS"), against a white void, with a vertical "Hatton Labs © 2021 / TM & © 2021 Playboy" credit along the right edge.

**Colour.** Measured: white void #F1F3F0, black #050608, pale TV glow (estimated ~#C8D8DC). The palette is black and white with almost nothing between: the black jacket, black chair canvas and black TV cases against the white void, the two TV screens as the only pale-blue-grey fields. Colour is absent by design — the image is a monochrome study with two glowing rectangles.

**Composition.** The figure sits left of centre, back to camera, at the lower third; the TV stack sits right of centre at the same height — a two-point balance across a wide empty frame. The upper two-thirds are blank white. The vertical credit line on the right edge is the image's only vertical text and it frames the void like a margin note. The composition's device is the back view: the viewer watches someone watching.

**Light & form.** Flat, even studio light with a soft floor shadow under the chair — the chrome chair frame catches the only highlights. Forms are rectilinear: the chair's tubular steel rectangle, the two TV boxes, the white plinth. The TV screens' glow is the only light source inside the scene, and it is cool against the warm-white void.

**Typography.** Two pieces of screen type: a bunny logo on the upper TV and "HATTON LABS" in a plain grotesque on the lower TV — both are content on a screen inside the photograph, not typography of the image. The vertical edge credit ("Hatton Labs © 2021 / TM & © 2021 Playboy. All rights reserved") is small print running down the right margin.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is the sparsest in the batch: a chair, two TVs, a plinth, a void. The CRT stack is the anachronism that makes the image — obsolete hardware as a set piece, glowing in an otherwise empty room. The reference value is that sparseness: two objects and a back view carry the whole frame.

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the back-view device: a figure seen from behind turns the viewer into a watcher of a watcher, and the two glowing screens become the image's focal point without any face in frame.

**Contrast.** Black #050608 on white #F1F3F0 ≈ 19:1 (computed from sampled colours) — the highest-contrast image in the batch, and the reason it reads at thumbnail size. The TV screens are mid-value against the void, so they read as grey rectangles until the viewer notices the logo and the wordmark.
