# REF-0063 — Logistics platform hero — serif headline over blue line-art skyline

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels, WCAG ratio computed from sampled colours); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a single hero screen for a logistics platform — centred serif headline, two buttons, and a blue line-art skyline filling the lower half. No other sections are shown.

**Colour.** Measured: page field #F4F4F4, headline near-black, and the skyline drawn in thin blue lines sampled around #A2A4FF (pale lavender-blue). Single accent — that blue — used only for the line art; the only other colour is the black of the primary button. The blue is decorative, not semantic: it draws the skyline and nothing else.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "Move cargo with complete visibility." in a transitional serif at about 32–36 px, centred, with generous leading; supporting copy at about 12–13 px grey; nav in a small monospace at about 10–11 px tracked caps. Two-tier scale with a serif display face — the only serif headline in this batch — and the mono nav is the page's second voice, technical against the serif's editorial tone.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, fully centred and symmetric: nav row, headline, one line of copy, two buttons, then the skyline occupying the entire lower half. Density is sparse — most of the page is empty grey — and the hierarchy is by size and position alone. The symmetry is what makes the RTL read clean: nothing is anchored left or right.

**Shape & surface.** Sharp corners everywhere — no radii on the buttons or the page frame — and flat surfaces with no shadow, border or depth. The only texture is the line-art skyline itself: hundreds of horizontal blue strokes of varying length forming a stepped city silhouette, denser at the edges, open in the middle under the headline.

**Buttons & components.** Two buttons, side by side, equal size: solid black "BOOK A DEMO" and white outline "SEE HOW IT WORKS". The black one is primary by fill alone — same size, same position, no colour help. The nav's "LOG IN" is a small black pill at the far right. Controls are quiet relative to the headline, which is the page.

**Gradient & imagery.** No photography, no gradient — the skyline is abstract line art, and it is the whole visual idea. Removing it would leave a centred headline on grey: still readable, but the page would lose the only thing that says "logistics / skyline / scale". Soft dependency — the layout survives, the identity does not.

**Contrast.** Measured: the blue line art #A2A4FF on #F4F4F4 is 2.06:1 — it fails as text contrast, but it is decorative line art, not text, and is not asked to be read. Black headline on #F4F4F4 is about 17:1 — passes. The pixelated green border framing the capture is a screenshot artefact, not part of the design, and is excluded from this read.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the serif-headline-over-line-art-skyline is a distinctive, low-cost hero mechanism — type plus one drawn motif, no photography — but a single hero with no other sections visible, so adapt, not adopt.
