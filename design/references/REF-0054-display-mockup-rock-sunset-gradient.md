# REF-0054 — Studio display on a rock against a teal-to-orange gradient sky, abstract wallpaper

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Background is a vertical duotone gradient: grey-teal #b5c8c4 at the top, through dusty rose #c4a392 and #cc8662, to orange #d16c1e with a hotter #df540f corner bottom-right. The screen is near-black #0e0402 with a gold light streak #e0c785; the rock is dark umber #240e01.

**Typography.** None.

**Layout & harmony.** Monitor centred slightly above middle on a rough rock plinth; the horizon of the gradient sits behind the screen's lower edge.

**Shape & surface.** All rendered: rock texture, aluminium stand, glossy screen.

**Buttons & components.** None.

**Gradient & imagery.** Entirely gradient and render; the screen's warm streak picks up the orange of the sky.

**Contrast.** No text to measure.

## Why the verdict

No UI — a device-presentation/mood image. Useful only as a staging and colour reference (gradient sky whose warm end matches the screen content).
