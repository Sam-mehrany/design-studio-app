# REF-0070 — Training timer + weekly plan — two-phone marketing shot, lime on black

## Read

Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); type sizes estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a marketing shot of two phones on a light-grey studio background — left, an exercise timer over a full-bleed workout photo; right, a "TRAINING" week view with continue-training cards and an upcoming-workout card.

**Colour.** Measured: phone black #101010, card surface #2C2C2C, light studio background #C2C3BE, lime accent (estimated ~#D9F24F). Single accent: lime marks the active day (Wed 21), the pause button, the "Continue now" arrows and the progress values (65%, 13%). The upcoming-workout card is a pale lime field, so the accent also carries the "next" state.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "TRAINING" ~20 px all-caps bold, timer "00:35" ~36–40 px tabular, percentages "65%"/"13%" ~22 px bold, body ~13–14 px. Two-tier scale with a display-numeral hero on the timer phone. Numerals are tabular in the timer and the day strip.

**Layout & harmony.** Two phones side by side on a grey void; inside each phone a single column. Left phone: photo fills the top ~70%, timer and progress sit in a black panel below. Right phone: day strip, two continue cards, one wide upcoming card, tab bar. Hierarchy is size (timer, percentages) then enclosure (cards). Density moderate.

**Shape & surface.** Radius scale estimated: cards ~16–20 px, pills fully rounded, phone frames ~48 px. Separation is tonal (card #2C2C2C on black #101010) with no shadow. The pale-lime upcoming card is the only light surface in either phone.

**Buttons & components.** The lime square pause button is the loudest control on the left phone; "Continue now" pills with lime arrow discs are the loudest on the right. The tab bar uses a white pill for the active "Trainings" item — a second loudness system (white = active, lime = action) that slightly competes with the accent rule.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Photography is load-bearing: the left phone is a full-bleed squat photo with the timer overlaid on its black panel, and the right phone's cards each carry a small exercise photo. Delete the photography and the left phone is a bare timer; imagery dependency is hard.

**Contrast.** Computed from sampled colours: white on black #101010 ≈ 18.9:1 — passes. Lime ~#D9F24F on black ≈ 15:1 — passes. Grey secondary text (estimated ~#8A8A8A on #101010 ≈ 5.5:1) — passes, estimated not measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the timer-over-photo plus week-strip home is a strong glanceable mechanism and the lime accent discipline is strict, but it is a marketing shot (two staged phones, no status-bar reality), photography is load-bearing, and the day strip reads left-to-right so RTL is risky — adapt, not adopt.
