# REF-0026 — Cyberhats — "Next-Gen Hats" black e-commerce hero on laptop (slide 1 of 3)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the dark staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a streetwear e-commerce hero on a laptop on a stone block — black field, white condensed "NEXT-GEN HATS." with a cyan hand-drawn ring around "HATS", two model photographs right, outline pill CTAs.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: black field (about #050505), white type, one electric cyan accent (about #00B4E6) used only for the hand-drawn ring and the active nav item "Hats", deep blue (about #0B3D91) in the model photographs. Single accent: cyan marks the one word and the one active link, nothing else.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "NEXT-GEN HATS." in a condensed grotesque at about 40–44 px extra-bold, body at about 11–12 px, nav at about 10–11 px. Two-tier scale with high weight contrast — the headline is roughly 4× the body and the only heavy weight on screen.

**Layout & harmony.** Split grid, estimated by eye: headline and copy left (~40%), two portrait photographs right (~60%) with a round arrow button at the seam, nav top-right, logo top-left. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by the cyan ring, which is the only colour on the black field. Density is moderate — one headline, one paragraph, two photos, two CTAs.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: photographs at about 12–16 px radius, pill CTAs at full pill radius, the rest sharp. Separation is by tone — photographs against black — with no border and no shadow. The hand-drawn cyan ellipse is the only organic shape on a rectilinear page.

**Buttons & components.** Two outline pill CTAs: "Shop now" top-right in the nav and "DISCOVER" under the copy, plus a round outline arrow button at the photo seam. The CTAs are quiet relative to the headline — outline pills on black, not filled — so the headline does the selling and the buttons only catch the click.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Two model photographs (bucket hats, metallic jackets) fill the right half and carry the product evidence. Delete them and the page is a black field with a headline and no product — so the dependency is hard.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white on black is about 19:1 — passes. The cyan ring on black, estimated at about #00B4E6 (about 6:1 estimated — passes for a graphic, estimated, not measured). The small grey body copy, estimated at about #B0B0B0 on black (about 8:1 estimated — passes, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the black hero with a condensed headline, a hand-drawn accent ring and a photo pair is a transferable streetwear-landing mechanism at medium cost, but it is a staged concept mockup and the composition collapses without its photographs (imagery_dep hard), so adapt, not adopt.
