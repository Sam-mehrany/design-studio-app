# REF-0048 — Studio display mockup on desk, white Swiss hairline grid with wide caps

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Environment: grey wall #c8c8c8, black desk #090909, black bezel #141313. Screen is pure #ffffff with black type and black hairlines — strictly monochrome; no accent.

**Typography.** Wide neo-grotesque all-caps display ("DARK MODE AESTHETICS", cap height ≈37 preview px) and stat numerals ("17", "3680X2760") at almost the same size (≈36) — a display tier and a tiny body tier with nothing between. One weight.

**Layout & harmony.** Screen is a 2×2 modular grid divided by hairline seams, labels top-left of each cell, numerals bottom. The scene adds dim silhouetted figures and plants for scale.

**Shape & surface.** Sharp corners; hairline rules do all the separating — no fills, no shadows.

**Buttons & components.** A single black rectangular button under the headline (white text 19.4:1).

**Gradient & imagery.** No imagery on screen. The environment render carries the mood; the screen design survives without it.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: headline black on #ffffff 21:1, body #2f2f2f 13.4:1, button white on black 19.4:1.

## Why the verdict

The on-screen grid is low-cost, high-contrast type-and-hairline design worth adapting; the staged render is a presentation cost. RTL risky only because of Latin numerals and the label-then-number reading order per cell.
