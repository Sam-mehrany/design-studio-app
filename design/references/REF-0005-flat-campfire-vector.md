# REF-0005 — Flat-vector campfire on maroon (Freepik stock)

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — maroon field #3b1113 / #3a1010 (85%); flame in a warm analogous stack from #f8ec42 yellow through #f3af2e to #8d4717, logs #57220c. Translucent darker flame silhouettes behind the front flame create a second plane in the same hue.

**Typography** — only the stock credit, "designed by freepik.com", small sans in orange.

**Layout & harmony** — centred object, large empty field; one subject per frame.

**Shape & surface** — flat fills with no outlines, depth faked by overlapping tints of the ground colour.

**Gradient & imagery** — no gradients; layered flat shapes do the work.

**Contrast** — flame #f3af2e on maroon 8.61:1; credit text #ecb16e on #3b1112 = 8.69:1.

## Why the verdict

Stock illustration; the transferable idea is depth made from tints of the ground colour rather than shadow. Finish is generic.
