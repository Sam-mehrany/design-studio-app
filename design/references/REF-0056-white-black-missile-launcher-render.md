# REF-0056 — White-and-black tracked missile launcher — 3D render in white hangar

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: a 3D render of a tracked missile-launcher vehicle — white angular hull, black triple launch tubes — staged in a white hangar. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: white hull (#E5E5E5 dominant field, hull sampled around #CFCFCF) against black launch tubes, black roof deck and black tracks. Strictly neutral, no hue. The black is concentrated in the top third — the launcher assembly — so the image reads as a white base with a black crown, the reverse of most vehicles on the board where the dark mass sits low.

**Composition & form.** Three-quarter front view, vehicle centred, the three hexagonal launch tubes pointing right and slightly up as the dominant diagonal. The hull is sharply faceted — flat white plates meeting at hard edges, with a black chevron stripe across the front deck. Seven road wheels repeat along the bottom. The launcher's geometry (three identical tubes) gives the image its rhythm: repetition of one cylinder, three times.

**Light & surface.** Soft hangar light, glossy floor reflection, white plates carrying gentle grey shading that models the facets. Surfaces are polished and hard-edged; the black tubes are matte with visible ring seams at their mouths. A white support strut angles down from the launcher to the hull — the one diagonal brace in the composition.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: black launcher and tracks against the white hull and white hangar field are high separation, and the white hull against the white field holds only by its grey shading — the hull's lower edges are soft where white meets white. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the black-crown/white-base inversion and the triple-tube repetition are the transferable lessons — a clean two-value composition with one repeated module — but a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
