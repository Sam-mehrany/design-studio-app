# REF-0053 — Tan-and-black tracked anti-aircraft vehicle with radar panel — 3D render in white hangar

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: a 3D render of a tracked vehicle with twin cannons and a raised radar panel, staged in a white hangar — an imagery / mood reference for game assets, not a UI screen. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: tan armour sampled around #8A857F–#D0B999 (weathered tan with grey drift) against black turret, tracks and grille insets, on a white hangar field (#ECECEA dominant). Two hues, muted saturation — the tan is dusty, not vivid — so the vehicle reads utilitarian rather than showy. The black is used for the working parts: guns, tracks, vents.

**Composition & form.** Three-quarter front view, vehicle centred, the twin cannon barrels pointing left and the flat radar panel rising above the turret as the tallest element — a clear vertical accent against the long horizontal hull. Six road wheels repeat along the bottom, and the side is broken by a large black grille block. The radar panel's flat tan face is the single largest plain surface in the image.

**Light & surface.** Soft hangar light, gentle floor reflection, weathering streaks and scuffs painted onto the tan plates — the only render on the board that leans on wear as a finish. Surfaces are matte-to-satin rather than glossy; the black areas are near-matte.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: black turret and tracks against the white hangar field are high separation; tan hull against white is moderate — the tan edges soften into the background where the light hits them. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the weathering and the radar-panel vertical accent are the transferable lessons — muted two-tone with wear as finish — but a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
