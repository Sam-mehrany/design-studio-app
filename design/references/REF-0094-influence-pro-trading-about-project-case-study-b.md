# REF-0094 — Influence Pro Trading — 'About Project' case-study board (orange container hero), Design Studio gallery capture

## Read

**Colour.** A case-study board for a corporate trading and logistics website. Measured off this screenshot: board field mode #1B1C1F, a near-black charcoal; single accent is the container orange, sampled #BF6536–#C8724E, appearing only in the photograph; the giant display title is mid-grey, sampled #949494, and the small body copy grey #6A6B6E. One hue carries all meaning; everything else is value steps on charcoal.

**Typography.** One condensed grotesque throughout, all caps. Measured in this screenshot, the 'ABOUT PROJECT' glyphs span about 160 px vertically; GOAL/RESULT labels about 20 px and body about 10–11 px, estimated by eye — a display-to-body ratio of roughly 6:1, a dramatic scale with heavy display weight against light body. No numerals are visible on this board.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column: GOAL text left, RESULT text right, the hanging container centred between them, the giant title spanning the full width below. Hierarchy is ranked by size first, then by the photograph. Density is moderate — one title and two short text blocks on the board. Spacing is rhythmic: the two text columns align to the same top edge and the same narrow measure.

**Shape & surface.** Sharp corners throughout; no radius anywhere on the board. Separation is by tone — charcoal field against the orange photograph — with a faint hairline grid behind the text columns. No cards, no shadows, no elevation; the surface is flat.

**Buttons & components.** No buttons are visible on this board — the giant title is the navigation, and the only marks besides type and photograph are the laurel emblems flanking GOAL and RESULT, which are decoration, not controls. Nothing on the board reads as tappable.

**Gradients & imagery.** No gradient. One photograph — the orange container hanging by crane chains — carries the board's entire visual weight; the faint grid behind it is the only drawn element. Delete the photograph and the board is giant grey type and two small text columns on charcoal — the composition survives as a poster, the subject is gone.

**Contrast.** Measured: grey title #949494 on #1B1C1F = 5.62:1, passing the 3:1 large-text floor; white on the board = 17.04:1. Failures, measured: body grey #6A6B6E on #1B1C1F = 3.20:1, below the 4.5:1 body floor; white stencilled labels on the container orange #BF6536 = 4.09:1, also below 4.5:1.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the Goal/Result columns around a centred hero photograph plus a giant display title is a transferable, low-cost portfolio mechanism, but the board collapses without its photograph (imagery_dep hard) and it is a concept presentation shot, so adapt, not adopt.
