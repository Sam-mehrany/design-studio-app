# REF-0060 — '05b logic of evolution' — annotated white 3D organism render (Synesthesia)

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — white ground `#fefefe`; the render lives in cool greys `#c1c6c3`–`#869093`. Neutral-only, no accent at all.

**Typography** — small serif italic headings ("/room_1", "chamber formation") with roman serif body notes; vertical serif title "05b logic of evolution" up the right edge.

**Layout & harmony** — one central object, leader lines out to annotation blocks on both sides, circular magnifier insets, a vertical ruler down the left edge. Dense but ordered by the leader lines.

**Shape & surface** — hairline leaders and ruler ticks; circles for detail insets; the render supplies all the depth.

**Gradient & imagery** — a large 3D render; without it the annotation system has nothing to point at.

**Contrast** — measured at the pin's 630 px width, where small type is blurred, so these are floors: annotation text `#787878` on white 4.38:1 (fails AA 4.5 for small text at this resolution); vertical title `#6a6a6a` 5.37:1; ruler ticks `#bababa` 1.93:1.

## Why the verdict

The callout system (leader lines, magnifier circles, ruler edge) transfers to annotated product or anatomy views; the render is expensive and not ours.

## Notes

Signed SYNESTHESIA (art print). Annotation ratio may be higher at original resolution.
