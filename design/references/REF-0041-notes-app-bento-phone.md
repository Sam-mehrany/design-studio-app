# REF-0041 — Notes app — "Your notes" bento home on iPhone (dot-matrix display type)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the lavender staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a notes app's home on an iPhone held in a hand — "Your notes" title, pill filters ("All notes", "Important", "To-do"), a bento grid of note cards with dot-matrix display type ("NEW PLAN", "DESIGN SPRINT LECT…"), a "+" / microphone FAB.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: a grey-green gradient screen (about #B8B8A8 to about #9AA88A) with four cards — dark plum ("NEW PLAN", about #2E2233), a photo card ("Image Notes"), a grey-green files card, and a pastel gradient card (cyan to peach to lavender). Polychrome but muted: the cards are tints and pastels, and the dot-matrix type is the unifying device, not a hue.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "Your notes" in a grotesque at about 30–34 px, card titles in a dot-matrix display face at about 22–26 px ("NEW PLAN", "DESIGN SPRINT LECT…"), list items at about 13–14 px, filter pills at about 13–14 px. Two-tier scale with two faces — grotesque for UI, dot-matrix for card titles — and the display face is the app's signature.

**Layout & harmony.** Bento grid, estimated by eye: title and filters top, then a 2-column bento (tall "NEW PLAN" checklist card left, "Image Notes" photo card and "Your files" card right), a wide gradient card at the bottom, the FAB floating over its top edge. Hierarchy is ranked by size (title first), then by enclosure (each card is a container). Density is moderate — five cards and a FAB on one screen.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: cards at about 20–24 px radius, filter pills at full pill radius, the FAB as a split pill (white "+" half, dark microphone half). Separation is by tone and by frosted-glass blur — the cards sit on a blurred gradient, and the "Image Notes" card is itself a blurred photograph. No borders, no hard shadows.

**Buttons & components.** The components are the filter pills (with a lavender "8" count badge on "All notes"), the checklist rows (one checked with a lavender tick, two unchecked circles), the file icons (PDF, document) and the split FAB. The FAB is the loudest control — a white circle that breaks the bottom card's edge — while the checklist is the loudest content.

**Gradient & imagery.** A pastel mesh gradient backs the bottom card and the screen itself grades grey-green; one blurred portrait photograph fills the "Image Notes" card. Delete the photo and the grid still reads — four cards and the FAB survive — so the dependency is soft. The gradients are the cost: live blur over a mesh gradient is medium cost on a midrange phone.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white "Your notes" on grey-green is about 3:1 estimated — fails 4.5:1 (estimated, not measured). White dot-matrix type on the dark plum card is about 12:1 estimated — passes. The "2h ago" micro-labels over the photo card, estimated at about #D8D8D8 on a mid-tone photo (about 2.5:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the bento note grid with a dot-matrix display face and a split FAB is a transferable browse mechanism at medium cost, but it is a staged concept mockup, the title fails contrast on its gradient, and the mesh gradients sit on the active brief's reject line (C.MAT.gradient-mesh), so adapt, not adopt.
