# REF-0091 — Laptop on rough stone slab — black brand page with iridescent light arcs and giant lowercase wordmark (sottozero)

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Grey studio gradient (`#626365` top to `#d3d5d4` mid), black stone `#0b0b07`, floor `#6c6b68`. Screen `#010101` with iridescent arcs — warm `#edab6b` through rust `#a35421` to blue/violet. The only colour in the frame is light on the screen.

**Typography.** Giant white lowercase geometric wordmark "sottozero®" bottom-left (26px glyph run, measured) against 4-column micro labels at ~2–3px x-height — a two-tier scale of roughly 8–10×, nothing in between.

**Layout & harmony.** Wordmark anchored bottom-left, micro labels in a top row of four columns, arcs sweeping through the middle. Sparse; size alone ranks.

**Shape & surface.** Matte black laptop on a rough, flat stone — texture contrast between device and plinth is the staging idea.

**Buttons & components.** None.

**Gradient & imagery.** The light arcs are the screen (hard). Live-rendered they would be costly.

**Contrast.** Wordmark white on black = 20.9:1 where clear; where arcs pass behind the letters the local ground rises to `#6b6565` (5.68:1). Micro labels `#9b9b9d` on black = 7.47:1 but only ~3px tall.

## Why the verdict

A mockup product's own promo; the wordmark-plus-micro-labels page is a brand gesture, not a UI. Left-anchored hero → rtl breaks. Adapt, low.
