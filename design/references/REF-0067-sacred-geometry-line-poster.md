# REF-0067 — Sacred-geometry line diagram on warm near-black

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — warm near-black `#24211d`/`#1d1916`; line colour cream peaking at `#fbf7ed`. One-colour drawing.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — strict vertical and horizontal symmetry: nested circles, diamonds and triangles on a shared centre; construction lines visible.

**Shape & surface** — hairline vector strokes, uniform weight.

**Gradient & imagery** — vector geometry only; cheap to reproduce.

**Contrast** — line peak `#fbf7ed` on `#24211d` 14.98:1 (thin strokes read lower in practice).

## Why the verdict

Hairline construction geometry in cream on warm black is a cheap, mirror-safe decorative layer; it is a decoration, not a layout.
