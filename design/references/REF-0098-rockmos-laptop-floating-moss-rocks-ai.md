# REF-0098 — Floating laptop between moss-covered rocks — "Mac Book" heavy headline with motion blur, moss photo on screen (AI mockup product)

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Neutral grey void `#c7c7c7`, pale page `#ddded8`, dark olive type `#1b2615`, moss greens `#7b9011`/`#53640c`, white rock `#d6d2cc`. One hue (moss) shared by props, screen photo and type.

**Typography.** Heavy geometric sans, title case, two tight lines "Mac / Book" — 55px glyph run (measured) on a ~300px screen; the second line is motion-blurred. Tiny bracketed list `[i] [ii] [iii]`. Dramatic two-tier scale.

**Layout & harmony.** Headline top-left, moss mound photo bottom-right overlapping it, list in between, pill tag top-right. Asymmetric.

**Shape & surface.** Device cut out and floating at an angle between two cut-out rocks; no ground plane.

**Buttons & components.** One dark olive pill tag "RockMos".

**Gradient & imagery.** Moss photo on screen and the moss props are the whole idea (hard).

**Contrast.** Headline `#1b2615` on `#dadcd4` = 11.3:1 (8.6:1 on the blurred line); list `#84857f` = 2.75:1 — fails.

## Why the verdict

Props that repeat the screen's subject (like REF-0061) is the transferable idea; this is an AI-generated mockup product and the blurred headline is a gimmick. Adapt, low.
