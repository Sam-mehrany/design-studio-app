# REF-0096 — Low-angle laptop in a dark room with window light — sepia leaf-photo slide, upright + italic serif headline (Cyrillic)

## Nature

Low-angle laptop in a dark room with window light; rendered.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Screen monochrome brown: ground `#301b08`–`#341f0d` (a darkened leaf photo), cream headline `#decbb9`, muted body `#877465`. Scene near-black `#000000` with hard window-light shafts `#e6e1dc` and a pale curtain `#f1ece7` — the room repeats the screen's warm value range.

**Typography.** Centred serif headline mixing upright and italic within the line ("Почему *название* — *больше*, чем просто *слово?*"), 11px glyph run (measured); small sans body. Serif display + sans text.

**Layout & harmony.** Centred single column with outlined pill tags in the corners ("naming", "graphic design", "@anasa_design") and small circular icon buttons — reads as a carousel/social slide more than a site.

**Shape & surface.** Pill outlines, hairline strokes, flat.

**Buttons & components.** Outline pills and circle icons only.

**Gradient & imagery.** Darkened leaf photo as ground — atmosphere (soft).

**Contrast.** Headline `#decbb9` on `#341f0d` = 9.90:1; body `#877465` = 3.55:1 — fails; pill tags 3.02:1 — fails.

## Why the verdict

The upright/italic serif mix in one line and the tonal match between room and screen transfer; body text fails. Adapt, med.
