# REF-0041 — Japanese kana pattern poster: dot grids, waves, speech bubbles

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Black on white only: white `#ffffff` ≈60% of area; dot fields resolve to `#adadad` at a distance. No hue.

**Typography.** Hiragana (gojūon row-heads: あかさたな / はまやら / わ を ん) in a single-weight gothic, widely spaced, set both horizontally and vertically — the characters are used as pattern elements as much as text.

**Layout & harmony.** Modular blocks of square dot grids at three dot sizes (large field, medium, small cluster) set against stacked wave-line motifs, hatched speech-bubble circles, a halftone moon and one heavy black zigzag; scattered but locked to an implied grid.

**Shape & surface.** Mix of hairline waves, solid dots, one heavy zigzag stroke — weight contrast comes from stroke, not fill.

**Gradient & imagery.** Fully vector except the halftone moon; cheap.

**Contrast.** Kana stroke cores `#000000` on `#ffffff` = 21:1; thin-stroke rendering averages `#4c4847` = 9.03:1.

## Why the verdict

Vector, monochrome, no photo: the dot-grid density blocks and wave motifs are a reusable pattern language for empty states or section dividers; the vertical/horizontal kana mix makes it script-specific and RTL-risky as a layout.

## Notes

Ties to the Japanese-motif cluster (REF-0038, REF-0043). Script is Japanese; any adaptation for RTL Persian would need its own native-authored arrangement.
