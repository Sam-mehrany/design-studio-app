# REF-0089 — Financia — Dark landing page with embedded finance dashboard hero

## Read

**Colour.** Values sampled from the image file. The field is near-black #010101, the dashboard panel #060606 and its cards #121214 — a three-step tonal ladder with no shadow doing the depth work. White #FFFFFF type on #010101 measures 21:1; the grey second headline line #808080 measures about 5.3:1. Green is the single accent: dark green #235424 in the backdrop strip and green +8% badges on dark pill grounds, with red reserved for the single −8% badge.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque sans throughout, one family, hierarchy by size and weight only. Hero headline cap height measured about 34 px (roughly 48 px size) against body text of about 13–14 px, a dramatic scale near 3.5:1 hero-to-body. Display numerals are the second voice: $19,270.56 and $50,734 set large, with thousands separators and fixed two-decimal precision. Sentence case everywhere; whether the figures are tabular cannot be determined from this capture.

**Layout & harmony.** Single-column landing: nav, left-aligned hero, full-width dashboard panel, centred partner strip, second left-aligned headline. Generous space around the two headlines; hierarchy comes from size and from white-versus-grey type, not from containers — the dashboard panel is the only enclosure. Radii are one family (pill controls, medium card corners) and the icons are a single thin line set.

**Shape & surface.** Medium card radii inside the dashboard, pill radii on the Get Started button and the All / Cash / Check filter row — a mixed radius scale split by component class. Depth is tonal only: #010101 field, #060606 panel, #121214 cards, with hairline separation and no drop shadow visible. The dashboard and its green-orange backdrop bleed to the page edges.

**Buttons & components.** One loud white pill, “Get Started”, is the only filled primary action; Log in is a ghost outline and Sign Up a small white pill in the nav. Inside the dashboard, the filter row is quiet outline pills with “All” filled white, and the tab bar is text with small line icons. The components stay quieter than the figures they frame.

**Gradients & imagery.** No gradient wash; the visual material is the dashboard screenshot itself, framed by a flat green-orange abstract strip (dark green #235424 with lime and orange bands) that reads as decoration. Inside the dashboard, a white bar sparkline and a dot-matrix chart carry the visual interest as data, not photography. Removing the backdrop strip would not break the page; removing the dashboard screenshot would leave the hero unproven — imagery dependence is soft.

**Contrast.** Measured: white #FFFFFF on #010101 is 21:1; grey #808080 on #010101 is about 5.3:1, passing 4.5:1 for the large second headline line. The small grey nav labels and micro-labels are mid-grey on near-black; their exact values cannot be determined from this capture, and the smallest labels sit closest to the floor. Neither measured pair falls below 4.5:1.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the dark landing structure — oversized display headline, dashboard-as-hero, partner strip — is transferable, but the repeated identical figures ($19,270.56 three times) mark it as a concept mock rather than a shipped product, and the decorative backdrop adds raster weight without carrying meaning.
