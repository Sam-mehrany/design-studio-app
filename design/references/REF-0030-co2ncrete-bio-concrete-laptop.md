# REF-0030 — CO2NCRETE — bio-concrete landing on laptop (red gradient, 3D blocks)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the dark staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a bio-concrete manufacturer's landing on a laptop among real concrete blocks — a black-to-red gradient field, a 3D render of two concrete blocks top, condensed caps headline bottom-left ("HIGHT QUALITY BIO CONCRETE MANUFACTRER", sic).

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: black top grading to red-orange (about #E8380D) at the bottom, white type, grey 3D blocks. Two hues — black and red-orange — with the gradient doing the work of a background photograph; no other accent.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline in a condensed grotesque all-caps at about 22–26 px, microcopy at about 8–9 px, nav at about 8–9 px. Two-tier scale with high weight contrast — the condensed caps are the only heavy weight, everything else is small and light.

**Layout & harmony.** Canvas layout, estimated by eye: the 3D blocks float top-centre, the headline sits bottom-left, a small text column and a white card sit bottom-right. Hierarchy is ranked by position (the blocks dominate by size and centrality), then by the headline's caps. Density is low — four elements on a gradient field.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: everything sharp-cornered, no radius anywhere. Separation is by the gradient's own light fall and by the blocks' cast shadows in the render — no borders, no UI shadows.

**Buttons & components.** There are no visible buttons; the only controls are the tiny top nav and a small white card bottom-right (illegible at this size). The loudest element is the 3D render, not any control.

**Gradient & imagery.** A black-to-red gradient backs the screen and a 3D render of two concrete blocks is the hero. Delete the render and the page is a headline on a gradient — the product evidence is gone — so the dependency is hard. The gradient alone is cheap; the render is the cost.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white on black is about 19:1 — passes. White on the red-orange gradient, estimated at about #E8380D (about 3.5:1 estimated — fails where the headline crosses the brightest red, estimated, not measured). The microcopy at 8–9 px is the most fragile text on screen.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the gradient field + floating 3D product render + condensed caps headline is a transferable industrial-landing mechanism at medium cost, but the composition collapses without its render (imagery_dep hard), the headline carries two typos ("HIGHT", "MANUFACTRER"), and it is a staged concept mockup, so adapt, not adopt.
