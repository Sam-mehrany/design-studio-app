# REF-0030 — Overdrawn audio waveform, hairline line art

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Monochrome on white `#ffffff` (53% of area); lines resolve to `#6a6a6a` where dense, `#b6b6b6` / `#e0e0e0` where sparse.

**Layout & harmony.** Many overlaid hairline traces of a waveform build density by overdraw — tone comes from line count, not fill.

**Shape & surface.** Single hairline stroke weight, no fills, no surfaces.

**Gradient & imagery.** Generative vector — cheap and scalable; it is the whole piece.

**Contrast.** No text. Densest line mass `#6a6a6a` on white = 5.41:1; sparse traces fall well under 3:1.

## Why the verdict

No photography, low cost; overdrawn hairlines as a data-texture motif (audio, activity history) is adaptable, but as a standalone piece it is decoration.

## Notes

Possible source for a decorative data motif behind a hero numeral; would need a real data series behind it to earn its place.
