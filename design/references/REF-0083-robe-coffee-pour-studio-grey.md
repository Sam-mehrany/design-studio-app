# REF-0083 — Robe and coffee pour — figure in grey robe, second arm pouring, grey void (slide 1 of 2)

## Read

This is a photography / mood reference, not a UI screen — no UI archetype applies. Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); proportions estimated by eye. One line of context: a fashion editorial photograph — a figure in a long grey robe over checked pyjamas and a navy bucket hat, holding out a cup while a second person's arm enters from the left pouring coffee from a steel pot, against a seamless grey void. Slide 1 only of a 2-slide carousel (two dots and a next arrow are visible); slide 2 was not supplied.

**Colour.** Measured: grey void #BCB6B6–#DEDAD7, dark robe trims #515151, navy hat (estimated ~#1F2A5A). The palette is grey-on-grey: the robe, the void and the socks are all near-neutral, with the navy hat and the steel pot as the two darkest masses and the skin tones as the only warmth. Colour is descriptive, not semantic.

**Composition.** The figure is centred, full-length, facing the camera; the pouring arm enters from the left edge and breaks the frame — the image is built on that intrusion. The vertical figure (robe, hat, socks, sandals) is one long column; the horizontal arm and pot are the single counter-line. The carousel UI (dots bottom centre, next arrow right, profile icon bottom left) is part of the captured screenshot, not part of the photograph.

**Light & form.** Soft, even studio light with a faint floor shadow under the feet — the robe's long drape is the dominant form, a grey column with a dark piped edge running its full length. The checked pyjama shirt and shorts show through the open robe as a small-scale pattern against the robe's plain field — the only pattern contrast in the image.

**Typography.** No type in the photograph itself. The only text-like marks are the carousel dots and arrows from the app the screenshot was taken in; they are capture artefacts, not part of the image's design.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is minimal to the point of absence: a seamless grey void, no props, no furniture — the robe and the pouring gesture are the entire scene. The reference value is that absence: the image works because the void leaves nothing to compete with the single action (being handed coffee).

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the gesture mechanism: a second person present only as an arm, telling a story with one intruding element instead of a second figure — economy of cast.

**Contrast.** The dark navy hat and the robe's dark piping read against the light grey void (dark #515151 on #DEDAD7 ≈ 5.9:1, computed from sampled colours). The robe itself is close in value to the void, so the figure's outline depends on the piping, the hat and the checked shirt, not on the robe's mass.

Adapt (provisional, as a mood reference): the one-gesture-plus-void formula is directly stealable for campaign imagery, but this is a brand fashion photograph — and only slide 1 of a 2-slide carousel, so the set's intent is unproven — so it can never be adopt; adapt, as imagery direction only.
