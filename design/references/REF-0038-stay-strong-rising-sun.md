# REF-0038 — "Stay Strong" — red disc with wave on crumpled paper

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Crumpled paper ground `#ded9d1` / `#dad6cc`, one accent red `#a8262b` disc with a white brush-wave cut into it. Single accent, nothing else.

**Typography.** "STAY STRONG" in thin, widely tracked geometric-sans caps, small, centred below the disc; single weight.

**Layout & harmony.** Japanese-flag composition: one centred disc, a short caption, huge negative space.

**Shape & surface.** Raster paper texture (crumples, shadows) does the surface work; no edges or frames.

**Gradient & imagery.** Paper texture is a photographic raster; delete it and you have a flat red circle on beige — still stands (soft).

**Contrast.** Red disc `#a8262b` on paper `#ded9d1` = 5.02:1. Caption `#aeaaa1` on `#ded9d1` = 1.65:1 — **fails** badly; the text is effectively decorative.

## Why the verdict

Single-accent restraint is sound but generic; the caption fails contrast (1.65:1) and the paper texture is raster, so nothing here beats a flat equivalent.
