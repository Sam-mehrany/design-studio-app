# REF-0043 — Koi yin-yang with starfield core, line illustration

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Four values and one hue: warm grey ground `#a8a7a2` (≈49%), white koi `#e5e4e4`, near-black starfield `#1d1d1d`, red `#bd3f31` used only twice (sun disc and crescent moon on the heads).

**Layout & harmony.** Two koi rotated 180° into a circle around a black starfield core — rotational symmetry, so the composition has no reading direction.

**Shape & surface.** Fine black line work on flat fills; no shading beyond line density in fins.

**Gradient & imagery.** Flat illustration; cheap.

**Contrast.** White `#e5e4e4` on grey `#a8a7a2` = 1.90:1 (the koi read by line work, not fill); black core on grey = 7.00:1; red on white = 4.22:1.

## Why the verdict

The mechanism — one red used exactly twice, as the only hue, on a mid-grey field — is a clean single-accent lesson; the illustration itself is not transferable. Low confidence because the takeaway is narrow.
