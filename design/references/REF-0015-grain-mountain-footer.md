# REF-0015 — Footer over a grainy polychrome mountain gradient

## Nature

Footer on its own over a grain gradient.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Figures measured off the image with Python/PIL (median-sampled hex, WCAG 2.x ratios, ink heights in original-image pixels unless stated); type-scale ratios are derived from those ink heights; radii in px are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Polychrome ground: pale blue `#edf7fb` and blush `#f9ebea` at the top fading into lilac `#9f9ce7`, magenta `#fa7fd2`, coral `#fb7b7c` and olive, all heavily dithered. UI itself is black/charcoal only.

**Typography.** Column heads cap 19px vs links 17px (orig px) ≈1.1× — hierarchy by weight, not size. Logo wordmark ~2×. Neo-grotesque, Title Case heads.

**Layout & harmony.** Brand blurb + social tiles left, four link columns right, hairline rule (`#e8d5d1`), legal row. Moderate density, columnar.

**Shape & surface.** Social icon tiles medium radius (~8px) with hairline border; otherwise bare — no cards.

**Buttons & components.** Only the social tiles (`#393738` glyphs).

**Gradient & imagery.** Full-bleed pixel-grain mountain gradient occupies the lower half. Decoration only; the footer reads fine without it.

**Contrast.** Links `#36373c` on blush = 10.2:1; legal on pink 8.4:1. All text sits on the pale top band, so the gradient never undermines legibility.

## Why the verdict

Concept template. Keeps type on the calm part of the gradient — the right call; adapt.
