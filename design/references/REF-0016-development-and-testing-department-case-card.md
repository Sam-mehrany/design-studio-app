# REF-0016 — Development and Testing Department — case card 01

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values and WCAG ratios computed from sampled colours); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a case-study teaser card — an orange silk-wave header with a "Behind the scenes:" label and a notched "case 01" tab, over a white panel with a three-line navy headline — staged on black.

**Colour.** Measured: orange header #DC3F20, white panel #FFFEFF, black field #1A1A1A (from the image palette). One accent hue (orange-red) for the header only; the panel is white and the headline is navy-black. The orange carries identity, not status; there is no second hue.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "Development and Testing Department" at about 36–40 px extrabold across three lines, "case 01" numeral at about 24–28 px bold with "case" at about 13 px grey, "Behind the scenes:" label at about 12–13 px, subline at about 13–14 px grey. Headline-to-body multiple about 2.8× — a dramatic poster scale. The face is a grotesque sans; the "01" is a lining display numeral.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: header about 35% of the card height, headline block with about 28–32 px padding, subline pinned to the bottom. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by position (label, then numeral tab, then subline). Density is low — the white panel is mostly empty — and the notched tab where the header meets the panel is the one structural detail.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: card about 28–32 px, header flush to the top with the card's radius, the "case 01" tab cut as an angled notch into the header's bottom edge. Separation is flat and tonal: orange header against white panel, no border, no shadow. The silk-wave image inside the header is the only texture.

**Buttons & components.** There are no buttons; the components are the card, the header image, the label pill and the numeral tab. The loudest element is the headline itself — the card is a poster, and its type is the content. The "Behind the scenes:" label is the quietest element, set as small white text on the orange.

**Gradient & imagery.** The header is a photographic silk-wave abstraction in orange-red — an abstract shape, not a product photo. Removing it would leave a flat orange header; the card would still read, but the header would lose the movement that justifies its height, so the dependency is soft. No gradient on interface surfaces.

**Contrast.** Measured: white on orange #DC3F20 is 4.4:1 — just below 4.5:1, so the small white "Behind the scenes:" label on the orange header is a measured failure for its size. Black on white #FFFEFF is 18.77:1 — passes. The grey subline, estimated by eye at about #9A9A9A on white (roughly 2.9:1 estimated — fails 4.5:1, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the poster-headline case card is a clean, low-cost teaser mechanism (flat colour, one image, no blur), but it is a concept shot, its white-on-orange label fails contrast as measured, and the notched tab is left-anchored — so adapt, not adopt.
