# REF-0029 — Buenoley — "Tus abogados de confianza" law-firm landing on laptop

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the orange chair, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a Spanish law firm's landing on a laptop on an orange chair — a white left column with a serif headline and a WhatsApp CTA, a photograph of a lawyer right with floating chat bubbles.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: white left column (about #FDFDFB), black serif type, one blue accent (about #2E5BFF) in the CTA pill, one green accent (about #25D366) in the WhatsApp chip, a warm grey photograph right. Two functional accents: blue is the action, green is the channel — both semantic, neither decorative.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "Tus abogados de confianza" in a transitional serif at about 26–30 px, body at about 11–12 px, nav at about 9–10 px. Two-tier scale — the serif headline is the only display size — with a serif/sans pairing (serif headline, grotesque body and UI).

**Layout & harmony.** Split grid, estimated by eye: white content column left (~55%), photograph right (~45%) with three floating chat-bubble cards overlapping it, logo and social proof (Google rating, Instagram, Facebook) along the bottom. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by the blue CTA. Density is moderate — headline, body, CTA, rating row and three bubbles on one screen.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: pill CTA and pill chips (full pill radius), chat bubbles at about 12–16 px radius, the rest sharp. Separation is by tone — the white column against the photograph — and by the bubbles' soft shadows. No borders.

**Buttons & components.** The components are the blue filled pill CTA, the WhatsApp pill with a green dot and phone number, and the three floating chat bubbles ("Ley de Segunda Oportunidad" and two others, one in English). The blue CTA is the loudest control; the bubbles are content dressed as UI, which is the page's main trick.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. One photograph of a lawyer signing papers fills the right half; the chat bubbles float over it. Delete the photograph and the left column still reads — headline, CTA, rating — so the dependency is soft; the photo adds trust, not structure.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: black on white is about 17:1 — passes. White on the blue CTA, estimated at about #2E5BFF (about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline, estimated, not measured). The small grey body copy, estimated at about #777777 on white (about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the serif-headline + WhatsApp-CTA + floating-chat-bubble split is a transferable service-landing mechanism at low cost, but it is a staged concept mockup with mixed Spanish/English copy and no edge cases, so adapt, not adopt.
