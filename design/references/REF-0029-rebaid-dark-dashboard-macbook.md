# REF-0029 — Dark campaign dashboard with pastel cards on a MacBook

## Nature

MacBook in a dark rendered studio.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Black screen `#000000`, panel `#181c1c`; campaign cards cycle white `#fefefe`, peach `#f8e0d0`, salmon `#de9488`, dusky lilac `#5c526c` — colour per card, not per meaning. Chart bars grey `#464a50` with one peach/salmon highlight.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque, medium weights; in perspective, not measured for scale.

**Layout & harmony.** Sidebar + horizontally scrolling card row, avatar row, bar chart. Moderate density.

**Shape & surface.** Large radii on cards and avatar rings; flat colour cards on black.

**Buttons & components.** White circular chevrons; small pill toggles (24h/7d/All).

**Gradient & imagery.** Dark staged laptop render (environment `#464a54`) and avatar photos carry the shot.

**Contrast.** Titles `#f3f3f3` on `#181c1c` = 15.5:1; nav `#8f9297` on black = 6.7:1. Muted text on white card `#bababa` = 1.9:1 and axis `#515256` on panel = 2.2:1 — **fail**.

## Why the verdict

Mockup, concept; the pastel-per-card palette is loose and the secondary text fails.
