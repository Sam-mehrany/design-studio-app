# REF-0016 — Flat orange hexagon mosaic

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — one hue in six tones: #d06100 / #d66e01 / #e78603 / #f79613 / #fda31f / #feaf2a, up to #fec437. Cells are assigned tones at random.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — a regular hex tessellation; no focal point.

**Shape & surface** — flat fills, cells separated only by tone.

**Gradient & imagery** — none.

**Contrast** — darkest to lightest tone 2.44:1; nothing here could carry text.

## Why the verdict

A single-hue orange value ramp; useful as a pattern or ramp source only. Low resolution (273×338).
