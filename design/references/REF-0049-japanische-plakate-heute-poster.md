# REF-0049 — 'Japanische Plakate heute' exhibition poster — red disc and engraved goldfish

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — white ground `#fdfdfd`, one flat red `#d81c26` disc (hinomaru), and an engraved goldfish in the same red with olive-grey line work `#aeb9b7`. One accent; the red is the whole idea.

**Typography** — a single regular-weight sans (lineage *estimated* from a 451 px image: grotesque/geometric, monoline), sentence case. Title line box 30 px vs. 7 px for the date line: a two-tier scale, no middle size.

**Layout & harmony** — asymmetric: disc upper-centre, fish diagonal across the right, the type block anchored bottom-left with generous empty white above it. Hierarchy is by size of shape, not by type.

**Shape & surface** — one perfect circle against one highly detailed engraving: the contrast is flat-geometric vs. fine-line, not colour vs. colour.

**Gradient & imagery** — no gradients. Remove the fish and you still have a working Swiss poster (red disc + small type), so imagery is decoration.

**Contrast** — title `#01100b` on white 19.16:1; subtitle `#3b3d3c` 10.72:1; dates `#252927` 14.53:1; red disc vs. white 5.02:1.

## Why the verdict

The mechanism (one flat geometric accent, generous white, small type pinned to a corner) transfers to any light surface at no cost; the engraving is the non-transferable finish, hence `adapt`.

## Notes

Museum für Angewandte Kunst Frankfurt exhibition poster (text on the image). The lower-left type anchor would need to mirror for RTL, which is feasible but changes the reading diagonal.
