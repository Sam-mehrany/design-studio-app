# REF-0090 — Monitor-and-phone mockup — dark smart-park IoT decision dashboard, isometric 3D campus with alert callouts

## Nature

Monitor and phone together showing one product; rendered.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Dark-first: screen `#0d0e13`, panels `#21242a`. Teal is the system hue (`#4e847e` rings and charts, `#4e7b81` active tab); alerts carry status colour — amber `#f5d077`, red/pink `#df9fa0`, phone highlight `#f16e7a`. Room is blue-grey `#1a222c`→`#8095aa`.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque, small; KPI numerals larger in light grey (`#cbccd2`). Numerals likely tabular in the tables (*estimated*). Single family.

**Layout & harmony.** Command-centre layout: top tab nav, isometric 3D campus centre with floating callout cards, KPI columns left and right, a bottom row of rings, counts and a line chart. Dense; position ranks (centre = map, edges = metrics).

**Shape & surface.** Small radii, thin borders, flat panels on a dark ground.

**Buttons & components.** Tabs and callouts; very few buttons — a monitoring display, not an editor.

**Gradient & imagery.** The 3D campus is data (where an alert is), not decoration — the layout survives without it but loses its index (soft). Rendering it is high cost.

**Contrast.** KPI numerals `#cbccd2` on `#212129` = 9.97:1; panel labels 7.2:1; header 7.96:1; nav `#7a7c80` on `#0d0e13` = 4.61:1 — borderline pass.

## Why the verdict

Real award-entry product (4Paradigm UED, A' Design Award 2022); status-mapped colour on a single system hue is sound. Wall-display glance context, high render cost → adapt, med.
