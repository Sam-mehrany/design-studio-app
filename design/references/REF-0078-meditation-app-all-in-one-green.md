# REF-0078 — Meditation app — "All your meditations in one app!" + "My practical plan"

## Read

Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); type sizes estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a two-phone marketing shot on a dark backdrop — left, an onboarding cover ("All your meditations in one app!") over a green glow; right, a "My practical plan" browse screen with filter chips, course cards and audio rows.

**Colour.** Measured: near-black #232323, mid grey #444444, white #FFFFFF, pale lime (estimated ~#D8F79A, sampled green #95B761 nearby). One accent: pale lime fills the "Run" pill, the "Sleep Meditation" card and the play buttons. The background is a soft green radial glow (estimated dark olive → lime, ~#3A4A22 → #8AA85A) — a subtle gradient used as atmosphere, not as data. No semantic hues; the "Affirmation" chip is lime because it is selected, not because lime means affirmation.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "All your / meditations / in one app!" ~34–38 px light with the middle line in lime, "My practical / plan" ~32 px light, card titles ~16 px semibold, chips ~13 px. Dramatic scale with a light weight — the large light type against dark is the wellness register. No display numeral; the numbers are small ("15 min", "10 min", "11").

**Layout & harmony.** Two phones side by side. Left: centred lotus mark, headline lower third, two pills bottom ("Get trained" dark, "Run" lime). Right: "courses" label, headline, filter chips ("All 11", "Mantras", "Meditation", "Sleep"), two course cards, two audio rows with play discs, bottom nav. Hierarchy is size first, then the lime fill. Space is generous; density moderate.

**Shape & surface.** Radius scale estimated: cards ~20–24 px, pills fully rounded, chips ~16 px. Separation is tonal (card #444444 on field #232323) with no shadow. The green glow is the only depth cue — a tonal field behind the type, not a shadow or border.

**Buttons & components.** The lime "Run" pill is the loudest control on the left phone; the lime play discs lead the audio rows on the right. Filter chips are a segmented control with the active chip outlined in white and badged "11". The course cards pair a title with a checkbox list ("Affirmation" checked, "Meditation", "Mantras" unchecked) — a plan as a checklist.

**Gradient & imagery.** One subtle radial gradient (the green glow) on each phone, used as atmosphere; remove it and both screens still read as white-on-dark type, so it is decorative. No photography, no illustration beyond the small lotus mark.

**Contrast.** Computed from sampled colours: white on #232323 ≈ 15.9:1 — passes. Black on pale lime ~#D8F79A ≈ 15:1 — passes. Lime text ("meditations" in lime, estimated ~#D8F79A on #232323 ≈ 13:1) — passes. Grey secondary text (estimated ~#9A9A9A on #232323 ≈ 6:1) — passes, estimated not measured. The softest text is the small chip labels over the glow, which are not measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the onboarding-cover-plus-plan-browse pair is a coherent, low-cost wellness pattern, but it is a staged concept shot, the soft-wellness family sits on the active brief's reject line (A.FAM.soft-wellness), and the green glow is atmosphere rather than a transferable mechanism — adapt, not adopt.
