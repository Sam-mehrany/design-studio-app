## Read

**Colour.** Environment: black `#000000` falling to slate `#5c5f6e`; desk `#3b4567`. Screen: vertical gradient from near-white `#eceffc` to periwinkle `#7290f1`. Two hues total — navy and periwinkle — one family.

**Typography.** Thin geometric sans ALL-CAPS "MAC PRO TOUCH", centred, 14px glyph run (measured); four tiny corner labels. One weight.

**Layout & harmony.** Symmetric: laptop dead-centre on a black bracketed desk, title centred on screen, labels in the corners.

**Shape & surface.** Matte black hardware, machined brackets; no UI surfaces.

**Buttons & components.** None.

**Gradient & imagery.** The screen gradient is the content (hard); the scene is a render.

**Contrast.** Title `#1b2950` on the gradient mid `#b1c0f1` = 7.89:1 (12.4:1 at the top). Bottom-corner micro `#122d7d` on `#6986e3` = 3.61:1 — fails.
