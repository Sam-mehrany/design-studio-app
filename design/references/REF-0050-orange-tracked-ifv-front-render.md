# REF-0050 — Orange-dominant tracked infantry fighting vehicle, front three-quarter — 3D render in white hangar

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: a 3D render of a tracked armoured vehicle staged in a white hangar — an imagery / mood reference for game assets, not a UI screen. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: orange hull sampled at #DA7638, with black front glacis and track skirts against a white hangar field (#DADBDF dominant). Unlike REF-0049 the orange is the majority colour here, not the accent — the balance flips to roughly 60% orange, 40% black — so the vehicle reads loud and toy-like rather than tactical. Same two-hue system, different ratio.

**Composition & form.** Front three-quarter view, vehicle centred and low in the frame, seven orange road wheels in a row giving a strong horizontal rhythm along the bottom third. The turret sits small and high, so the hull — not the gun — carries the image. Panel seams and rivets are drawn on the flat orange plates, breaking the large colour fields into a grid.

**Light & surface.** Even, soft hangar light with a long floor reflection; the orange plates carry a slight gloss gradient from light to dark across the hull. Surfaces are polished hard panels with mesh grille insets on the flanks — the black mesh is the only texture break in the orange field.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: vivid orange #DA7638 against black track and glacis is high figure/ground separation, and the whole vehicle stands hard against the white hangar. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the orange-majority ratio is the useful lesson here — same two hues as REF-0049, a different balance, a different register — but it is a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
