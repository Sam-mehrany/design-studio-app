# REF-0014 — Print ad: Kraft peanut butter with honey — jar hanging in a tree like a hive

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — sky and foliage do it all: pale sky #e8ecc5 / #c9d8c0, olive greens #65652e / #a59c4b, dark leaf #312c12; no brand colour outside the product.

**Typography** — one line at the bottom right: humanist sans regular "Your favourite peanut butter, now with honey." plus a bold-italic tag "Spread something new."

**Layout & harmony** — the visual pun (jar as hive, bees around it) is the composition; copy is tucked into the lowest strip.

**Shape & surface** — photo composite, full bleed.

**Gradient & imagery** — everything depends on the photograph.

**Contrast** — tagline #f5fed0 measured 6.94:1 against the median foliage #515b29 but 2.06:1 against lighter leaf patches #a9b681 — legibility depends on where the photo lands.

## Why the verdict

Hard imagery dependency and text set over an uncontrolled photo ground; the idea is advertising craft, not a transferable design mechanism.
