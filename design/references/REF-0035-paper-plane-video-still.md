# REF-0035 — Origami paper plane, vertical video still

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Overcast sky `#eff4fa` / `#eaeef4`, foliage and lawn `#2f2813` / `#585143`, ochre paper plane `#c59b52`.

**Layout & harmony.** A landscape clip letterboxed into 9:16 with blurred, enlarged copies of itself filling top and bottom bands — the standard phone-video fill, not a design choice.

**Gradient & imagery.** Entirely photographic; the blur bands are live-video blur.

**Contrast.** White watermark "Tink leo" `#feffff` on sky `#ecf1f8` = 1.13:1 — illegible.

## Why the verdict

Craft video still; imagery_dep hard, nothing transferable.

Pinterest pin was a video. `kind` has no value for documentary/craft photography; `shipped` is used here to mean a real published photo, not a concept shot.
