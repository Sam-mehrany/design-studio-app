The speakers' circular drivers are the only circles in the frame, repeated twice, and they rhyme with the cap's curve.

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the wordmark-as-headline mechanism: one line of type on the figure does the work of a caption, a logo and a title at once — the same economy as a single display line on a screen.
