# REF-0085 — Floating laptop mockup on plum-to-pink gradient — split product page, dark text panel beside macro salt photo

## Nature

Floating laptop on a plum gradient; rendered.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Environment: near-black plum `#090714` falling to dusty pink `#c89598` at lower left. Screen: charcoal text panel `#2f2f2f` on the left, macro photo on the right (crystal `#9c4831`, pale pink ground `#a1847b` in shadow). Pink `#dfb2ae` button. Scene, photo and button share one hue — the product sets the palette.

**Typography.** Thin light-weight sans headline "Pure Composition" in off-white, small pink label, small grey body. Single weight family (*estimated*: hero ~3× body).

**Layout & harmony.** 50/50 split: text panel left, full-height product macro right; content bottom-aligned with a full-width pill button. Sparse; size ranks.

**Shape & surface.** Rounded full-width button (~6px, *estimated*); flat panel. Depth all in the staging — tilted floating laptop, dark rock in foreground.

**Buttons & components.** One full-width pink button carrying a full sentence ("Experience the difference…") — soft, product-led.

**Gradient & imagery.** The macro photograph is half the screen and the whole colour story (hard). Background gradient is staging.

**Contrast.** Headline `#eeeeee` on `#2f2f2f` = 11.5:1; body `#918785` on `#333231` = 3.66:1 — fails; button label `#2f211d` on pink = 6.87:1.

## Why the verdict

Saved for the colour-matched staging (scene gradient keyed to the product). Hard photo dependency, high-cost render → adapt.
