# REF-0027 — CRM concept on a laptop against a green backdrop

## Nature

Laptop tilted against a flat green backdrop, clean CG light → rendered mockup.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Backdrop is the accent: flat green `#40d86c`. Screen light grey `#dcdcdc` with cards `#eeeeee`; one black card `#000000` with a glowing green area chart (`#20b840` → `#20f868`). A dusty pink `#d8a8a8` gauge marks the negative value.

**Typography.** Geometric sans; heavy two-line H1 ('Organization Overview') against light-weight numerals. Perspective prevents a reliable scale measurement.

**Layout & harmony.** Top nav of text links + black pill, KPI row, then a light gauge card beside a black revenue card.

**Shape & surface.** Pills for nav and filters, large card radii. Laptop rendered in steep perspective, a concrete block `#626462` in the foreground.

**Buttons & components.** Black pill = active nav; circular arrow buttons.

**Gradient & imagery.** Device render, concrete prop and the glow chart are the image; strip them and the UI is ordinary.

**Contrast.** Labels `#323232` on `#dcdcdc` = 9.4:1; white on black card 21:1; micro-labels `#a7a7a7` on `#ececec` = 2.0:1 — **fail**.

## Why the verdict

Saved for the staged presentation (accent-matched backdrop), not a transferable UI; mockup, concept.
