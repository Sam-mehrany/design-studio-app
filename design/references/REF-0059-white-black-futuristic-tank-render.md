# REF-0059 — White-and-black futuristic battle tank, long white barrel — 3D render in white hangar

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: a 3D render of a white futuristic battle tank — long white barrel pointing right, black tracks and sensor pods — staged in a white hangar. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: white hull and turret on a white hangar field, with black tracks, black turret pods and a black barrel section as the dark masses. Strictly neutral, no hue — the lightest vehicle on the board, and the only one where the barrel itself is white. The white-on-white staging means the vehicle is read by its black parts: the tracks anchor the bottom, the turret pods punctuate the top.

**Composition & form.** Side profile, barrel pointing right, tank centred and low in the frame with a large glossy floor reflection doubling it. The turret is low and wide, almost merged with the hull — the flattest silhouette on the board — and the barrel is the longest single line in the image, crossing the right third. Two thin antennae rise from the turret as the only verticals.

**Light & surface.** Bright, even hangar light, strong floor reflection, white plates carrying soft grey shading that models the low facets. Surfaces are polished and seamless; the black track band is the only matte, textured element. The barrel's white finish with a black mid-section is the one deliberate two-tone detail.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: black tracks and pods against the white field are high separation; white hull against the white hangar is low separation — the hull's outline holds only where grey shading separates it from the background. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the lightest, flattest silhouette on the board — a useful counterpoint to the dark, tall-turreted tanks — and the white-barrel detail is a clean inversion of the usual black gun, but a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
