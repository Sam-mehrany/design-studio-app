# REF-0023 — Russian food-market growth — dark data slide on laptop (5,7% / 11,7% / 18,1%)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the dark staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a Russian-language data slide on a laptop — "Рост российского рынка продуктов питания" with three big percentages in a grid — staged against a dark blue wall with Russian captions above the screen.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: near-black panel (about #0B0F1A) grading to deep navy (about #16233D), white numerals and type, one small food photograph bottom-right. Monochrome dark with zero accent: meaning is carried by size and position, not by hue.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: title at about 16–18 px, display numerals "5,7%", "11,7%", "18,1%" at about 32–36 px, labels at about 9–10 px. Two-tier scale — the numerals are the hero at roughly 3× the labels — with tabular-style lining numerals and a comma decimal separator. All copy is Russian (Cyrillic).

**Layout & harmony.** Modular grid, estimated by eye: title top-left, a 2×2 stat grid with hairline seams, a small caption column bottom-left. Hierarchy is ranked by size (numerals first), then by enclosure (each stat in its own cell). Density is moderate — the grid is tight but each cell holds only a number and a label.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the screen is sharp-cornered; stat cells are separated by hairline seams, not by cards or shadows. Separation is by tone and by the seam lines. No elevation.

**Buttons & components.** There are no buttons; the components are the stat cells and the title block. The loudest elements are the three numerals — the data is the content and the control in the same stroke.

**Gradient & imagery.** A subtle dark gradient (black to navy) backs the panel; one small tight-cropped food photograph sits at the bottom-right of the grid. Delete the photograph and nothing breaks — the numbers carry the slide — so the dependency is soft.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white on near-black is about 17:1 — passes. The small grey labels, estimated at about #9A9A9A on near-black (about 7:1 estimated — passes, estimated, not measured). The title's grey-blue, estimated at about #8A97AD on dark navy, is the quietest pair at about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline, estimated, not measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the 2×2 stat grid with hero numerals and hairline seams is a clean, low-cost data-slide mechanism, but it is a staged concept mockup with Russian-only copy and no edge cases, so adapt, not adopt.
