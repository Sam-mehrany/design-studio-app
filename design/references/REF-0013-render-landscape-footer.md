# REF-0013 — Footer card sitting on a 3D-rendered orange landscape

## Read

*Figures measured off the image with Python/PIL (median-sampled hex, WCAG 2.x ratios, ink heights in original-image pixels unless stated); type-scale ratios are derived from those ink heights; radii in px are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** White card `#fefefe` inside a light frame `#f6f6f6`; the colour is all in the render — orange hills `#dc491c` through peach. Logo orange is the single UI accent.

**Typography.** Column heads bold black (18.1:1), links regular grey `#696969`. Small, tight scale; Title Case heads. Neo-grotesque.

**Layout & harmony.** Logo left, three link columns right, hairline rule, copyright left, round social icons right. The card overlaps the top of the render so the landscape reads as the page's ground.

**Shape & surface.** Large radius (~16px) card, flat, framed inside the image. Social icons in outlined circles.

**Buttons & components.** No buttons; social circles only.

**Gradient & imagery.** A large 3D-rendered landscape with a truck and road — the image is the brand moment. The footer works without it (soft), but the render is expensive to make and to ship.

**Contrast.** Heads 18.1:1, links/copy `#696969`/`#6c6c6c` on white = 5.2–5.5:1, pass.

## Why the verdict

Concept; cost (bespoke render) is the deciding killer. Card-over-illustration footer is adaptable with a cheaper image.
