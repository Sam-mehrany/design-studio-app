# REF-0013 — Photo: dripping honeycomb on a light grey seamless backdrop

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — light grey sweep #dadad9 / #e0dfe0 / #d3d2d1 (≈83%), darker at the top edge #c6c4c4; the honey is the only hue, #c48f3e amber to #6c3514 brown.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — subject centred and small, most of the frame left empty; one idea, lots of air.

**Shape & surface** — soft graded seamless; no props.

**Gradient & imagery** — the subtle backdrop falloff is the only gradient.

**Contrast** — dark honey #6c3514 on #dcdcdc 7.10:1; amber #c48f3e only 2.08:1.

## Why the verdict

Neutral light field with one warm accent and generous space — the composition, not the subject, is the reference.
