# REF-0037 — Photoshop tutorial: cutout dancer over twirled disc

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Canvas warm off-white `#f2eee9` / `#e6e4dd`; Photoshop chrome `#232323` / `#3f3536` / `#040404`; brown twirled disc `#6b4740`; red accent `#ab3c3a` (cap and shoes) is the only saturated hue.

**Layout & harmony.** Cutout figure mid-kick overlapping a circular "twirl" disc built from the photo itself — figure breaks the circle's edge to create depth.

**Gradient & imagery.** The disc is a distortion of photographic pixels; collapses without the photo.

**Contrast.** App UI only: Photoshop title text on `#494949` chrome ≈8.5:1 and Layers labels ≈10.9:1 (extremes of glyph vs panel).

## Why the verdict

Technique demo dependent on the photo (imagery_dep hard); the "subject breaks a circle" mechanism is a well-known poster trick rather than a UI reference.
