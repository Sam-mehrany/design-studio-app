# REF-0088 — Laptop on leather pouf — dark fintech landing, centred headline under striped orange glow, ghost + orange CTAs (Velox)

## Nature

Laptop on a leather pouf; rendered.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Screen `#020001`, warm peach-white headline `#e8cec8`, orange CTA `#e15508`, glow stripes `#8c4427`. Scene is the same hue family: tan leather `#381408` in a black room. The environment and accent are one colour.

**Typography.** Geometric sans, sentence case, centred two-line headline with ~18px glyph run (measured); tiny body and card text. Hero-to-body ~4× (*estimated*).

**Layout & harmony.** Centred single column: nav, headline, button pair, then three testimonial cards. Symmetric — mirrors cleanly.

**Shape & surface.** Pill buttons, rounded cards (~8px, *estimated*), flat.

**Buttons & components.** Ghost pill + orange filled pill side by side.

**Gradient & imagery.** Vertical striped radial glow behind the header — cheap if CSS, decoration only.

**Contrast.** Headline on glow `#441a0c` = 10.1:1. Card text `#5b4d4e` on black = 2.58:1 — fails; sub `#4c3733` = 1.73:1 — fails (tiny). White on the orange `#e15508` = 3.82:1 — fails for text; black on it = 5.5:1.

## Why the verdict

Generic dark fintech hero; the leather-matches-accent staging is the only distinctive move. Adapt, low.
