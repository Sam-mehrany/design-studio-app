# REF-0061 — Gold dragon over stitched calligraphy roundel on black

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — black `#000000` (≈44%); gold `#c2a54b` body with highlights to `#ecd768` and a few flame oranges `#985a28`; grey-white `#dfd1b9` sketchy calligraphy. Single accent.

**Typography** — brush-script Chinese characters rendered as rough white stitch strokes, top and bottom and in a circular roundel; illustrative, not typographic.

**Layout & harmony** — vertical S-curve dragon over a centred circle; symmetric framing by the characters.

**Shape & surface** — embroidery-like scale texture.

**Gradient & imagery** — illustration is the artefact.

**Contrast** — not measured as text (characters are decorative).

## Why the verdict

Decorative illustration with no system to lift.
