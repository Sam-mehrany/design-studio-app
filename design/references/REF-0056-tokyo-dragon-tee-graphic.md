# REF-0056 — 'Tokyo Japan' dragon and Mt Fuji tee graphic (stock vector)

## Read

**Measured** with PIL off the source file unless a line says *estimated*.

**Colour** — warm black `#221e1f` (≈52%); triad of dusty pink `#904161`, teal `#47968d` and gold `#e3b024`, with the sun a vertical gradient from gold to the ground.

**Typography** — "TOKYO JAPAN" in widely tracked sans caps across the foot, white; a vertical kanji box at upper left.

**Layout & harmony** — centred emblem: sun, Fuji, dragon, sakura and palms stacked into one badge; dense and symmetric.

**Shape & surface** — flat vector with line detail; no texture.

**Gradient & imagery** — one decorative sun gradient; the illustration is the artefact.

**Contrast** — caption `#fffeff` on `#211d1e` 16.41:1; kanji 16.47:1.

## Why the verdict

Maximalist badge illustration with total imagery dependence; the only liftable piece is the tracked-caps caption, which is generic.
