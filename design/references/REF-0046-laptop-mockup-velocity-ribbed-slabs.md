# REF-0046 — Laptop mockup over ribbed grey slabs, "Velocity." running-photo hero

## Nature

Laptop over ribbed grey slabs under hard CG light.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Environment is greyscale: black void #000000, ribbed slabs #3f3f3f–#7a7a7a, ground #525252, silver chassis #ababab. The only warmth is the screen photo (#8e7863 wall, #4b3d28 shadow). The on-screen stat card is pure #ffffff.

**Typography.** On screen: one giant neo-grotesque word "Velocity." (cap height ≈70 preview px, tight tracking) against micro body copy — a display-only, dramatic scale.

**Layout & harmony.** Top-down three-quarter view of the laptop, cropped hard at frame edges; the slabs lead the eye diagonally to the screen. Screen layout: left-anchored giant word over full-bleed photo, small white stat card ("98%") lower right.

**Shape & surface.** Environment depth is real rendered geometry and soft studio light. On screen, the stat card has a medium radius and no shadow.

**Buttons & components.** None legible at this scale; the screen is a hero only.

**Gradient & imagery.** The render is the subject: remove it and nothing remains. On screen, the hero depends entirely on the running photograph.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: "Velocity." #ffffff on photo #938275 ≈3.69:1 — passes as large display only; stat card black on white 21:1.

## Why the verdict

Device presentation: imagery-dependent and costly by nature. Worth keeping as a presentation reference (monochrome staging so the screen is the only warm object); the on-screen hero breaks under RTL because it is a left-anchored word over a photo.
