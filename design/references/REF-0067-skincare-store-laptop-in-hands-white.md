# REF-0067 — Hands-holding-laptop mockup on white — skincare store with giant thin "071" wordmark and editorial photography

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye.

**Colour.** White void (`#fefefe` field, screen `#f8f8f8`), black type; the only hue is the product's amber (`#f28213`) arriving through photography — the brand colour lives in the product, not the UI.

**Typography.** Oversized thin geometric display numerals "071" occupy the top-left (≈15% of screen height, *estimated*), with a tracked-caps tagline spaced across beside it; everything else is micro caps (`#403e41`). Extreme two-tier scale: wordmark-to-body well over 10× (*estimated*).

**Layout & harmony.** Asymmetric editorial grid with hairline cell borders: large tight-crop portrait, smaller detail crops, a product card at right. The wordmark breaks the top of the grid.

**Shape & surface.** Sharp corners, 1px hairlines, no shadows.

**Buttons & components.** Micro text links and small black tag chips; commerce is barely signalled — the photography sells.

**Gradient & imagery.** Hard: tight-crop portrait and product macros carry the composition.

**Contrast.** Wordmark black on white = 21:1; micro copy `#403e41` on white = 10.5:1. The risk is size, not ratio — caps at ~5px rendered.

## Why the verdict

Fashion-editorial mechanism (one giant display element, everything else tiny, colour from product photography) transfers; it is hard-dependent on art-directed photography and left-anchored. Concept → adapt.
