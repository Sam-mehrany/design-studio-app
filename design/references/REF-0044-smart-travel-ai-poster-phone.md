# REF-0044 — Smart (Travel) AI — travel-app poster landing around a phone

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the sky background, not the phone screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a travel app's landing presented as a poster — a phone in the centre ("Hi Slava! Plan The Best Trip To The Vacation", Earth-at-night photo, Dubai card, SFO→DUB, 39°C) surrounded by giant white type ("Smart", "(Travel)", "AI") and microcopy on a sky-and-clouds photograph.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: sky blue (about #5A8AC8) with white clouds, white giant type, black phone with a dark screen (Earth photo, about #0A1A2E). Single accent: the phone's dark screen is the only dark mass on a bright sky — the contrast between sky and screen is the composition.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "Smart" and "(Travel)" at about 64–72 px, "AI" at about 120–140 px, microcopy ("CHASE THE SUNSET", "PACK YOUR BAGS", "GET LOST IN THE CHARM OF EXOTIC DESTINATIONS") at about 8–10 px caps, phone UI at about 11–13 px. Dramatic scale — "AI" is roughly 12× the microcopy — with one grotesque face at three weights.

**Layout & harmony.** Canvas layout, estimated by eye: the phone is centred, the giant words are placed around it (Smart left, (Travel) right, AI bottom-right), microcopy fills the gaps as captions. Hierarchy is ranked by size ("AI" first), then by the phone's centrality. Density is moderate — the sky is busy with clouds, but the type placement is sparse and deliberate.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the phone at about 28–32 px radius, its inner cards at about 12–16 px, the poster type with no containers at all. Separation is by the sky photograph alone — type sits directly on clouds, with no cards, no borders, no shadows behind the poster words.

**Buttons & components.** The phone's own components are the search chips ("Beach", "Auxiliary", "Home"), the Dubai card with a Burj Khalifa photo, the SFO→DUB flight card and a 39°C weather card, plus a bottom tab bar. The poster's only control is the phone itself — the giant type is not clickable, it is a headline.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Two photographs: the sky-and-clouds poster background and the Earth-at-night photo inside the phone. Delete the sky and the poster is white type on white; delete the Earth photo and the phone is a dark slab — so the dependency is hard for both layers.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white "AI" on mid-blue sky is about 3:1 estimated — fails 4.5:1 where it crosses the brighter clouds (estimated, not measured). White on the phone's dark screen is about 15:1 estimated — passes. The 8–10 px microcopy on clouds is the least legible text on the poster.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the giant-poster-type-around-a-centred-phone is a transferable, low-cost app-launch presentation mechanism, but the poster collapses without its sky photograph (imagery_dep hard) and it is a staged concept mockup, so adapt, not adopt.
