# REF-0036 — TQA — "Unlocking Boundless Potential Through Agentic AI" black landing on monitor

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the orange staging wall, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: an AI consultancy's landing on a monitor on a rock — black hero, "UNLOCKING BOUNDLESS POTENTIAL THROUGH AGENTIC AI" with "AGENTIC AI" in italic mint green, two article cards right, a partner-logo strip along the bottom.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: black hero (about #0A0A0A), white type, one mint-green accent (about #7FE8C0) used only for the italic "AGENTIC AI" line and the "CONTACT US" pill, an orange-red light streak (about #E85A1A) crossing the hero diagonally. Single accent: mint marks the product phrase and the one CTA, nothing else.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline in a grotesque at about 28–32 px with the third line in italic, body at about 11–12 px, nav at about 9–10 px, partner logos at about 10–12 px. Two-tier scale — the headline is the only display size — with the italic line as the emphasis device inside an otherwise upright grotesque.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: nav across the top, headline left (~55%) with body and two text links below, two article cards right (~45%) with photographs, a partner-logo strip (UiPath, Microsoft, Databricks, OpenAI, Gemini, ServiceNow, Anthropic) along the bottom. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by the mint italic line. Density is moderate — headline, cards and logo strip fill the screen without crowding.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the article cards are sharp-cornered with hairline dividers, the "CONTACT US" pill is the only curve. Separation is by tone — the black field against the photographs — and by the orange light streak. No shadows, no elevation.

**Buttons & components.** One mint filled pill CTA ("CONTACT US") top-right, two text links under the body ("PLAY VIDEO", "REQUEST A MEETING") and "READ ARTICLE" links under each card. The CTA is the loudest control; the article cards are quiet content containers with a date line and a title each.

**Gradient & imagery.** A diagonal orange-red light streak crosses the black hero; two small photographs top the article cards. Delete the photographs and the hero still reads — headline, CTA and logo strip survive — so the dependency is soft; the streak is decorative and the photos are supporting, not structural.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white on black is about 19:1 — passes. Mint on black, estimated at about #7FE8C0 (about 12:1 estimated — passes, estimated, not measured). The small grey body copy, estimated at about #9A9A9A on black (about 7:1 estimated — passes, estimated, not measured). The white card titles over the photographs are the least legible pair.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the black hero with an italic accent line, article cards and a partner-logo strip is a transferable B2B-landing mechanism at medium cost, but it is a staged concept mockup and the orange streak is decorative rather than structural, so adapt, not adopt.
