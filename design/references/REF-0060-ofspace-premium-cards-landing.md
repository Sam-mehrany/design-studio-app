# REF-0060 — Ofspace — premium cards fintech landing page, black hero with lime accent

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels, WCAG ratios computed from sampled colours); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a full-page fintech landing capture — black hero, white body, pricing $9/$19/$39 a month — with 3D card renders as the hero image.

**Colour.** Measured: hero field sampled at #222627 (near-black), lime call-to-action sampled at #C5E26C. Single accent — lime — used only for the primary button, price highlights and the closing banner; everything else is black, white and grey. True dual value strategy: a black hero block and a black pricing card sit inside an otherwise white page, so the dark sections read as deliberate inversions, not a theme.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: hero headline "Get our premium cards and pay anywhere" at about 40–48 px, tight leading, grotesque sans, sentence case; section headlines at about 28–32 px; body at about 13–14 px grey. Two-tier scale — large headlines, one quiet body tier — with the $9/$19/$39 prices as the only numerals, set large and lining. Hierarchy is by size alone; weight barely changes.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, full-page capture: hero, centred mission statement, four feature cards in a row, pricing table, testimonial, lime closing banner, footer. Spacing is generous between sections and tight inside cards. The four feature cards share one template (image top, title, two lines of copy, "Start now" link), so the middle of the page reads as a calm, repeated rhythm after the loud hero.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated by eye: hero and cards use large radii (about 16–20 px), separation is by tone (black hero, white cards on white) and by thin borders on the pricing table — no shadows. The card renders inside the hero are the only dimensional objects; the page itself is flat.

**Buttons & components.** Two buttons in the hero: lime filled "Join Ofspace" and a dark outline "Compare all offers" — the lime one is unmistakably primary. Elsewhere the actions are small text links ("Start now >>"), much quieter than the data, which keeps the feature cards feeling like content rather than a sales grid. Pricing uses a dark "Premium" card to mark the top tier.

**Gradient & imagery.** The hero image is two 3D card renders floating over black — no photography. Removing the renders would leave a black box with a headline; the page would still read, but the product (the cards) would be invisible, so the imagery is soft-dependency: decorative for layout, load-bearing for meaning.

**Contrast.** Measured: lime #C5E26C on hero black #222627 is 10.53:1 — passes. White headline on the same black is about 15:1 — passes. Body grey on white is estimated by eye at about #6B7280, roughly 4.8:1 — passes, estimated, not measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the black-hero/white-body inversion with a single lime accent is a clean, transferable landing mechanism at medium cost, but this is a concept capture (no live data, no edge cases visible), so adapt, not adopt.
