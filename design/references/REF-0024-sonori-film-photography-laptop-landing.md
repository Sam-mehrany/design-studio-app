# REF-0024 — Sonori — film & photography studio landing on laptop (orange diptych)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the dark staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a film/photography studio's landing on a laptop — a bold all-caps statement over two orange photographs (a rider on a motorcycle, a concept car) on a white page.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: white page (about #FAFAF8), black type, two saturated orange photographs (about #E88A00 and about #C85A1A). The photographs are the only colour on the page — there is no UI accent hue at all; the orange is content, not interface.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: the statement "SONORI EXPLORES THE EMOTIONAL POWER OF FILM AND PHOTOGRAPHY…" in a condensed grotesque all-caps at about 20–24 px, micro-labels at about 8–9 px. Two-tier scale — the statement is the only display size, everything else is microcopy. The all-caps block reads as a poster line, not a headline.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: hairline nav at the top, the statement block, then a two-column photo diptych filling the lower half. Hierarchy is ranked by size (statement first), then by the photographs' colour weight. Density is low — three elements and a lot of white.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: everything sharp-cornered, no radius anywhere. Separation is by the white gutters between the two photographs and by hairline rules. No cards, no shadow, no elevation.

**Buttons & components.** There are no visible buttons; the nav is micro text links. The components are the statement block and the photo diptych — the photographs are the call to action, which is the risk of the design.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Two full-bleed photographs carry the lower half of the page and all of its colour. Delete them and the page is a white sheet with a statement and no reason to stay — so the dependency is hard.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: black on white is about 17:1 — passes. The micro-labels under the statement, estimated at about #8A8A8A on white (about 3.5:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured). No text sits on the photographs, so the photos create no text-contrast problem.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the poster-statement-over-photo-diptych is a transferable studio-landing mechanism at medium cost, but the page collapses without its two photographs (imagery_dep hard) and it is a staged concept mockup, so adapt, not adopt.
