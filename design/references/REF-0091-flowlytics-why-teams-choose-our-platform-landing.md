# REF-0091 — Flowlytics — Why Teams Choose Our Platform landing section with revenue chart and footer

## Read

**Colour.** A Flowlytics landing section on a measured near-black field, #010101, with testimonial cards one step up at #0E0E0E; a single lime accent carries all meaning — chart line sampled #5AAF48 and the Submit fill #A1EC6E — against otherwise white-to-grey type, with no second hue anywhere in the frame.

**Typography.** One grotesque sans throughout (exact face cannot be determined from the screenshot); sentence-case body against a semibold display heading roughly twice body size, so a moderate two-step weight scale, with tracked all-caps month labels JAN–AUG under the chart and small muted footer links.

**Layout & harmony.** Split two-column feature block — checklist and Get Started on the left, Revenue vs Target chart on the right — above a four-column footer, with large vertical voids between sections; hierarchy is carried by size and position, and spacing is generous rather than rhythmic.

**Shape & surface.** Surfaces separate by tone, not shadow: cards and the email field are flat tonal steps off the black field with hairline borders; radii are medium on cards and the email field, fully pill on the Get Started button, with no elevation or blur anywhere.

**Buttons & components.** Two actions only: a white pill Get Started button and a lime Submit button inside a bordered email field; both are solid fills louder than the surrounding data, with checkmark list items, avatar-and-quote testimonial cards, and a line chart with a two-item legend as the supporting components.

**Gradients & imagery.** No gradients; the only photography is three small circular-cropped avatar portraits in the testimonial cards, and the chart is drawn as flat vector lines with a faint area fill under the green revenue line — deleting the portraits would leave the composition intact.

**Contrast.** Headings near-white #EEEEEE on #010101 measure about 18:1 and pass; the green chart line #5AAF48 on #010101 measures 7.6:1 and the dark text on the lime Submit fill measures about 12:1, both passing; body grey sampled around #606060–#707070 measures 3.3–4.2:1 and footer links around #505050 measure about 2.6:1, both below the 4.5:1 floor and failing.

## Why the verdict

The split feature-plus-chart mechanism and the dark single-accent system stand without the avatar photography and cost little to build, but the data is polished and edge-case-free with no sign of real-world use, and the left-anchored split layout needs rework to mirror — so adapt, not adopt.
