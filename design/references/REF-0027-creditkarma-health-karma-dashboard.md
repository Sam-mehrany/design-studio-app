# REF-0027 — CreditKarma — "Health Karma" credit dashboard on monitor (score 832)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a credit-score dashboard on a monitor, watched by a silhouetted person — hero score "832" left, six factor cards in a bento grid right, a black "Credit Offers" strip along the bottom.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: white page, black text, one orange-to-blue gradient card ("Credit Card Use", about #E8862E to about #4A7FA5) among five white cards, black bottom strip with three tinted offer cards (grey-blue, olive, mauve). Single accent family: the gradient card is the only saturated surface; everything else is neutral.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "Health Karma" at about 22–24 px, hero score "832" at about 40–44 px bold, card values ("9%", "100", "0", "4", "12") at about 20–24 px, labels at about 10–11 px. Two-tier scale — the score is the hero at roughly 2× the card values — with tabular lining numerals throughout.

**Layout & harmony.** Bento grid, estimated by eye: score block left (~35%) with a small gauge arc, six factor cards right in a 3×2 grid, black offers strip below spanning the full width. Hierarchy is ranked by size (832 first), then by enclosure (the gradient card is the only coloured cell). Density is high — seven data cells plus an offers strip on one screen.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: cards at about 16–20 px radius with a soft drop shadow, the gradient card the same radius. Separation is by shadow and by tone — white cards on a white page read through their shadows, the black strip reads by tone. No borders.

**Buttons & components.** The components are the factor cards themselves (each with a small icon, a label, a value and a sparkline or dot row), a segmented control ("Equifax / TransUnion"), pill tabs ("Karma / Credit / Money") and the offers strip. The loudest element is the "832" score, not any control — the cards are quiet data containers.

**Gradient & imagery.** One mesh-style gradient card (orange to blue) and three small tinted offer photos. Delete the gradient and the grid still reads — five white cards and the score survive — so the dependency is soft; the gradient marks the featured card, it does not carry the layout.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: black on white is about 17:1 — passes. White text on the gradient card, estimated at about 3:1 where it crosses the orange (estimated — fails, estimated, not measured). The grey "See all" and micro-labels, estimated at about #8A8A8A on white (about 3.5:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the hero-score-left / bento-factor-grid / offers-strip dashboard is a transferable data-home mechanism at medium cost, but it is a staged concept mockup, and the gradient card sits on the active brief's reject line (C.MAT.gradient-mesh), so adapt, not adopt.
