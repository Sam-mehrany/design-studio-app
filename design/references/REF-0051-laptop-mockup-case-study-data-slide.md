# REF-0051 — Laptop mockup in a dark case-study layout, data slide with big percentage numerals

## Nature

Laptop in a dark rendered set inside a case-study layout.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Deep navy gradient field: #050810 at the top, #1b2940 mid, lifting to #5b7487 at the lower-right horizon. Hairline grid #22252d. The on-screen slide is near-black #161618 cells on a #737375 surround with a faint cool glow #667280. No accent hue.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque captions in the page frame (Cyrillic, cap height ≈23–24 preview px), grey #b1bec2 for the label column and white for the statement. On screen: large display numerals ("5,7%", "11,7%", "18,1%") with small labels beneath, a "2/5" page counter.

**Layout & harmony.** Case-study page structure: a thin hairline grid divides the frame into columns; caption left, statement right, laptop shot from a low angle filling the lower two-thirds. Screen is a modular grid of stat cells.

**Shape & surface.** Flat dark tiles separated by seams; environment depth is the gradient field and the laptop's reflections.

**Buttons & components.** None — a presentation slide.

**Gradient & imagery.** Navy field gradient plus a rendered laptop. The slide on screen stands on its own as numerals on dark tiles.

**Contrast.** Measured: page captions white on #04050c 20.35:1, grey label column ≈14:1; on screen numerals white on #262628 15.1:1.

## Why the verdict

Mockup of a data slide inside a case-study page; numerals on dark tiles is a cheap, adaptable mechanism, the rendered presentation is not. Low confidence on archetype — the image mixes case-study page, device and slide.
