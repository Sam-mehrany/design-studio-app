# REF-0001 — Flat-vector top-down designer desk illustration

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — white #fefefe (40%) and pale grey #e3e5e6 / #f5f5f6 carry the field; near-black #151513 (26%) for the monitor bezel, keyboard and top band; the only hue is a tan wood/skin #e3c19e (6%) and a dusty teal #364b4d (3%) on the plant. Neutral-led with two low-chroma notes; nothing signals meaning.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — top-down flat-lay: blank monitor as the anchor, tablet + stylus, keyboard, mug, plant and notes arranged around it on white with generous gaps. One stroke-free shape language, no outlines except the black bezel.

**Shape & surface** — flat fills, no shading or shadow; corners slightly rounded on devices, consistent across objects.

**Gradient & imagery** — no gradients. The illustration is the whole image.

**Contrast** — black on white 18.13:1; tan #e3c19e on white only 1.68:1, so the warm accent reads as a tint, not an edge.

## Why the verdict

Not a UI; the flat-vector, near-neutral illustration style is the transferable part (empty-state or onboarding spot art). Stock-art finish keeps confidence low.
