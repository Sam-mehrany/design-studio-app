# REF-0053 — Tablet mockup on perforated hex metal, grey landing with 79% stat bento

## Nature

Tablet on perforated hex metal; rendered.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Environment: perforated hex metal #3a3738 with black holes. Screen surface #cccccc with tonal cards #c4c5c5 and a darker photo card; the hero photo is a warm blurred dark #1f1713 → #33191a. Black primary pill. Achromatic UI; colour only in the photo.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque. A big "79%" stat (cap height ≈17 preview px) is the loudest type on the page, larger than the section heading (≈11).

**Layout & harmony.** Bento: hero photo card, stat card, text card and image cards tiled in a grid. Hierarchy by the display numeral first, then by enclosure.

**Shape & surface.** Medium radius on all cards — one family. Depth is tonal; cards are a shade off the surface, no shadows.

**Buttons & components.** Black pill "Book a demo" top right; secondary links in text.

**Gradient & imagery.** Photo cards decorate; the bento grid stands without them.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: heading and "79%" black on #cccccc 13.1:1, card text #454344 on #adadad 4.37:1, small body #5d5a5b on #c3c4c4 3.90:1. Failure: stat caption #a3a2a2 on #cccccc 1.59:1.

## Why the verdict

Grey-on-grey tonal bento is cheap to build and adaptable; captions at 1.6:1 must not be copied.
