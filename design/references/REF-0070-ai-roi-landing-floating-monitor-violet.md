Floating monitor; garbled on-screen copy gives it away as image-model output.

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye.

**Colour.** Monochrome violet: page `#f5f4fc`, hero panel a soft radial violet gradient from `#464270` bottom-left to `#e2e0ed`; white text and buttons. Environment navy `#01030e` fading to grey `#cecfd4`.

**Typography.** Headline mixes a sans first line with a serif second line ("— Not Another Pilot"); small sans body; tiny nav. Hero-to-body about 2.5× (*estimated*).

**Layout & harmony.** Framed hero panel inset with a large radius inside the white page; claim bottom-left, logo strip bottom-right. Sparse, size ranks.

**Shape & surface.** Large radius on the hero panel, pill buttons, flat surfaces.

**Buttons & components.** White filled pill "Try for free" (`#fbfcff`) next to an outline pill "View Demo"; same pair repeated in the nav.

**Gradient & imagery.** The violet soft gradient is the only imagery; without it the hero is white on white (soft dependency, but the copy then has no ground).

**Contrast.** Headline `#f7f5ff` on `#8f8ca8` = 3.00:1 (large-text pass only); body `#c4c1dc` on `#747094` = 2.68:1 — fails; nav `#bfbfbf` on `#fbfbfb` = 1.78:1 — fails. White button on the deep violet = 9.0:1.

## Why the verdict

Reject: the copy is AI-garbled ("production=greds.hl", "taams"), so it is not a real design, and body and nav fail contrast. Low contrast is the mechanism here, not an accident.
