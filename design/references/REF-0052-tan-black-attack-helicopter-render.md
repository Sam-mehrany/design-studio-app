# REF-0052 — Tan-and-black attack helicopter in flight against cloud sky — 3D render

## Read

Values below were **measured by sampling pixels** (hex values from sampled points); there is no typography in the image. One line of context: a 3D render of an attack helicopter in flight — the only aircraft on the board, and the only image shot outdoors in a sky rather than staged in a hangar. No UI archetype applies.

**Colour.** Measured: tan armour sampled at #A3865E in a two-hue system with black — bold, irregular tan/black blocks across fuselage, tail and engine pods, a hard-edged disruptive pattern rather than a gradient. The sky behind is cool grey-blue cloud (#767E81 dominant), so the warm tan reads warmer still by contrast. No accent colour; the pattern itself is the decoration.

**Composition & form.** Side profile, nose left, tail right, rotor disc cutting horizontally across the top third of the frame and breaking the frame edge on both sides. The helicopter sits slightly above centre with empty sky below, giving it altitude. Form is angular and faceted — flat glass canopy panels, slab-sided pods, rocket racks slung under the fuselage — with the spinning rotor blur as the only soft element.

**Light & surface.** Bright daylight from above, hard shadows under the pods and inside the intakes, gloss on the canopy glass. Surfaces are polished hard panels with weathering streaks and rivets; the black pattern blocks are flat, the tan carries the shading. The rotor blur is the only motion cue in an otherwise frozen frame.

**Typography.** None — no legible type anywhere in the image.

**Contrast.** Measured: dark fuselage masses against the pale cloud field are high figure/ground separation, and the tan/black pattern blocks are high-contrast against each other. No text, so no WCAG pair applies.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the strongest camouflage-pattern study on the board — hard-edged two-tone blocks that break up a large form — and the only outdoor, in-motion shot, but a concept render, so adapt as mood/form reference only.
