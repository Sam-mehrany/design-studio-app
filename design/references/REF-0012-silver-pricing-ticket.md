# REF-0012 — Silver pricing card — metallic $20 numeral and ticket perforation

## Read

*Figures measured off the image with Python/PIL (median-sampled hex, WCAG 2.x ratios, ink heights in original-image pixels unless stated); type-scale ratios are derived from those ink heights; radii in px are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Monochrome on navy-black: page `#0d0e12`, card `#0d0e13` (card ≈ page; it exists only through its rim), a single cool rim light `#218cb8` top-left. Metallic greys from `#e1e2e4` down through `#909193`.

**Typography.** Price numeral ≈95px tall vs body cap ≈11px (preview) — ~8.6×, the definition of a dramatic scale. All-caps 'PER MONTH' micro-label; lead copy two-tone (grey then white). Neo-grotesque.

**Layout & harmony.** Centred single column: tier + badge → price → unit → pitch → perforation → CTA → billing note. Sparse and symmetric.

**Shape & surface.** Large radius card with a ticket notch and dashed perforation dividing pitch from action; hairline rim instead of shadow; polished metal finish on numeral and button.

**Buttons & components.** Full-width metallic pill (`#909193` → `#c9c9cb`), black text 6.46:1 at the darkest point — legible but lower than it looks.

**Gradient & imagery.** Metallic gradients on text and button, a rim glow. No imagery; medium cost.

**Contrast.** Body grey `#9fa0a4` on `#0d0e13` = 7.38:1; 'PER MONTH' ≈ 17:1. Numeral contrast varies along its gradient (measured highlight ≈14.7:1).

## Why the verdict

Concept; centred and symmetric so RTL is clean. Ticket perforation and the huge numeral transfer; metallic finish is optional dressing.
