# REF-0092 — Laptop on dark terrain under grey sky — red-orange headline panel with oversized black grotesque, B/W photo strip (SHIFT5)

## Read

Measured: hex values and WCAG ratios sampled from the original file with Python/PIL (median of a region for grounds, extreme-luminance pixels for text, so anti-aliasing can pull a ratio slightly low); cap heights by row profiling where stated. Anything marked *estimated* was read by eye. These pins are ~564px wide, so small UI text is only 3–6px tall in the file; its sampled ratio reads lower than the designed colour would, and is flagged where that matters.

**Colour.** Scene desaturated: sky `#a2a3a3`, terrain `#0e0e0e`. Screen: a red-orange panel `#f05a46` takes ~70% of the hero; the rest is grey `#afafaf`, a dark status panel `#303030` and a B/W mountain photo strip. One hue against an otherwise greyscale frame; the orange even spills onto the keyboard deck (`#6a3e38`).

**Typography.** Oversized regular-weight neo-grotesque "Op ⁄ Intelligence" in black, title case, tight — 37px glyph run (measured) on a ~300px screen, vs ~4px body: dramatic scale.

**Layout & harmony.** Asymmetric: headline flush-left across the orange block, a narrow right column (copy above, "System Status" list below), a full-width photo strip at the base. Size ranks.

**Shape & surface.** Sharp corners, flat blocks of colour, no shadows.

**Buttons & components.** Nothing loud; small text links and a status list.

**Gradient & imagery.** B/W photo strip is atmosphere (soft); the orange block carries the page.

**Contrast.** Headline sampled `#3d160e` on `#e86250` = 4.77:1 (pure black on `#f05a46` = 6.26:1 — passes). Small copy on orange `#a03724` = 2.03:1 — fails; grey-panel text 2.27:1 — fails; status text `#7c7c7b` on `#252525` = 3.67:1 — fails.

## Why the verdict

Black type on a saturated colour block is a strong, cheap poster move, and the grey scene keeps the one hue loud. Left-anchored headline breaks under RTL; small text fails. Adapt, med.
