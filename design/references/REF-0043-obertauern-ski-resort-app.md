# REF-0043 — Obertauern Ski Resort — Austria detail screen on iPhone (-8°C, 198 frosties)

## Read

Values below: black background and white card were **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a ski-resort detail screen on an iPhone — a photo hero of a snowy mountain lake with a "-8°C Temperature Currently" pill and avatar hearts, a white card below ("Obertauern Ski Resort, Austria, Salzburg", "26 ski runs / 112 km / 8 types"), a second resort card starting underneath.

**Colour.** Measured: black screen surround #05050D, white card #EAEAEA. Monochrome UI — black header, white cards, black type — with the photograph as the only colour: blue sky, white snow, dark green forest.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "Obertauern Ski Resort" at about 20–22 px semibold, "Austria, Salzburg" at about 14–15 px grey, stats ("26 ski runs", "112 km", "8 types") at about 14–15 px, temperature "-8°C" in a dot-matrix numeral at about 16–18 px. Two-tier scale — the resort name is the display size — with tabular numerals in the stats and a dot-matrix face for the temperature.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: back chevron and "Austria" title top, a thumbnail strip (five small photos, centre one selected with a ring), the photo hero with floating pills, a white detail card, a segmented control (bell / bed / snowflake) and the next resort card. Hierarchy is ranked by size (resort name first), then by the photo hero's weight. Density is moderate — six stacked sections on one screen.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the hero and the cards at about 24–28 px radius, the thumbnails at about 12–16 px, the pills at full pill radius. Separation is by tone and soft shadow — white cards on a black surround — with the floating pills as black chips on the photograph. No borders.

**Buttons & components.** The components are the thumbnail strip, the temperature pill, the "198 frosties" pill, the avatar-and-heart clusters on the photo, the stats pill row and the three-icon segmented control. The loudest controls are the floating black pills on the photograph — they read as chips, not buttons — while the segmented control is the quietest element.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. One full-bleed photograph of the mountain lake is the hero: delete it and the screen is a black header with white cards and no reason to choose this resort — so the dependency is hard. The avatar hearts are social proof dressed as UI.

**Contrast.** Measured: black type #1A1A1A on the white card #EAEAEA is 14.47:1 — passes (measured, from the sampled card). White on the black temperature pill is about 19:1 estimated — passes. The grey "Austria, Salzburg" subtitle, estimated at about #777777 on white (about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the photo hero with floating info pills + white detail card + stats pill row is a transferable, low-cost travel-detail mechanism, but the screen collapses without its photograph (imagery_dep hard) and it is a staged concept mockup, so adapt, not adopt.
