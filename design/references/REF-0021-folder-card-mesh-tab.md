# REF-0021 — Folder-tab card with mesh-gradient insert, heavy black frame

## Nature

One folder-tab card with a mesh-gradient insert.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured with PIL off the pin image (hex = mode of a sampled patch; text ink = strongest anti-aliased pixel in the glyph box, so small-text ratios can understate slightly; sizes are ink heights in image px, not CSS px).*

**Colour.** Neutral card on a white field: backdrop `#fafafa`, card body `#222222`, frame stroke `#000000`. The only hue is a mesh gradient in the tab window: peach `#f8d2a6` → `#eaa074` → coral `#ec7c5c` → magenta `#dc50e4`. Colour decorates; it carries no meaning.

**Typography.** Geometric sans (Poppins/Montserrat class, estimated). Title cap height 16.9px, count numeral `04` 34.4px — a 2.0× jump over the title and 2.3× over the `1012 Shots` label (14.9px). Bold numeral, unit word (`Tags`) set small beside it.

**Layout & harmony.** One object. Title + subtitle top-left, numeral + unit bottom-left, secondary count bottom-right — a two-corner composition with the mesh window filling the middle third.

**Shape & surface.** Large outer radius (~24px est.) with a folder-tab notch cut into the top edge; ~12px solid black frame (heavy stroke) around a flat dark body. No shadow, no tone steps.

**Buttons & components.** None; the card is the control.

**Gradient & imagery.** The mesh gradient is the whole personality. Delete it and the card still reads (frame, numeral, labels), but becomes a plain dark tile.

**Contrast.** Title and numerals `#ffffff` on `#222222` = 16.3:1. Subtitle `#8f8f8f` on `#222222` = 5.0:1 — passes.

## Why the verdict

Mechanism (big count + small unit on a folder tile) is transferable; the mesh window and the 12px black frame are a showcase finish, and it is a concept shot.
