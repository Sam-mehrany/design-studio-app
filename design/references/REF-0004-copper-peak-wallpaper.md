# REF-0004 — Phone wallpaper: single slate peak against a copper sky

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — two families only: a muted copper/terracotta ramp #71453a → #935947 → #af7e6e (≈56% of pixels) and blue-black slate #07080d / #1c1b20 / #3b3032 (≈44%). Warm field, cold mass.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — one centred peak, sky above and copper-lit ground below; a single silhouette carries the frame.

**Shape & surface** — soft photographic falloff, no hard edges besides the ridge line.

**Gradient & imagery** — the copper reads as a gentle vertical gradient (#7e4e40 at the top, #a06858 lower). Without the photo nothing remains.

**Contrast** — copper #935947 against slate #1c1b20 only 3.06:1: a low-contrast, atmospheric pairing.

## Why the verdict

Palette is the only transferable part (muted copper + blue-black as a two-family dark theme); low contrast means it needs a separate text colour.
