# REF-0082 — Figure standing in a moss garden — white tee, yellow shoes, white void

## Read

This is a photography / mood reference, not a UI screen — no UI archetype applies. Values below: hex values **measured by pixel sampling** (dominant colours); proportions estimated by eye. One line of context: a fashion editorial photograph — a man in a white tee, faded jeans and yellow shoes standing centred in a low island of moss and potted plants against a seamless white void.

**Colour.** Measured: white void #FAFBFD, moss greens (sampled dark green #2B3515, pale moss #A7A681), denim grey-blue. The palette is white plus green: the plants are the only colour field, the figure is white tee and grey denim, and the yellow shoes (estimated ~#E8D44A) are the single saturated accent — two small points of yellow at the bottom of the frame that anchor the whole image.

**Composition.** The figure is centred horizontally and stands at the vertical middle; the plant island is a low horizontal band across the lower third, and the upper half is empty white. The tall spiky plant on the right balances the dark leafy mass on the left — an asymmetric balance inside a centred figure. About 55% of the frame is blank white.

**Light & form.** Bright, even studio light, almost shadowless — the plants read by their own dark/light values, not by cast shadow. Forms are verticals: the figure's straight stance, the spiky plant stems, the jeans' straight legs. The moss island is the only horizontal mass, and its irregular top edge is the image's one organic line.

**Typography.** No type in the image at all — no wordmark, no label, no caption. The white tee carries a tiny chest graphic too small to read at this scale; it is a garment detail, not typography.

**Surface & staging.** The staging is the reference: a "garden" built as a freestanding island on a white floor — moss, ferns and potted plants arranged as a set, with the figure planted in it like one more element. The void does the work: without the white surround, the island would be a garden bed; with it, it is a diorama.

**Imagery.** Photography is the entire artefact. Its value as a reference is the colour mechanism: a monochrome figure and void, one green mass, one tiny saturated accent (the shoes) — restraint with a single loud note, the same discipline as a single-accent UI.

**Contrast.** The dark figure and dark plants read strongly against white #FAFBFD (black-on-white ≈ 21:1 at the extremes). The white tee is close in value to the void, so the figure's torso dissolves into the background and the silhouette is carried by the dark jeans, the skin and the yellow shoes.

Adapt (provisional, as a mood reference): the white-void-plus-one-green-mass-plus-one-accent formula is directly stealable for campaign and landing imagery, but this is a brand fashion photograph, not a UI pattern, so it can never be adopt — adapt, as imagery direction only.
