# REF-0064 — Supervity AI — presentation deck, three slides with lime green blobs

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a presentation-design promo showing three slides for "Supervity AI" — hero, "Why automation alone isn't enough", "B2A migration" — stacked on a black background with a Russian order-contact line at the top. This is a slide deck, not a website; J.ACQ.feature is the closest archetype, not an exact fit.

**Colour.** Measured: slides are white (#FFFFFF) and black, with a single lime-green accent in the blob shapes and numbered cards; the page background holding the three slides is black. True dual value strategy across the slides: white hero, mixed second slide, black-and-green third slide. The lime is used for emphasis shapes and one numbered card, never for body text.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: slide headlines at about 24–32 px grotesque, sentence case, with "Supervity AI — your strategic partner in Agentic Era" as the largest line; body at about 11–12 px; numbered cards use a large numeral (about 20 px) as the anchor. Dramatic scale within each slide — headline roughly 3× body — but the three slides do not share one type scale; each slide sets its own sizes.

**Layout & harmony.** Three slides stacked vertically with black gutters between them, each slide its own grid: hero is centred headline with green blobs at the corners; the second slide is three cards in a row (two dark, one lime); the third is a two-column split with a photo card. The lime blobs are the unifying device — one appears on every slide, always at an edge, always partly cropped.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated by eye: large radii (about 16–20 px) on slide cards, separation by tone (black/white/lime blocks) with no shadows. The blobs are soft organic shapes against otherwise rectangular slides — the only curves in the deck — which is why they read as the brand mark rather than decoration.

**Buttons & components.** There are no buttons; the components are the slides, the numbered cards and the blobs. The loudest element on each slide is its headline, followed by the lime shape. A small pill ("Learn more", "Build & Scale") appears on the hero and third slide as the only action-like element, set quieter than the headline.

**Gradient & imagery.** No photography except one small portrait photo on the third slide; no gradients. The visual material is flat colour shapes — blobs and cards — so the deck has no imagery dependency in the photographic sense: remove the blobs and the slides still read, but they lose the one element that ties the three slides together.

**Contrast.** Measured: black headlines on white slides are about 18:1 — pass. White text on the black cards is the same in reverse — pass. The lime cards carry dark text; lime-on-black and black-on-lime are both high. The Russian header line ("Для заказа пишите в tg: @SashaS45") is white on black at small size — legible, but it is the designer's contact line, not part of the slides.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the cropped-blob-as-brand-device across slides is a clean, low-cost presentation mechanism, and the three-slide stack shows it surviving three different layouts — but this is a deck promo, not a website, so adapt, not adopt.
