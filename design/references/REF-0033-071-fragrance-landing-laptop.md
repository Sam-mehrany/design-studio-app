# REF-0033 — 071 — fragrance landing on laptop (giant numerals, model with amber bottle)

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the white staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a fragrance/cosmetic landing on a laptop held in two hands — a giant "071" numeral top-left, a tight-cropped photograph of a model holding an amber bottle, small product thumbnails and microcopy in a grid.

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: white page (about #FAFAF8), black type and numerals, one amber accent (about #C87A1A) in the bottle and a dried-orange slice. Monochrome plus amber: the amber is the product's colour, not a UI accent — it appears only inside the photographs.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: "071" in a grotesque at about 72–80 px extra-bold, microcopy at about 8–10 px, small labels at about 7–8 px. Dramatic scale — the numeral is roughly 8× the body copy — and the numeral is the headline: there is no headline text, only the number.

**Layout & harmony.** Modular grid, estimated by eye: the "071" numeral spans the top, the model photograph fills the left two-thirds below it, a narrow right column holds a product thumbnail, a price block and a small chart, a dried-orange slice sits bottom-centre. Hierarchy is ranked by size (071 first), then by the photograph's weight. Density is moderate — the grid is busy with small elements around one big number and one big photo.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: everything sharp-cornered, no radius anywhere. Separation is by the grid's hairlines and by the photographs' edges — no cards, no borders, no shadows. The page reads as a printed editorial spread.

**Buttons & components.** There are no visible buttons; the components are the numeral, the photographs and the microcopy blocks. The loudest element is the "071" numeral — the number is the brand.

**Gradient & imagery.** No gradient. Two photographs (the model with the bottle, the orange slice) and a product thumbnail carry the page's colour and weight. Delete them and the page is a giant number on white with microcopy — the composition survives as a poster but the product is gone — so the dependency is hard for the landing's purpose.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: black on white is about 17:1 — passes. The microcopy at 7–8 px, estimated at about #777777 on white (about 4.5:1 estimated — borderline at that size, estimated, not measured). The small text over the photograph's mid-tones is the least legible element.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the giant-numeral-as-headline over an editorial photo grid is a transferable fragrance-landing mechanism at medium cost, but the page collapses without its photographs (imagery_dep hard) and it is a staged concept mockup, so adapt, not adopt.
