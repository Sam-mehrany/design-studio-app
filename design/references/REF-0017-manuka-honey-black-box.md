# REF-0017 — Packaging: black Mānuka Honey box with white caps and a white data band (video still)

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — box #151920 / #12151e on a cool off-white table #dae3e8 → #b2b2b1. White caps, gold for provenance only (seal and "PRODUCT OF NEW ZEALAND", #b7aea1 measured on the compressed frame), white band #d4d9e5 for the grade. Black + white + one restrained gold.

**Typography** — "MĀNUKA HONEY" in heavy geometric-sans caps, flush left, ~2 lines; tracked small caps for the origin line; "1000+" as a display numeral with "MGO" set small beside it, net weight in micro-text below.

**Layout & harmony** — logo seal top-left, name at the optical centre, white band in the lower quarter holding the data; large black margins.

**Shape & surface** — matte black, square corners; the white band is the only containment.

**Gradient & imagery** — none on the pack; type and flat colour only.

**Contrast** — white title measured #c7d0da on #12151e = 11.69:1 (video compression lowers the white, so this is a floor); gold origin line 8.50:1; "1000+" #0b0c15 on band = 13.79:1.

## Why the verdict

Type-and-flat-colour system with one value promoted to a hero numeral with an adjacent unit — transferable. Flush-left caps make it risky to mirror; packaging rather than screen keeps it at adapt.
