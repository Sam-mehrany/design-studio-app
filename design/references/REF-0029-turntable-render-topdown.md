# REF-0029 — Turntable 3D render, top-down

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** Grey tonal field: ground/plinth `#909291` / `#898a8d`, platter and vinyl `#2b2c2e` / `#202022`, one orange record label `#e87f29` as the only hue.

**Layout & harmony.** Orthographic top-down product shot, object centred with even margin.

**Shape & surface.** Soft studio light, chrome tonearm and brushed platter edge; small radii on the plinth.

**Gradient & imagery.** Render is the whole piece — high cost, nothing left without it.

**Contrast.** No text. Orange `#e87f29` on the grey `#909291` ground = 1.12:1 by luminance — the accent reads purely by hue, not value.

## Why the verdict

imagery_dep hard and cost high; the only takeaway (single warm accent on a mid-grey field) is a palette note, not a reference.

## Notes

Single orange accent on mid-grey is worth noting because its luminance contrast is ~1.1:1 — it works only through hue, which would fail for colour-blind users if used as a UI signal.
