# REF-0010 — Iznik-style tile panel: cobalt border, white field, blue–turquoise–red florals

## Read

_Measured: colours are k-means clusters and region medians sampled off the image with Python/PIL; contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x computed from those measured hexes._

**Colour** — white glaze #f3f7f4 (32%) as the field; cobalt #153372 / border #162b71 (≈14%) as the structural colour; turquoise #549ca4 (5.7%) and tomato red #942a2c (1.4%) as decorative accents. A triad where blue frames, turquoise fills and red punctuates.

**Typography** — none.

**Layout & harmony** — a framed field: thick cobalt border, dense symmetric floral repeat inside, everything mirrored on the vertical axis.

**Shape & surface** — glazed ceramic with tile seams; the repeat is the surface.

**Gradient & imagery** — none; the pattern is the image.

**Contrast** — cobalt on white 11.11:1, red 7.39:1, turquoise only 2.91:1 (decorative only).

## Why the verdict

The palette roles transfer: cobalt as structure, red as a rare accent, turquoise as fill that must never carry text. Symmetric, so it mirrors cleanly.
