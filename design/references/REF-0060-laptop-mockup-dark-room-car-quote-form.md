# REF-0060 — Over-the-shoulder laptop in a dark room, car-import quote form on frosted glass

## Nature

Over-the-shoulder laptop in a dark room with a person → photographed scene.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Near-black room #040404 fading to #5f5f5f at the lower left; person and chair in charcoal. Screen: full-bleed photo of a black car (#19202a) in a beige concrete set (#d2cac7, #cfc7c4). Frosted form #353334 with fields #302f2d; one accent, pale butter yellow #f4f2b9 on the "Далее" (Next) pill.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque, Cyrillic. Headline cap ≈17 preview px, sentence case; small field labels. Page caption below the scene in tracked caps ("ОБСУДИМ ВАШ ПРОЕКТ? TG @DIANAWIDNER").

**Layout & harmony.** Over-the-shoulder photo puts the viewer behind the user. Screen: car left, headline and a two-column dropdown form on a frosted panel right, full-width pill button.

**Shape & surface.** Frosted live-blur panel with medium radius; dropdowns medium radius; pill CTA.

**Buttons & components.** The pale yellow pill is the only light, filled object in the form — loud by value, not saturation.

**Gradient & imagery.** Full-bleed car photo plus blur panel; the form floats on the photo and would need its own ground without it.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: button label #696741 on #f4f2b7 5.04:1; caption white on #0e0e0e 19.3:1. Failures: white headline over the light concrete wall ≈1.6:1 in places (7.3:1 where it crosses the car); field labels #b8b9be on #7d7c81 2.11:1.

## Why the verdict

Pale-yellow pill on a dark frosted form is a cheap, adaptable accent idea; the photo-dependent headline legibility and the blur cost are not.
