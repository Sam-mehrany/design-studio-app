# REF-0028 — AI consultancy landing — "Turn AI Into Measurable ROI" lavender gradient monitor

## Read

Values below: lavender field was **measured by sampling pixels**; type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: an AI consultancy's landing hero on a monitor — a white serif headline bottom-left over a soft lavender gradient, a white nav on top, a small client-logo row bottom-right.

**Colour.** Measured: lavender field #EAE8F5, white nav #F1F1F1. Monochrome lavender: the whole screen is one pale violet wash darkening toward the bottom-left, with black type and no accent hue at all.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "Turn AI Into Measurable ROI — Not Another Pilot" in a transitional serif at about 26–30 px, body at about 11–12 px, nav at about 10–11 px. Two-tier scale — the serif headline is the only display size, everything else is one quiet tier; the serif is the voice of the page.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, estimated by eye: white nav bar across the top, the gradient field filling the rest, headline and body pinned bottom-left, client logos pinned bottom-right. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by position (bottom-left is the only occupied corner). Density is very low — the gradient field is mostly empty.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: the inner content panel at about 16–20 px radius, pill CTAs at full pill radius, the monitor sharp-cornered. Separation is by tone — the lavender panel against the white nav — with no border and no shadow.

**Buttons & components.** Two pill CTAs under the body copy: a filled white pill ("Try for free") and an outlined pill ("View Demo"), plus a "Try for free" pill in the nav. The CTAs are quiet relative to the headline — white pills on lavender, not filled colour — so the serif line does the selling.

**Gradient & imagery.** A soft radial lavender gradient is the only visual material on the page; no photography, no illustration. Delete it and the page is black type on white — it still reads, but it loses the whole atmosphere, so the gradient is decorative but load-bearing for identity. It is a cheap flat gradient, not a mesh.

**Contrast.** Measured: the lavender field #EAE8F5 against the white nav #F1F1F1 is 1.07:1 — the two surfaces are nearly indistinguishable (measured). Black type on lavender is about 13:1 estimated — passes. The small grey client logos bottom-right, estimated at about #9A9A9A on lavender (about 3:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the serif-headline-over-soft-gradient hero is a transferable, low-cost landing mechanism, but it is a staged concept mockup and the page's only visual material is a gradient wash, so adapt, not adopt.
