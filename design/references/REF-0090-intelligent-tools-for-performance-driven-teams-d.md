# REF-0090 — Intelligent tools for performance driven teams — dark landing page with aerial photography and embedded dashboard card

## Read

**Colour.** Measured off the 901px-wide image: field near-black #010101; white #FFFFFF headline type at 20.9:1; body grey sampled around #5A5A5A–#6E6E6E. The UI palette is neutral-only — all hue lives in the aerial farmland photography (field green sampled #204D23–#255727, bright lime #A4D34B at 11.9:1 on the field, red-orange road tones whose exact hex cannot be determined). Colour is layered, not semantic: no hue in the interface carries meaning.

**Typography.** One neo-grotesque family throughout, two weights (regular body, semibold headline and feature titles), sentence case everywhere. Headline cap height ~26px per line across three lines, intro body ~11–12px estimated by eye; the lower-section display numerals '20+' and '94%' are ~55px cap height estimated by eye, a ~4.6:1 jump over body — a dramatic two-tier scale. Numerals are lining Latin figures with thousands separators ($60,764).

**Layout & harmony.** Two stacked full-width sections on a split grid: upper section pairs the photo-plus-dashboard-card at left against three feature rows at right; lower section pairs a text block at left against the two display numerals at right. Generous black voids separate the sections; hierarchy is ranked by size alone — headline, then numerals, then feature titles. No containers except the dashboard card; grouping elsewhere is by space.

**Shape & surface.** Mixed radii: the dashboard card has a small radius (~4–6px estimated by eye) while the feature icons are full circles and the captured notification pill is fully rounded. Depth is tonal, not shadowed: a #000000 card sits on the #010101 field, separated mainly by the photograph beneath it; no shadows are visible anywhere.

**Buttons & components.** No filled button appears anywhere. The only actions are 'Explore Feature +' text links with a plus sign. Three feature rows each pair a white circular icon disc (dark line glyph) with a title and one-line description, divided by hairlines. The dashboard card holds a dot-matrix chart and three income rows (Salary $15,300, Freelance $9,750, Investment $11,200) under a $60,764 total; the three rows sum to $36,250, so the figures do not reconcile — a mockup tell. A three-avatar cluster sits beside 'Integrated tools'.

**Gradients & imagery.** Full-bleed aerial farmland photography carries both sections, darkened with a scrim in the lower section so the white numerals sit on it. The dot chart is a single-hue white sequential ramp — data as image — and there are no gradient fills in the UI itself. Remove the photography and both sections collapse to black voids with floating type.

**Contrast.** Measured: white headline on #010101 is 20.9:1; white numerals on the lower section's #081B0D ground are 17.9:1. Body grey sampled ~#5A5A5A–#6E6E6E measures 3.0–4.1:1, below the 4.5:1 body floor. White type over the darkened photograph in the lower section cannot be measured exactly because the scrim value varies across the image.

## Why the verdict

The dark field, oversized display numerals and dot-chart dashboard card are transferable mechanisms, but the composition collapses without the aerial photography (hard imagery dependency) and the small grey body copy fails the 4.5:1 floor, so the finish cannot be adopted as-is; as a concept shot it could never be adopt in any case.
