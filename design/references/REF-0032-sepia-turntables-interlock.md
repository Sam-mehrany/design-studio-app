# REF-0032 — Interlocking twin turntables, sepia illustration

## Read

*All hex values and contrast ratios below are **measured** with Python/PIL off the image (region medians, median-cut clusters, or darkest/lightest-5% glyph samples; WCAG 2.x formula). Type sizes and ratios are estimated by eye.*

**Colour.** One-hue sepia ladder: ground `#c8b3a3` / `#cfbbac`, mids `#887161` / `#614f46`, darkest `#463730`. No accent.

**Layout & harmony.** Two turntables rotated 180° into an interlocking S / yin-yang figure, centred on the square.

**Gradient & imagery.** Flat-shaded illustration; low cost.

**Contrast.** No text. Darkest `#463730` on ground `#c8b3a3` = 5.64:1.

## Why the verdict

Subject-locked illustration; the tonal sepia ladder is the only takeaway and it is generic.
