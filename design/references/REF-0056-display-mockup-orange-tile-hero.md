# REF-0056 — Pro display front-on in grey studio, orange-red 3D tile hero

## Nature

Pro display front-on in a grey rendered studio.

*Nature backfilled by Hark, 27 Nov 2026, from the image.*

## Read

*Measured* with Python/PIL on the original file: hex values are region medians, contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x. Cap heights are in px of a preview scaled to ≤1400 px on the long edge; on mockups, on-screen values are as photographed/rendered.

**Colour.** Cool grey studio: #383f47 top, a bright band #9ea9bb behind the stand, #16191e floor. The screen is a single hue run from deep red #750101 through #fe3e19 to orange #ff8027 across a grid of rounded 3D tiles.

**Typography.** Neo-grotesque. Title "Ap-Mockups" cap ≈17 preview px; a giant lowercase "ap" bottom-right at ≈29 x-height; micro body in two columns top-right.

**Layout & harmony.** Monitor dead centre, slightly from above. Screen: number "01" and title top-left, two text columns top-right, monogram bottom-right — corners only, centre left to the tiles.

**Shape & surface.** Rendered rounded tiles fill the screen; the device stands on a reflective floor.

**Buttons & components.** None visible.

**Gradient & imagery.** The red-orange tile render is the whole screen; nothing stands without it.

**Contrast.** Measured on screen: title white on #ab0b0b 7.27:1. Failures: "ap" monogram white on #f69260 2.26:1, micro body ≈2.24:1, and white on the #ff8027 end of the gradient is only 2.51:1.

## Why the verdict

Rejected on contrast and cost: white type over a light orange render falls to ≈2.3:1, and the screen is one large 3D render.
