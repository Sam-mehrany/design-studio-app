# REF-0031 — Hypergate — "Built For Your Business" pricing screen on laptop

## Read

Values below are **estimated by eye** — pixel samples landed on the staging background, not the screen, so no hex is claimed as measured. One line of context: a pricing screen on a laptop on a stand — "BUILT FOR YOUR BUSINESS" centred over an orange-red glow, two frosted-glass cards below ("For e-commerce, SaaS and marketplaces" / "For platforms, switch and payment providers").

**Colour.** Estimated by eye: dark charcoal top grading to orange-red glow (about #D85A2E) at the bottom, white type, one orange accent in the pill CTA (about #F06A1D). Single accent: the orange CTA and the glow are one family; the glass cards are neutral tints of the background.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "BUILT FOR YOUR BUSINESS" in a grotesque at about 20–24 px, card titles at about 11–12 px caps, body at about 9–10 px. Two-tier scale — the centred headline is the only display size, everything else is one small tier.

**Layout & harmony.** Split grid, estimated by eye: centred headline and CTA top, two equal glass cards below in a row, each with a title, body and a pill row at the bottom. Hierarchy is ranked by size (headline first), then by enclosure (the two cards are the only containers). Density is moderate — the screen is symmetric and evenly filled.

**Shape & surface.** Estimated radius scale, by eye: glass cards at about 12–16 px radius, pill CTA at full pill radius. Separation is by frosted-glass tone — the cards are lighter tints of the background with a hairline border — not by shadow. No elevation beyond the glow.

**Buttons & components.** One orange filled pill CTA under the headline and a row of small pills at the bottom of each card (illegible at this size). The CTA is the loudest control on the page after the headline; the card pills are quiet metadata.

**Gradient & imagery.** A dark-to-orange radial glow backs the screen; no photography, no illustration. Delete the glow and the page is white type on charcoal — it still reads, but it loses the warmth that distinguishes the two cards' frosted glass, so the gradient is decorative but load-bearing for the glass effect.

**Contrast.** Estimated by eye: white on charcoal is about 14:1 — passes. White on the orange CTA, estimated at about #F06A1D (about 3:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured). The small grey body copy on the glass cards, estimated at about #B8B0AC on a mid-tone glow (about 3:1 estimated — fails, estimated, not measured).

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the centred headline + two frosted-glass pricing cards over a glow is a transferable, low-cost pricing mechanism, but it is a staged concept mockup (the frame is labelled "[18-28] PRICE SCREEN [MAIN PAGE] / HYPERGATE") and the small copy fails contrast, so adapt, not adopt.
