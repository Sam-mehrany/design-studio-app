# REF-0068 — Movetrans — logistics services landing page, service list with orange markers and stats

## Read

Values below are **measured where stated** (hex values sampled from pixels); type sizes are estimated by eye and labelled as such. One line of context: a two-section logistics landing capture — a white services list (Ocean, Warehousing, Land, Rail Freight) over a black "Why choose us" section with a photo and statistics (25+ years, 48+ partners, 72+ cities).

**Colour.** Measured: white upper section (#FCFCFA dominant) and a near-black lower section (#171717 sampled), with a single orange accent used only as small square markers beside each service name and in the "More Service" button. Single accent, muted saturation — the orange is a wayfinding mark, not a field — and the true-dual structure (white over black) is the page's main colour event.

**Typography.** Estimated by eye: headline "Movetrans offers reliable, efficient, and scalable logistics solutions" at about 28–32 px grotesque, left-aligned; service names at about 18–20 px semibold; descriptions and tag pills at about 12–13 px; statistics "25+ / 48+ / 72+" at about 32–36 px with small captions. Two-tier scale, with the statistics as the numerals — large, lining, and the loudest elements in the black section.

**Layout & harmony.** Single column, two stacked blocks: the services list is four rows, each a bordered card with a photo thumbnail on the Warehousing row only, tag pills under the description; the black section is a two-column split (photo left, headline and stats right). Density is moderate — the list rows have generous padding, the tags add a second line of texture. Hierarchy is by size and by the orange markers, which give the eye a vertical line to follow down the list.

**Shape & surface.** Sharp corners everywhere — no radii on cards, buttons or photos — and separation by hairline border only (border-over-shadow throughout). The black section's photo is the only image with any depth, and it is flat against the black. One family of sharp rectangles; the only soft element is the photo itself.

**Buttons & components.** One small outline button ("More Service") in the headline row; the tag pills (Global Routes, Secure Shipping, Cost Efficient…) are the page's main component — small grey pills that read as metadata, quieter than the service names. The statistics row in the black section is separated by thin vertical rules, a quieter device than a card. Controls are the quietest elements on the page; the list itself is the content.

**Gradient & imagery.** Two photographs: a warehouse interior on the Warehousing row and a courier with a box on the black section. Soft dependency — remove the photos and the service list still reads completely; the photos add proof, not structure. No gradients anywhere, which is the point of the page's restraint.

**Contrast.** Measured: black headlines on #FCFCFA are about 17:1 — pass. White statistics on the #171717 black section are about 17:1 — pass. The orange markers are decorative, not text. Grey body copy is estimated by eye at about #6B7280 on white, roughly 4.8:1 — passes, estimated, not measured. No failing pair measured.

## Why the verdict

Adapt: the sharp-cornered service list with orange markers and a white-over-black two-block structure is a clean, low-cost, transferable landing pattern — flat colour, type and borders, no effects — but a concept capture, so adapt, not adopt.
